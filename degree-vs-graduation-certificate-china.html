Degree Certificate vs Graduation Certificate from Chinese Universities

By ORIGIN Education · 2026-11-23

Chinese universities hand graduating students two documents, not one, and they are not interchangeable. The 毕业证 records that you finished the curriculum; the 学位证 records that a committee decided you earned the degree. Students who plan for only one certificate often discover the gap years later, at a work-permit window or a credential evaluation desk.

What each certificate actually proves

A graduation certificate (毕业证) proves you completed the required courses and left the university in good standing. A degree certificate (学位证) proves the institution's degree evaluation committee formally awarded you the bachelor's or master's degree. Employers, visa officers and credential evaluators usually ask for both, because finishing a programme and earning the qualification are two separate decisions.

The 毕业证 is signed by the university president and stamped with the institution's steel seal. The 学位证 carries a degree certificate number issued under the national numbering system and is signed on behalf of the degree evaluation committee. That is why a university can issue the first without the second.

学历 versus 学位 in one sentence

学历 is the record of study; 学位 is the academic title. Every graduate has 学历, but only those who pass the defence and the committee vote have 学位.

Why some graduates receive only one

The degree certificate is withheld when a student fails the thesis defence, fails a compulsory course twice, or is found to have committed academic misconduct, while the graduation certificate can still be issued. This is the most common surprise for international students, because the two documents are decided by different committees on different timelines.

The rules that govern it

The Regulations on Academic Degrees took effect in 1981, and the replacement 学位法 was adopted on 26 April 2024 and came into force on 1 January 2025. The new law keeps revocation powers: a degree can be withdrawn years later if the thesis or admissions documents turn out to be fabricated.

What a 结业证 means

A 结业证 records that you completed the courses but did not graduate, usually because of a failed course or an unfinished thesis. It is not a degree and cannot be converted later by paying a fee, only by re-sitting the missing requirement within the university's deadline.

How to verify both certificates online

Two ministry databases hold the records, and Chinese employers check them rather than the paper. 学信网 (CHSI) covers graduation certificates issued from 2001 onward, and 学位网, run by the China Degree and Graduate Education Development Center, covers degree awards from 2008. Both issue downloadable verification reports, including English versions.

What to order before you leave

  1. The 教育部学历证书电子注册备案表 from CHSI, valid for six months.
  2. The 学位在线验证报告 from 学位网.
  3. A sealed transcript in Chinese and English.
  4. Certified translations of all three if you are applying abroad.

Order them while your student account still works, because alumni access to some university portals is switched off within a year.

The documents employers and visa offices want

Chinese work-permit applications under the foreigner employment system ask for the degree certificate and a verification report, and the qualification field must match the certificate exactly. A master's graduate who writes "bachelor" or uses a translated title that does not match the Chinese text will be sent back to reapply.

For jobs outside China, the sequence is usually translation, then notarisation or apostille, then recognition by the destination country's evaluation service. None of those steps accepts a screenshot, so keep the originals and two colour scans.

Apostille replaced legalisation in 2023

China joined the Hague Apostille Convention, which entered into force for China on 7 November 2023. Since then a Chinese public document can be apostilled by the foreign affairs office instead of being legalised at an embassy, which cut the processing time for degree documents from weeks to days in most provinces.

Replacing a lost certificate

A lost 毕业证 is not reprinted; the university issues a 毕业证明书, which carries the same legal effect and the same number series. A lost 学位证 is handled by a 学位证明 issued by the degree-awarding institution. Both replacements are recorded in the national databases, so a prospective employer can confirm them.

If your name changed

A marriage certificate or a deed poll does not automatically update a Chinese university record. You need the old passport, the new one and a notarised name-change document, and the credential evaluator must be told in writing that the two names refer to one person.

Checklist for your final semester

The gap between finishing and proving it is where job offers die, so treat the paperwork as a course with a deadline rather than as an afterthought. Start in your final semester, not the week after graduation, because the degree committee sits on a fixed schedule and a missed sitting costs a full academic year.

  1. Confirm in writing that your thesis defence date is scheduled, not merely expected.
  2. Ask the registrar whether your 学位证 is on the committee agenda for the correct sitting.
  3. Clear every failed course, because a resit after the graduation date usually delays the degree by a full year.
  4. Order CHSI and 学位网 reports the week the certificates are issued.
  5. Get two notarised copies and one apostille if you plan to work outside China.
  6. Scan everything at 600 dpi and store it in two places you control.

If a certificate is missing when you expected it, ask the 学位评定委员会 office rather than your department. The department knows your grades; only the committee knows whether your name was on the award list.
