Open Education, Deep Connection: CACIE 2026

By ORIGIN Education · 2026-10-25 · 原文：微信公众号

ENIn just a few days, Beijing will host the 27th China Annual Conference for International Education (CACIE 2026) at the China National Convention Center from October 28 to 31. Billed as Asia's most influential international education exchange event, CACIE is no longer merely a trade fair — it has become the flagship stage where China translates its "High-level Education Opening-up" strategy into concrete, yearly momentum for the "Study in China" brand.

中文再过几天，北京国家会议中心将于 10 月 28 日至 31 日迎来第 27 届中国国际教育年会（CACIE 2026）。作为亚洲最具影响力的国际教育交流盛会，CACIE 早已不只是展会——它已成为中国把"高水平教育对外开放"战略转化为"留学中国"品牌年度势能的旗舰舞台。

ENThe conference carries a resonant theme this year: "Open Education, Deep Connection" (开放育新，合作致远). Thailand serves as the Country of Honor — the third consecutive year a guest nation has been written into a major China diplomatic communiqué, after France and New Zealand. Organizers expect over 5,000 educators, embassy officials and industry leaders from more than 70 countries and regions, gathered around nearly 100 curated events.

中文本届年会主题意味深长："开放育新，合作致远"。泰国担任主宾国——继法国、新西兰之后，连续第三年主宾国被写入中国重大外交成果文件。主办方预计将迎来来自 70 多个国家和地区的 5,000 余名教育工作者、驻华使领馆官员与行业代表，围绕近百场精心策划的活动相聚。

ENAmong the five headline highlights unveiled at the October 22 press briefing, two stand out for prospective international students. First, CACIE will release for the first time the Development Report on China's Higher Education International Cooperation and Exchange (2026) — an annual "industry weathervane" mapping trends in transnational education. Second, the concurrent China Education Expo reaches a record scale, bringing 415 institutions from 35 countries and regions to Chinese students and parents as an authoritative one-stop channel.

中文在 10 月 22 日新闻发布会公布的五大看点中，有两项与潜在来华留学生直接相关。其一，年会首次发布《中国高等教育国际合作与交流发展报告（2026）》，作为年度"行业风向标"，梳理跨境教育的发展趋势；其二，同期中国国际教育展规模创近年新高，汇聚 35 个国家和地区的 415 所院校与机构，为广大学生和家长提供对话海外优质教育资源的权威渠道。

ENArtificial intelligence is woven through the entire event. Of the 40 parallel forums, 14 focus directly on digital and AI applications in education; a dedicated Digital Education Zone and a "one-code-to-know-all / one-code-to-access-all" smart-format turn the conference itself into a living lab of how education is being re-shaped by technology. The signal is clear: the future of "Study in China" will be open, connected and intelligent by design.

中文人工智能贯穿了整场年会。40 场平行论坛中，有 14 场直接聚焦教育数字与 AI 应用；专门设立的数字教育专区，以及"一码知全景""一码通全场"的智慧办会形式，让年会本身成为教育被技术重塑的实景实验室。信号很明确：未来的"留学中国"，从设计之初就是开放、互联、智能的。

ENBehind the spectacle is a deliberate service architecture. In May 2025, the "Study in China" brand identity and an integrated service platform were launched, unifying visual identity with an "open, inclusive, mutual-learning" message and consolidating smart school-selection, visa applications, study-abroad experience and internship-employment into a single "one-network-do-all, worry-free study" portal. China Scholarship Council's 2026 strategy further builds the brand on six pillars — promotion, exam selection, review and evaluation, funding, cultivation support and alumni liaison.

中文盛会背后，是一套刻意的服务体系。2025 年 5 月，"留学中国"品牌形象与综合服务平台同步发布，统一视觉标识，以"开放包容、互学互鉴"为内涵，并将智能择校、签证申请、留学体验、实习就业整合为"一网通办，留学无忧"的一站式门户。国家留学基金委 2026 年战略更以宣传推广、考试选拔、评审评估、经费资助、培养支撑、校友联络六大体系夯实这一品牌。

ENAll of this sits on the bedrock of the "15th Five-Year Plan for Education Development," released in June 2026, which explicitly calls for strengthening "Study in China" brand and capability building. The direction is unmistakable: from scale expansion toward quality, digitalization and transparency — with CSCA academic competency testing and the upgraded HSK 3.0 language gate now anchoring a merit-based, auditable admissions pipeline.

中文这一切都建立在 2026 年 6 月发布的《教育发展"十五五"规划》之上，规划明确提出加强"留学中国"品牌和能力建设。方向一目了然：从规模扩张转向质量、数字化与透明化——CSCA 学业水平测试与升级后的 HSK 3.0 语言门槛，如今共同锚定一条以学业能力为导向、可审计的录取通道。

ENFor ORIGIN (Originedu), CACIE 2026 is both a mirror and a mandate. As the ecosystem around "Study in China" turns open, digital and AI-enabled, ORIGIN positions itself not as an agency but as a transparent self-service digital ecosystem that connects global applicants to that opportunity: its CSCA platform (csca.originedu.net) delivers diagnostics and mock tests for the very exam-selection pillar CSCA now represents; Edumatch 3.0 matches students to 810 institutions with 535 admission abstracts — the digital counterpart to the 415 exhibitors and growing transnational-program pipeline; and OrigineduCRM tracks every visa, insurance and internship-employment milestone across 22 auditable stages, realizing the "worry-free study" promise end to end.

中文对尚源教育科技（ORIGIN / Originedu）而言，CACIE 2026 既是一面镜子，也是一份使命。当"留学中国"生态走向开放、数字化与 AI 赋能，尚源的定位不是中介，而是把全球申请者与这一机遇相连的透明合规自助数字生态：其 CSCA 平台（csca.originedu.net）正好承接 CSCA 所代表的"考试选拔"体系，提供诊断与模拟；Edumatch 3.0 在 810 所院校库、535 条录取摘要中为学生智能选校——对应 415 家参展院校与不断扩展的中外合作办学通道；OrigineduCRM 则以 22 个可审计阶段覆盖签证、保险、实习就业每一步，把"留学无忧"的承诺落到实处。

ENAs the world's educators gather under the banner of "Open Education, Deep Connection," the invitation to international students is equally open: come prepared, come transparent, and let a disciplined, self-driven journey carry you into the next chapter of "Study in China." ORIGIN is built to walk that road with you.

中文当全球教育工作者在"开放育新，合作致远"的旗帜下相聚，递给国际学生的邀请同样开放：做好准备、秉持透明，让一段自律而自主的旅程，带你走进"留学中国"的下一个篇章。尚源教育科技，正是为陪你走完这条路而生。
