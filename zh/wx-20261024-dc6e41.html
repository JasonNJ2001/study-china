今日推荐大学 ｜ Today's University

EN — Nanjing University of Posts and Telecommunications (NUPT, 南京邮电大学) is a key provincial university co-established by China's Ministry of Industry and Information Technology (MIIT) and the Jiangsu Provincial Government. Its roots go back to 1942, and it has built its identity around information technology and telecommunications. Engineering is the core, with strengths spanning electronic information, computer science, communication engineering, and a growing set of management, economics and arts programs. The campus covers more than 1.3 million square metres. For international students it offers both English-taught and Chinese-taught bachelor's, master's and PhD programs in a city — Nanjing — that is one of China's great historical and educational centres.

中文 — 南京邮电大学（NUPT）是工业和信息化部与江苏省人民政府共建的高校，前身可追溯至 1942 年，长期以信息科技与通信为办学特色。学校以工为主，在电子信息、计算机、通信工程等领域实力突出，并逐步拓展管理、经济、文学、艺术等学科。校园面积逾 130 万平方米。面向国际学生，学校提供英文授课与中文授课的本科、硕士、博士项目，所在城市南京更是中国历史名城与高等教育重镇。

学校一句话亮点 ｜ One-line Highlight

EN — An MIIT-co-built university where English-taught IT and telecom degrees meet the living history of Nanjing — practical, future-facing, and internationally open.

中文 — 工信部共建、以信息通信见长的高校——英文授课 IT/通信学位 + 南京古城文脉，务实、面向未来、国际化程度持续提升。

适合哪些留学生 ｜ Who This University Is For

EN — NUPT is a strong match for students who want to study electronic information, computer science, communication engineering or related IT fields in English, without first needing advanced Chinese; learners aiming at careers in telecom, the internet of things, software, or the digital economy; applicants who already hold HSK 5 and want a Chinese-taught degree in engineering or business; and students who value studying in a historic, student-friendly city with a large international academic community.

中文 — 南京邮电大学尤其适合：想用英语学习电子信息、计算机、通信工程等 IT 方向、暂时不用高深中文的同学；立志进入通信、物联网、软件或数字经济行业发展的同学；已具备 HSK5、想在工程或商科读中文授课学位的同学；以及看重历史名城、求学环境友好、国际学术氛围浓厚城市的同学。

优势专业与项目 ｜ Strength Programs

EN — NUPT's international undergraduate offering includes English-taught majors such as Electronic Information Engineering (电子信息工程, School of Communications and Information Engineering, 4 years) and Computer Science and Technology (计算机科学与技术, 4 years). It also offers Chinese-taught bachelor's, master's and PhD programs across communication engineering, electronic information, computer science, business administration, electronic commerce and more. NUPT is not a 211 / 985 / Double First-Class university, but it is a well-regarded, industry-linked institution in China's information and communication sector, with active global partnerships (e.g. joint platforms with Central Asian universities and its own "NJUPT IoT" satellite). Its strength lies in focused, employable ICT training rather than broad disciplinary coverage.

中文 — 南京邮电大学国际本科开设英文授课专业，包括电子信息工程（电子信息与通信工程学院，4 年制）与计算机科学与技术（4 年制）。学校另设中文授课的本科、硕士、博士项目，覆盖通信工程、电子信息、计算机、工商管理、电子商务等方向。需说明：南京邮电大学是一所行业特色鲜明、与产业联系紧密的信息通信类高校（非 211 / 985 /"双一流"），其优势在于聚焦、就业导向强的 ICT 培养，而非学科广度。学校国际化合作活跃（如与中亚高校共建平台、自研"南邮物联网"卫星），在全球伙伴网络方面持续拓展。

语言与入学要求 ｜ Language & Admission

EN — Per NUPT's official 2026 international admissions brochure: Chinese-taught programs (bachelor / master / PhD) require HSK Level 5; English-taught programs require IELTS 5.5+, TOEFL 72+ or Duolingo 100+. CSCA is MANDATORY for ALL undergraduate applicants (self-funded or scholarship): Electronic Information Engineering → Math + Physics; Computer Science and Technology → Math + Physics; Business Administration → Math; Electronic Commerce → Math (details at https://www.csca.cn). Application window (2026 reference): bachelor's cycle closed around 31 May 2026 with a September intake — confirm 2027 dates with the office / an advisor. HSK 3.0 note: reformed HSK is "three levels, nine grades" (L1–3 / L4–6 / L7–9); NUPT still uses the legacy HSK 5 banding — confirm conversion and CSCA details with an advisor.

中文 — 依据南京邮电大学 2026 年官方国际学生招生简章：中文授课项目（本/硕/博）须 HSK5 级及以上；英文授课项目须雅思 5.5+、托福 72+ 或多邻国 100+。CSCA 为本科硬性要求：所有本科申请者（自费或奖学金）均须参加 CSCA——电子信息工程→数学+物理；计算机科学与技术→数学+物理；工商管理→数学；电子商务→数学（详见 https://www.csca.cn）。申请时间（2026 参考）：本科批次约 5 月 31 日截止、秋季入学，2027 年具体日期请向招生办或顾问确认。HSK 3.0 提示：新版"三等九级"（初级 L1–3/中级 L4–6/高级 L7–9），学校仍按旧版 HSK5，换算与 CSCA 细节请向顾问确认。

城市与生活 ｜ City & Living

EN — Nanjing (南京) is the capital of Jiangsu Province and one of China's Four Great Ancient Capitals, home to the Qinhuai River, the city wall, Purple Mountain (紫金山) and numerous universities. It is a major education hub with a large, diverse student population and a comparatively moderate cost of living — lower than Shanghai or Beijing, though higher than inland cities. Daily expenses for an international student are generally manageable, and on-campus or nearby accommodation is common. For HSK exam preparation, Nanjing has multiple authorized test centres hosted by local universities — confirm the latest venues and sessions on www.chinesetest.cn or with an advisor.

中文 — 南京是江苏省省会、中国四大古都之一，秦淮河、明城墙、紫金山与众多高校云集于此，是一座高等教育重镇，学生群体多元、规模庞大，生活成本相对适中——低于上海、北京，但高于内陆城市。国际学生日常开销总体可控，校内或周边住宿是常见选择。HSK 备考方面，南京设有多所高校承办的汉语考试授权考点——具体考点与场次以 www.chinesetest.cn 公布或向顾问确认为准。

奖学金与申请提示 ｜ Scholarships & Tips

EN — NUPT participates in the Chinese Government Scholarship (CSC) system — its English site advertises "High Level Postgraduate" and "Silk Road" CSC programs for 2026/2027 — and students may also explore Jiangsu Provincial / Nanjing Municipal government scholarships and university-level awards. Exact quotas, amounts and eligibility vary by year and are not invented here; confirm with an Origineduglob advisor. 2026 official tuition: Bachelor RMB 18,000 / year; Master RMB 22,000 / year; PhD RMB 26,000 / year (textbooks, accommodation, insurance and other fees extra; confirm the latest fee schedule with the university). With English-taught IT majors and CSCA as a transparent entry gate, NUPT is an accessible, career-oriented choice for international students interested in China's digital economy.

中文 — 南京邮电大学参与中国政府奖学金（CSC）体系——其英文官网已发布 2026/2027 年"高水平研究生"与"丝绸之路"CSC 项目；学生还可关注江苏省/南京市政府奖学金及校级奖项。具体名额、金额与资格逐年不同，本文不作虚构，建议与 Origineduglob 留学顾问确认。2026 年官方学费：本科 18,000 元/年；硕士 22,000 元/年；博士 26,000 元/年（教材、住宿、保险等另计，最新收费标准以学校公布为准）。凭借英文授课 IT 专业与透明化的 CSCA 入学通道，NUPT 是关注中国数字经济的国际学生务实、可达的选择。
