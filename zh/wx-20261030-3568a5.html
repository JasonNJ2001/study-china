来华留学中介服务纳入备案管理

《从事出境入境中介服务的机构和人员备案管理办法》要点解读

Key points of the filing rules for entry-exit service agencies

2026 年 10 月 30 日 · 南京

 

根据国家移民管理局、国家市场监督管理总局 2026 年第 6 号公告，《从事出境入境中介服务的机构和人员备案管理办法》已于 2026 年 9 月 15 日起施行，学习类中介服务纳入备案管理。尚源（南京）教育科技有限公司已按规定完成备案，备案信息可在国家移民管理局备案信息公示平台查询。
Under Announcement No. 6 of 2026 issued by the National Immigration Administration and the State Administration for Market Regulation, the Measures for the Record-Filing of Agencies and Personnel Providing Entry-Exit Intermediary Services took effect on 15 September 2026, bringing study-related intermediary services under record-filing management. Origin EduGlob (Nanjing) Education Technology Co., Ltd. has completed the filing as required, and its filing information is available on the NIA public register.

■  文件说了什么  WHAT THE RULE SAYS

办法第二条把中介服务的范围写明：为旅游、商务、学习、工作、定居提供政策咨询、证件代办、手续办理。来华留学正在其中——学生办学习类签证，机构代办手续。
Article 2 defines the scope of intermediary services: policy consultation, document processing and procedure handling for travel, business, study, work and settlement. Study in China falls squarely within it — students apply for a study visa, and agencies handle the procedures.

接受出境入境人员委托，为旅游、商务、学习、工作、定居等活动提供出境入境政策咨询、证件代办、手续办理等中介服务，并按照约定收取相关费用的活动。
Activities in which, entrusted by persons entering or leaving China, an agency provides entry-exit policy consultation, document processing and procedure handling for travel, business, study, work, settlement and other purposes, and charges agreed fees.

——《从事出境入境中介服务的机构和人员备案管理办法》第二条　/　Article 2, Measures for the Record-Filing of Agencies and Personnel Providing Entry-Exit Intermediary Services

第三条、第四条给出时间表：机构向所在地县级以上公安机关出入境管理机构备案；新设机构 15 日内办理，存量机构 90 日内补齐，也就是今年 12 月 14 日之前。逾期按《国务院关于出境入境管理的规定》处罚。
Articles 3 and 4 set the timetable: agencies file with the exit-entry administration of the public security organ at or above the county level where they are located; new agencies within 15 days of establishment, and existing agencies within 90 days of the Measures taking effect — that is, before 14 December this year. Failure to file is penalised under the State Council Provisions on the Administration of Exit and Entry.

第五条要求提交与境外服务机构签署的合作协议；第九条要求建立全国统一的备案信息公示平台；第十二条要求机构建立服务台账与用户档案。备案不止于取得凭证，更在于把服务责任纳入可核查的记录体系。
Article 5 requires a cooperation agreement with an overseas service provider; Article 9 requires a unified national public register; Article 12 requires agencies to keep service records and client files. Filing is more than obtaining a certificate: it brings service responsibility into a verifiable record system.

■  JW202 表为什么也要备案  WHY THE JW202 FORM ALSO REQUIRES FILING

来华留学的手续是一条固定链：录取通知书、学校填写的 JW202 表（《外国留学人员来华签证申请表》）、X1 签证、入境 30 日内办理的居留许可，环环相扣。中国政府奖学金生用 JW201 表。
The procedure for studying in China follows a fixed chain: the admission letter, the JW202 Form (Application Form for Foreign Students Studying in China) completed by the host institution, the X1 visa, and the residence permit obtained within 30 days of entry. Chinese Government Scholarship students use the JW201 Form.

录取通知书　→　JW202 表　→　X1 签证　→　居留许可

Offer Letter　→　JW202 Form　→　X1 Visa　→　Residence Permit

JW202 表只能由接收院校申办，中介不能签发，也不能替学校出这份表。但中介要做的是另一半——把成绩单、语言成绩、学习计划、资金证明准备到能通过学校审核，这恰恰属于办法第二条所说的“为学习提供手续办理”。
The JW202 Form can only be applied for by the host institution; an agency cannot issue it or produce it on the school's behalf. What the agency handles is the other half — preparing transcripts, language scores, study plans and proof of funds to the standard required for institutional review, which is precisely the “procedure handling for study” described in Article 2.

所以 JW202 表管学生“能不能来”，备案管谁有资格替他办。一条线两个关口，凡是代办来华留学手续的，都绕不开，也都要备案。
The JW202 Form governs whether a student can come; filing governs who is qualified to handle the process on the student's behalf. Two checkpoints on one line — any agency handling study-in-China procedures must pass both, and must file.

■  有备案的中介，为什么必不可少  WHY A FILED AGENCY IS INDISPENSABLE

没有备案的机构，编号查不到，责任主体不清，材料出错时找不到该找谁。备案把这件事变成可查：一个编号对应一个机构名称、一条统一社会信用代码。
For an unfiled agency, no number can be found, the responsible entity is unclear, and when documents go wrong there is no one to hold accountable. Filing makes this checkable: one number corresponds to one institution name and one unified social credit code.

第十二条的服务台账已经写进流程：每份申请对应的院校、专业、材料版本、递交时间与结果，留档可查。从选校匹配、申请代办、JW202 表材料准备与签证辅导，到行前落地与居留许可办理，每一步都有记录，也能对应到具体经办人。
The service records required by Article 12 are already built into the workflow: for each application, the institution, programme, document version, submission date and outcome are kept on file. From programme matching and application handling to JW202 preparation, visa coaching, pre-departure arrangements and the residence permit, every step is recorded and traceable to the person who handled it.

来华留学不是“交钱等通知”，每一步都要有人对结果负责。有备案的中介，就是这道链子里不可或缺、也能被追责的一环。
Studying in China is not a matter of paying and waiting for a notice; every step needs someone accountable for the outcome. A filed agency is the indispensable — and accountable — link in that chain.
