Today's China-Study University Pick · East China University of Science & Technology (ECUST)

今日来华留学高校推荐 · 华东理工大学（ECUST）

Today's University【今日推荐大学】

East China University of Science and Technology (ECUST) was founded in 1952 as the East China Institute of Chemical Technology (华东化工学院), formed by merging the chemistry departments of Jiaotong University (Shanghai), Aurora University, Datong University, Soochow University (Suzhou) and Jiangnan University (Wuxi). It is the first higher institution in New China renowned for chemical engineering. Renamed ECUST in 1993, it is a national Project 211 university (1996), a Double First-Class university (2017), and holds the 985 Platform (2008). Its three Double First-Class disciplines are Chemistry, Materials Science & Engineering, and Chemical Engineering & Technology. ECUST runs three campuses in Shanghai (Xuhui, Fengxian, Xuhui South), covers 2,336.9 mu, and holds a library of 3.54 million volumes. It has enrolled international students since 1956 and is an official HSK test center. Chemistry, Engineering and Materials science all rank in the global ESI top 1‰; its Chemical Engineering program is ABET-accredited, and the business school holds AACSB / EQUIS / AMBA&BGA accreditation.

华东理工大学（ECUST）原名华东化工学院，1952 年由交通大学（上海）、震旦大学、大同大学、东吴大学（苏州）、江南大学（无锡）等校化工系合并组建，是新中国第一所以化工特色闻名的高等学府。1993 年更名；1996 年入"211 工程"；2008 年获"985 工程优势学科创新平台"；2017 年入选国家"双一流"建设高校。3 个"双一流"建设学科为化学、材料科学与工程、化学工程与技术。学校在上海有徐汇、奉贤、徐汇南 3 个校区，占地 2336.9 亩，图书馆藏书 354 万册。自 1956 年起招收国际学生，是承办 HSK 的官方考点。化学、工程学、材料学均进入 ESI 全球 前 1‰；化学工程与工艺通过 ABET 认证，商学院通过 AACSB / EQUIS / AMBA&BGA 认证。

One-line Highlight【学校一句话亮点】

A 211 & Double First-Class university in Shanghai, world-famous for chemical engineering — China's "cradle of chemical engineers," with English-taught joint degrees and an on-campus HSK test center.

上海的 211 与"双一流"高校，以化工闻名世界——被誉为中国"化学工程师的摇篮"，开设英文授课联合学位，并设有校内 HSK 考点。

Who It's Best For【适合哪些留学生】

ECUST is a strong fit for future chemical / process engineers, materials scientists and bioengineers (its core strength); students aiming for English-taught STEM degrees (international joint multi-degree programs in Chemical Engineering, Bioengineering, Intelligent Manufacturing, AI, International Business, Visual Communication Design, etc.); business and economics students who want Chinese-taught or English-taught tracks; learners who want a solid, applied, industry-linked STEM education in Shanghai without the C9 ultra-competition; and those who value being able to take the HSK exam right on campus.

华东理工大学非常适合未来的化工、过程、材料与生物工程师（华理核心优势）；希望读英文授课理工学位的同学（国际联合多学位项目：化学工程、生物工程、智能制造、人工智能、国际经济与贸易、视觉传达设计等）；商科与经济类学生（中文或英文通道）；希望在上海接受扎实、应用型、产教结合 STEM 教育、又不想卷最顶尖 C9 的同学；以及看重"校内即可考 HSK"便利的人。

Strengths & Programs【优势专业 / 项目】

Chemical Engineering & Technology: a Double First-Class discipline and ABET-accredited; ECUST is literally called China's "cradle of chemical engineers." 
Materials Science & Engineering and Chemistry: both Double First-Class; Chemistry ranks in the global ESI top 1‰. 
Bioengineering, Food Science & Pharmacy: a strong applied life-science cluster. 
English-taught International Joint Multi-Degree (3-year): Chemical Engineering, Bioengineering (Biomedical), Intelligent Manufacturing Engineering, Artificial Intelligence, International Business, Visual Communication Design (Digital Design) — with dual-degree options at partner universities abroad. 
Business School (AACSB / EQUIS / AMBA&BGA accredited): Economics, Finance, Accounting, etc. 
The 2026 undergraduate catalogue spans 50+ majors across 17 schools.

化学工程与技术："双一流"学科、ABET 认证，华理被誉为中国"化学工程师的摇篮"。
材料科学与工程、化学：均入选"双一流"，化学进入 ESI 全球 前 1‰。
生物工程、食品科学、药学：扎实的应用生命科学集群。
英文授课国际联合多学位（三年制）：化学工程、生物工程（生物医药）、智能制造工程、人工智能、国际经济与贸易、视觉传达设计（数字设计）等，含赴外方合作院校的双学位通道。
商学院（AACSB / EQUIS / AMBA&BGA 认证）：经济学、金融学、会计学等。
2026 年本科招生专业目录覆盖 17 个学院 50 余个专业。

Language & Admission【语言与入学要求】

For Chinese-taught undergraduate programs, ECUST requires HSK 4, with Science / Engineering ≥ 190, Humanities / Business ≥ 210, and Writing ≥ 60; a prior Chinese-taught degree can waive the HSK certificate. For English-taught programs, ECUST accepts TOEFL 80, IELTS 6.0, or Duolingo 105 (within two years), or a native-English background / prior English-taught degree. CSCA is mandatory for all international undergraduates — the China-Study College Admission test (来华留学本科入学学业水平测试). Subjects follow your major: Science Chinese + Math + Physics (most engineering, CS, AI, robotics, automation, mechanical); Humanities Chinese + Math (business, economics, social science, law, foreign languages, design); Science Chinese + Math + Physics + Chemistry (chemical engineering, applied chemistry, materials chemistry, bioengineering, food, pharmacy); English-taught joint degrees have their own sets (e.g., ChemE = Math + Physics + Chemistry). Applicants must be 16+; those under 18 need a guardian guarantee.

中文授课本科要求 HSK4：理工科 ≥190、文商科 ≥210、书写 ≥60；既往全中文授课学位可免 HSK 证书。英文授课认可 TOEFL 80、IELTS 6.0 或 Duolingo 105（两年内），或英语母语 / 既往全英文授课学位。CSCA 是所有国际本科生的必备材料——即"来华留学本科入学学业水平测试"。科目随专业而定：理科中文 + 数学 + 物理（多数工程、计算机、人工智能、机器人、自动化、机械等）；文科中文 + 数学（商科、经济、社科、法学、外语、设计等）；理科中文 + 数学 + 物理 + 化学（化工、应化、材料化学、生物工程、食品、药学等）；英文授课联合学位另有对应科目（如化工 = 数学 + 物理 + 化学）。申请者须 16 周岁以上；未满 18 岁须提供监护保证书。

About HSK 3.0: the new standard uses "three tiers, nine levels" (Level 1–3 beginner / 4–6 intermediate / 7–9 advanced) and begins global rollout on 13 December 2026. Most programs (including ECUST) still publish requirements in the legacy HSK scale; if a program references the new HSK 7–9, it generally maps to the former HSK 6. Always confirm which level and which HSK version your target program expects. For exact cutoffs, program lists, and deadlines, 建议与 Origineduglob 的留学顾问联系.

HSK 3.0 背景提示：新版 HSK 采用"三等九级"（初级 1-3 / 中级 4-6 / 高级 7-9），于 2026 年 12 月 13 日起全球启用。目前多数专业（含华理）仍以旧版 HSK 等级发布要求；若某专业采用新版 HSK7-9，通常对应原 HSK6。请务必向目标专业确认所需等级与 HSK 版本。具体的分数线、可申请专业清单、截止日期，建议与 Origineduglob 的留学顾问联系确认。

City & Living【城市与生活】

ECUST's Xuhui campus sits in downtown Shanghai — directly served by Metro Line 15 (ECUST Station), surrounded by the city's universities, research institutes and enterprises; the newer, larger Fengxian campus is a self-contained, green student town. Shanghai is China's largest and most international city — a global finance, tech and culture hub with a huge international-student and expat community, world-class metro, and abundant HSK test centers (including ECUST's own). The trade-off is cost: Shanghai's living expenses are among the highest in China, though on-campus dorms keep housing affordable.

华理徐汇校区位于上海市中心，地铁 15 号线（华东理工大学站）直达，周边高校、科研院所与企业云集；更新、更大的奉贤校区是配套完善的绿色大学城。上海是中国最大、最国际化的城市——全球金融、科技与文化枢纽，国际学生与外籍社群庞大，地铁发达，HSK 考点充足（含华理本校）。代价是成本：上海生活开销居全国前列，不过校内宿舍能让住宿保持可负担。

Scholarships & Tips【奖学金与申请提示】

ECUST offers four scholarship channels for international students: the Chinese Government Scholarship (Type A, Chinese-taught only); the Shanghai Municipal Government Scholarship (Type B, Chinese-taught only); the Shanghai Municipal Government Scholarship (1+4 Program, Chinese-taught only); and the ECUST President's Scholarship. Application timeline (2026 intake reference): scholarship applications 15 Nov 2025 – 15 Apr 2026; self-funded 15 Nov 2025 – 20 Jul 2026. Tip: because CSCA is mandatory and subject-specific, start your subject prep early and align it with your intended major.

华理为国际学生提供四类奖学金：中国政府奖学金（A 类，仅限中文授课）；上海市政府奖学金（B 等，仅限中文授课）；上海市政府奖学金（1+4 项目，仅限中文授课）；以及华东理工大学校长奖学金。申请时间（以 2026 年招生为参考）：奖学金申请 2025-11-15 至 2026-04-15；自费申请 2025-11-15 至 2026-07-20。建议：CSCA 为必备且按专业设科目，尽早开始科目备考，并与意向专业对齐。

🎓 奖学金的具体金额、覆盖比例、申请条件与轮次每年不同，且多数学金仅限中文授课——建议与 Origineduglob 的留学顾问联系，评估你最适合的申请通道。

One Tip for You【给留学生的一句话建议】

At ECUST you don't just study chemistry — you study how China turns science into industry; let Shanghai be your lab, and let the HSK exam center on campus be your milestone.

在华理，你学的不仅是化学，更是中国如何把科学变成产业；让上海成为你的实验室，让校内 HSK 考点成为你成长的里程碑。
