今日推荐大学 ｜ Today's University

EN — Inner Mongolia University of Finance and Economics (IMUFE, 内蒙古财经大学) is a public university founded in 1960 and based in Hohhot, the capital of the Inner Mongolia Autonomous Region in northern China. As its name suggests, it specializes in finance, economics, management, trade and tourism — a focused, practice-oriented business school rather than a comprehensive research mega-university. Its campus covers about 1.35 million square metres and enrolls roughly 22,000 students. For international students it offers Chinese-taught bachelor's degrees in business fields plus a Chinese-language foundation program, in a city where the cost of living is far below China's coastal first-tier metros.

中文 — 内蒙古财经大学（IMUFE）创办于 1960 年，坐落于中国北部内蒙古自治区首府呼和浩特。顾名思义，学校以财经、经济、管理、贸易、旅游为特色，是一所以应用见长的财经类高校，而非综合性研究型巨校。校园占地约 135 万平方米，在校生约 2.2 万人。面向国际学生，学校提供中文授课的财经类本科以及汉语进修（预科）项目，所在城市的生活成本远低于沿海一线城市。

学校一句话亮点 ｜ One-line Highlight

EN — A specialized finance-and-economics university in Hohhot where affordable, Chinese-taught business degrees meet authentic grassland and Mongolian culture.

中文 — 坐落呼和浩特、以财经见长的特色高校——中文授课的财经本科性价比高，草原与蒙古族文化触手可及。

适合哪些留学生 ｜ Who This University Is For

EN — IMUFE is a good fit for students aiming for careers in economics, finance, international trade, tourism or business administration; learners who want a more affordable, less hyper-competitive study destination than Beijing, Shanghai or coastal hubs; students curious about Inner Mongolian and Mongolian culture, grassland scenery and northern-China life; and beginners who have not yet reached HSK 4 — the Chinese-language (汉语进修) program requires no HSK to enrol and builds you up toward HSK 1–4 before a degree.

中文 — 内蒙古财经大学尤其适合：想在经济学、金融学、国际贸易、旅游管理、工商管理方向发展的同学；希望去比北京、上海等沿海都市更划算、竞争更温和的地方求学的同学；对内蒙古与蒙古族文化、草原风光、北方生活感兴趣的同学；以及暂未达 HSK4、想先打语言基础的同学——汉语进修项目入学不要求 HSK，可一路学到 HSK1–4 再衔接学位。

优势专业与项目 ｜ Strength Programs

EN — IMUFE's international undergraduate offering (2026 self-financed brochure) centres on five Chinese-taught bachelor's majors: Economics (经济学), Finance (金融学), International Economics and Trade (国际经济与贸易), Tourism Management (旅游管理) and Business Administration (工商管理). It also runs a non-degree Chinese-language program (汉语进修生) with courses from foundational Chinese up to HSK 4, designed as a pathway into a Chinese-taught degree. Note: IMUFE is a regional, specialized finance-and-economics university (not a 211 / 985 / Double First-Class university) — its strength is a concentrated, career-oriented business curriculum rather than breadth across sciences or medicine.

中文 — 内蒙古财经大学国际本科（2026 自费来华留学本科生招生简章）开设 5 个中文授课本科专业：经济学、金融学、国际经济与贸易、旅游管理、工商管理。学校另设非学历汉语进修项目（汉语进修生），课程从基础汉语覆盖到 HSK4，可作为升读中文授课学位的衔接路径。需说明：内蒙古财经大学是一所区域特色鲜明的财经类高校（非 211 / 985 /"双一流"），其优势在于聚焦、面向就业的财经商科培养，而非理、工、医等学科的广度。

语言与入学要求 ｜ Language & Admission

EN — From IMUFE's official 2026 international admissions brochures: Bachelor's (Chinese-taught) require a valid HSK 4 certificate (score ≥180); applicant must be a non-Chinese citizen, aged 18–25, with a high school diploma; duration 4 years (max 6); admission includes an interview. Application window (2026): June 1 – July 30, by email to lmufegjy@imufe.edu.cn. The Chinese-language program requires no HSK to enrol (age 18+, high-school certificate; courses cover HSK 1–4; Sept 2026 intake). CSCA note: IMUFE's 2026 brochure does NOT list CSCA as a requirement — only HSK 4 + interview; but China is rolling out CSCA under HSK 3.0 reform, so confirm applicability with the admissions office / an advisor. HSK 3.0 note: reformed HSK is "three levels, nine grades" (L1–3 / L4–6 / L7–9); IMUFE still uses the legacy HSK 4 banding (≥180) — confirm conversion with an advisor.

中文 — 依据内蒙古财经大学 2026 年官方国际招生简章：本科（中文授课）须持有效 HSK4 证书（≥180）；申请人须为非中国籍、18–25 周岁、具高中及以上学历；学制 4 年（最长 6 年）；录取含面试。申请时间（2026）：6 月 1 日–7 月 30 日，材料发送至 lmufegjy@imufe.edu.cn。汉语进修项目入学不要求 HSK（18 周岁以上、具高中毕业证；课程覆盖 HSK1–4；2026 年秋季入学）。CSCA 提示：学校 2026 简章未将 CSCA 列为要求，仅要求 HSK4 + 面试；但中国正随 HSK 3.0 改革逐步推行 CSCA，是否适用建议向招生办或顾问确认。HSK 3.0 提示：新版"三等九级"（初级 L1–3/中级 L4–6/高级 L7–9），学校仍按旧版 HSK4（≥180），换算请向顾问确认。

城市与生活 ｜ City & Living

EN — Hohhot (呼和浩特) is the capital of Inner Mongolia, where Han Chinese and Mongolian cultures meet. It sits on the southern edge of the grasslands — the Xilamuren grassland is about a 1.5-hour drive away — and is famous for dairy (Yili and Mengniu are Inner-Mongolia headquartered brands), roast lamb, hand-grabbed mutton (手把肉) and salty milk tea. Winters are cold and dry but sunny; summers are mild. The cost of living is markedly lower than in Beijing, Shanghai or coastal first-tier cities, and on-campus accommodation is listed at RMB 4,000 per academic year in the 2026 brochure. For HSK exam preparation, Hohhot has local authorized test centres — confirm the latest venues and sessions on www.chinesetest.cn or with an advisor.

中文 — 呼和浩特是内蒙古自治区首府，是一座汉族与蒙古族文化交汇的城市。它地处草原南缘——希拉穆仁草原距市区约 1.5 小时车程；这里还是乳业重镇（伊利、蒙牛均为内蒙古本土品牌），以烤全羊、手把肉、咸奶茶闻名。冬季干冷但日照充足，夏季凉爽。生活成本明显低于北京、上海等沿海一线城市；2026 简章列明校内住宿 4,000 元/学年。HSK 备考方面，呼和浩特当地设有汉语考试授权考点——具体考点与场次以 www.chinesetest.cn 公布或向顾问确认为准。

奖学金与申请提示 ｜ Scholarships & Tips

EN — IMUFE awards its own "Academic Scholarship" (学业奖学金) to outstanding self-financed students: First class RMB 10,000 / year (top 10%), Second class RMB 7,000 / year (top 20%), Third class RMB 5,000 / year (top 30%), paid monthly over 10 months. Beyond the school scholarship, students may also apply for the Chinese Government Scholarship (CSC) and provincial/institutional awards — exact quotas and amounts vary by year and should be confirmed with an Origineduglob advisor. 2026 official fees: undergraduate tuition RMB 14,000 / year; on-campus accommodation RMB 4,000 / year; insurance RMB 800 / year. The Chinese-language program tuition is RMB 10,000 / year (2026). With Hohhot's low daily costs, IMUFE is one of the more budget-friendly options for studying business in China.

中文 — 内蒙古财经大学为优秀自费留学生设立"学业奖学金"：一等奖 10,000 元/年（约 10%）、二等奖 7,000 元/年（约 20%）、三等奖 5,000 元/年（约 30%），分 10 个月逐月发放。此外，学生还可申请中国政府奖学金（CSC）及省/校各级奖项——具体名额与金额逐年不同，建议与 Origineduglob 留学顾问确认。2026 年官方费用：本科学费 14,000 元/年；校内住宿 4,000 元/年；保险费 800 元/年。汉语进修项目学费 10,000 元/年（2026）。叠加呼和浩特较低的日常开销，IMUFE 是在华攻读商科中性价比较高的选择之一。
