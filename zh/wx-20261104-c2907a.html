You Priced Three Countries. You Didn't Price This One. | 你算了三个国家，唯独没算这个

By ORIGIN Education · 2026-11-04 · 原文：微信公众号

ENIf you have opened this, you are probably already deep in the arithmetic. You know what a year in the UK costs. You know what a year in the US costs, and you know which direction that number has been moving. You may have a spreadsheet with four columns and a currency tab.

中文如果你点开这篇，说明你大概已经在算账了。英国一年多少、美国一年多少、汇率往哪边走，你心里有数。你的表格里大概已经有四栏，还有一个换算页签。

ENThis piece adds one more row. Not because the others are wrong — they are not — but because a list built on four countries, when there are dozens, is not a shortlist. It is a habit.

中文这篇文章只补一行。不是前面那几栏算错了——它们没错——是因为世界上有几十个目的地，你只看了四个的时候，那不叫清单，那叫习惯。

ENHere is that row, in one line. Four years of undergraduate study in the United States: CNY 2.5 to 3 million. In the UK: CNY 720,000 to 1.47 million. In China: CNY 320,000 to 480,000.

中文先把这一行摆出来。美国读四年本科：250 万到 300 万人民币。英国：72 万到 147 万。中国：32 万到 48 万。

ENThat is not a discount. That is a different order of magnitude. And before you decide what it means — be careful. The number on its own tells you nothing about whether it is worth it. The rest of this piece is about that.

中文这不是打折，这是另一个数量级。但在你决定这意味着什么之前，先别急——光看这个数字，什么都说明不了。值不值，是后面整篇文章的事。

ENChina hosts around 380,000 international students from 191 countries, across roughly 1,100 institutions in 30 provinces. Of those, 205,000 are degree-seeking, and postgraduates are 35% of that group. Engineering is the single largest field, at 27.8% of degree students — ahead of medicine and management.

中文中国目前有约 38 万名国际学生，来自 191 个国家，分布在 30 个省份的 1100 多所院校。其中学历生 20.5 万，研究生占 35%。工学是最大的学科门类，占学历生 27.8%，排在医学和管理学前面。

ENThat cuts both ways, and both sides are real. Less competition for supervision and attention. A thinner alumni network pointing back to your own country. You will meet far fewer people who can open doors in your home market, and far more who can open doors in a market you have never worked in. Decide which of those two you need more.

中文这件事有两面，而且两面都是真的。老师关注更集中、同类竞争更少；但指向你本国的校友网络更薄。你会遇到的多是能在中国市场给你开门的人，少的是能回你本国给你开门的人。你自己权衡更需要哪一种。

ENSo why is this row not in your spreadsheet already? For most families we speak to, the answer is not a judgement about quality. It is that nobody ever put the numbers in front of them. There is no brochure in the school counsellor's office. No booth at the fair. The information is published, in English, every year — it just does not walk over to you.

中文那为什么这一行不在你表里？我们接触过的大多数家庭，答案不是「觉得中国不行」，而是——没人把数字放到他们面前过。升学顾问的办公室里没有它的册子，教育展上没有它的展位。信息是公开的，每年都有，也有英文版——只是它不会自己走到你面前。

First: three questions before any number

第一，先问三个问题，再谈钱

1. How much can you put in, every year, reliably — not how much you can scrape together once.
2. What is your child's Chinese level today: HSK 4, HSK 2, or zero?
3. In the year they graduate, where do you intend them to be standing?

1. 每年稳定能拿出多少？（不是能凑出多少，是每年。）
2. 孩子现在的中文到什么水平：HSK 四级、二级，还是零基础？
3. 毕业那一年，你希望他站在哪儿？

ENSkip these three and everything downstream is noise. It is the same as buying a flat: if you will not state a budget, an agent who shows you villas is not being helpful, he is wasting your afternoon. Study abroad is a four-year cash flow. The number that decides where you can go is the annual one, not the total.

中文这三问不答，后面全是废话。跟买房一个道理：你不说预算，中介带你去看别墅，那不叫服务，那叫浪费你一个下午。留学是四年的现金流，决定你能去哪儿的是「每年」那个数，不是「一共」那个数。

Second: what a ranking can and cannot tell you

第二，排名能告诉你什么，不能告诉你什么

ENRankings are built for universities, not for families. QS weights academic reputation at 30% — that is a survey of tens of thousands of academics, who score on papers, labs and peer prestige. When your child sits in an interview, which recruiter opens a ranking table to compare number 137 against number 152? None. They ask what you can do, and how long until you are useful.

中文排名是给学校用的，不是给家庭用的。QS 的学术声誉权重占 30%，那是发问卷给几万个学者，他们打分看的是论文、实验室、同行名气。你孩子去面试的时候，哪个招聘官会打开排名表去比第 137 名和第 152 名？没有。人家问的是你会干什么，多久能上手。

ENThis is not an argument that rankings are worthless. They have concrete uses: some employers' campus shortlists, some immigration and residency scoring systems, some scholarship eligibility rules. Use them as a tool. But notice that the market is already moving — QS's own survey shows the share of students weighing employability rising from 36% to 44% between 2020 and 2026, and 59% now weigh it when judging teaching quality. The habit of ranking-first is being dropped fastest by the families who chased it hardest.

中文这不是说排名一文不值。它有具体用途：部分雇主的校招名单、部分国家的居留与人才打分、部分奖学金的资格条件。当工具用。但你要注意到市场已经在动——QS 自己的调研显示，2020 到 2026 年，把就业率当重要考量的学生占比从 36% 涨到 44%；评估教学质量时看就业率的有 59%。追排名追得最凶的那批家庭，放弃得也最快。

ENWhat transfers instead is the subject. Engineering is 27.8% of degree-seeking international students in China, ahead of medicine and management. That is not a marketing achievement. That is global industry demand voting with its feet, and it is the most useful signal in this article.

中文真正能带走的是专业。工学占在华学历国际学生的 27.8%，排在医学和管理学前面。这不是招生宣传的成绩，这是全球产业需求在用脚投票——也是这篇文章里最有用的一个信号。

Fourth: the scholarship that changes the arithmetic

第四，那个改写账本的奖学金

ENThis is the section almost nobody outside China reads, and it is worth more than everything above. The Chinese Government Scholarship, in full, covers: tuition, accommodation (or a housing allowance), a monthly stipend, and comprehensive medical insurance at CNY 800 a year.

中文这一节，中国境外几乎没人认真读过，但它比上面所有内容都值钱。中国政府奖学金全额，覆盖：学费、住宿（或住宿补贴）、每月生活费、以及 800 元一年的综合医疗保险。

ENDo the arithmetic on one undergraduate. Tuition CNY 25,000. Dormitory CNY 6,000. Stipend CNY 2,500 × 12 = CNY 30,000. Insurance CNY 800. That is a package worth roughly CNY 62,000 a year — about CNY 250,000 across four years that you do not pay.

中文拿一个本科生算。学费 2.5 万，住宿 6000，生活费 2500 × 12 = 3 万，保险 800。一年是 6.2 万左右的一个资助包，四年下来差不多 25 万，你不用掏。

ENCompare the odds. For an international undergraduate in the United States, a full ride is rare enough that most counsellors will not promise it — many schools are need-aware, so asking for aid affects admission. In the UK, full scholarships at undergraduate level for international students are close to non-existent. Here it is a published, annual, government-funded programme with embassies as the intake channel.

中文再对比概率。美国本科国际生拿全奖，稀缺到绝大多数顾问不敢承诺——很多学校是 need-aware，你要钱就影响录取。英国本科阶段面向国际生的全奖，基本等于没有。而这边是一个公开的、每年都有的、政府出钱的、以大使馆为受理渠道的项目。

The one requirement that silently disqualifies people · 一条会静默淘汰你的要求

Since the 2026/27 academic year, undergraduate applicants for the Chinese Government Scholarship must submit a CSCA score. Mathematics and Major Chinese are compulsory; whether Physics or Chemistry is required depends on the university and the programme. Score submission deadlines have fallen around 31 May — miss it and it is treated as waiving your place.

自 2026/27 学年起，申请中国政府奖学金本科项目，必须提交 CSCA（来华留学本科入学学业水平测试）成绩。数学和专业中文为必考科目，物理或化学是否必考取决于院校和专业。成绩提交截止时间通常在 5 月 31 日前后——错过，视为放弃录取资格。很多家庭材料准备了一整年，最后卡在这一张成绩单上。

ENAnd now the number that keeps this honest. Government scholarship students are roughly 8% of international students in China — which means about 92% pay their own way. Read that the right way round: self-funding is the normal path here, not the last resort. The scholarship is a bonus, not the premise.

中文再说一个让这件事保持诚实的数字：政府奖学金生约占在华国际学生的 8%。也就是说约 92% 是自费的。这个数字要正着读——自费在这里是常态，不是退路。奖学金是加分项，不是前提。

Fifth: cheap is not a reason

第五，便宜不是理由

ENI have never told anyone to pick a place because it is cheap. Cheap is an entry ticket, not an argument. If you spend CNY 80,000 and someone else spends CNY 600,000, and on graduation day the two students have the same ability and the same options, the CNY 80,000 was the smart money. But if it bought an unknown university, a language they cannot speak and a certificate nobody uses, then you did not save — you spent four years and lost the interest.

中文我从来不劝人因为便宜选一个地方。便宜是入场券，不是理由。你花 8 万，别人花 60 万，毕业那天两个孩子能力一样、去处一样，那 8 万是聪明的钱。但如果这 8 万买来的是一个没人知道的学校、一门不会说的语言、一张用不上的文凭，那你不是省了，你是花了四年还赔了利息。

ENSo the calculation has to run past graduation. Three real paths, and they are not equally open.

中文所以这笔账必须算到毕业以后。三条真实路径，敞开程度并不一样。

· Work in China
The routes exist and the thresholds are real: Z visa plus work permit, the outstanding-graduate policy, free-trade-zone facilitation pilots, and the K visa introduced in 2025 — which is not itself a work permit. Conditions change yearly; check the current official notices.留华就业　通道是有的，门槛也是真的。Z 字签证 + 工作许可、优秀外籍高校毕业生政策、部分自贸区的便利化试点、以及 2025 年新增的 K 字签证（青年科技人才，但持 K 签不能直接全职工作）。具体条件逐年调整，必须以当年官方公告为准。

· Work back home, or for a Chinese company there
76 Chinese firms rank among the world's top international contractors, with USD 127.3 billion in overseas revenue in 2024. The chairman of CRRC put it plainly: localised talent that understands local rules is the key to overseas operations. Chinese fluency, four years on the ground, plus fluency in your own home market — that combination is scarce.回国就业，或进入你所在国的中资企业　76 家中国企业上榜全球顶级国际承包商，2024 年国际营业额 1,273.2 亿美元。中国中车董事长孙永才的原话是「通晓属地规则的本土化人才是海外经营关键」。会中文、在中国待过四年、懂中国的规则，同时懂你自己的市场——这个组合稀缺。

· Progress to a third country
Chinese degrees are better recognised across the Commonwealth system than most people assume — but it varies sharply by institution and by programme. Verify each destination university's postgraduate entry requirements yourself.第三国升学　中国学位在英联邦体系内的认可度比很多人想的高，但具体到学校、具体到专业，差异很大。必须逐校核对对方的研究生入学要求，不要听中介一句话就信。

ENNote what is and is not being claimed. This is not a promise of a job. It is a statement about scarcity: Chinese fluency, four years on the ground, plus fluency in your own home market — very few people hold all three. Your salary is proportional to how hard you are to replace. That has not changed anywhere.

中文注意我在说什么，也在说什么。这不是就业承诺，这是一个关于稀缺性的判断：中文能力、四年在地经验、加你自己母国市场——这三样同时具备的人很少。你的工资永远和你的不可替代性成正比，这条在哪儿都一样。

Sixth: three places this goes wrong

第六，三个最容易出错的地方

· 1. Price the tuition, ignore the city
Beijing and Shanghai run CNY 3,500–5,500 a month; Xi'an runs CNY 2,000–2,500. On the same CNY 20,000 tuition, the annual living cost gap alone can reach CNY 20,000–36,000. Pick the wrong city and every yuan you saved on tuition goes straight back out.只算学费，不算城市　北京上海 3,500–5,500 元/月，西安 2,000–2,500 元/月。同样是 2 万元的学费，一年的生活成本能差出 2 万到 3.6 万。城市选错，学费省的那点钱全填进去。

· 2. Pay more to avoid learning Chinese
English-taught programmes cost 20–40% more than Chinese-taught ones. You pay a premium for the comfort of not learning Chinese, and graduate without the language and without real exposure to the Chinese market. That is the most expensive way to save money.为了「不用学中文」选英文授课　英文授课比中文授课贵 20%–40%。你多花了钱，买了一个「不用学中文」的舒适区，结果毕业时既没学会中文，也没真正进过中国市场。这是最贵的一种省钱。

· 3. The extra preparatory year
Fall short on HSK and you spend a year in language preparation first. That year is pure cost — it counts toward neither the degree nor the programme length, and plenty of students finish it still short. Start the language now, or budget the year in.语言预科多出来的一年　HSK 不到线就要先读一年汉语预科。这一年是纯成本：不算学位、不算学制，而且不少人读完还是不够。要么现在就开始学，要么把这个成本先算进总账。

Seventh: the ten-year question

第七，十年之后那个问题

ENI will ask this once. In ten years, when your child is in a hiring conversation, what makes them hard to replace? If the answer is "I have a foreign degree" — that is worth nothing; millions are issued every year. If the answer is "I speak Chinese, I spent four years there, I understand how that market and its rules and its people actually work, and I can still go back to my own market" — very few people on earth hold that.

中文这个问题我只问一次。十年以后，你孩子坐在面试桌前，靠什么让自己不可替代？如果答案是「我有一个外国学位」——不值钱，全世界一年发几百万个。如果答案是「我会中文，我在那儿待了四年，我懂那个市场、它的规则、它的人怎么打交道，同时我还能回我自己的市场」——这个东西，全世界没几个人有。

Eighth: five things to do this month

第八，这个月就能做的五件事

· 1. Budget per year, not in total
Study abroad is a four-year cash flow. The amount you can commit every year is the number that actually decides where you can go — a one-off lump sum is not.把预算算成「每年」，不是「一共」　留学是四年或更久的现金流。每年稳定能拿出多少，才是真正决定你能去哪儿的数字。

· 2. Start Chinese now, not after admission
Every HSK level you climb changes what you can study and what you pay. Language is the one investment where starting earlier is always the better trade.中文现在就开始，别等录取　HSK 每高一级，你能选的专业和能省的钱都不一样。语言是唯一一个「早开始一定划算」的投入。

· 3. Find your country's scholarship channel
The intake body and deadline differ by country, usually falling between January and April. Miss it and there is no late round.查清你所在国家的奖学金受理渠道　中国政府奖学金的受理机构与截止时间各国不同，通常在 1 月到 4 月之间。错过就是错过，没有补录。

· 4. Bachelor applicants: register for CSCA now
From 2026/27, undergraduate scholarship applications require a CSCA score. Mathematics and Major Chinese are compulsory; score submission closes around May.本科申请人：CSCA 现在报名　2026/27 学年起，奖学金本科申请必须提交 CSCA 成绩。数学和专业中文必考，成绩提交约在 5 月底截止。

· 5. Write down where you intend to be after graduating
Write it on paper, then work backwards to the university, the field and the city. If you cannot write it, you are not ready to spend the money yet.先把「毕业后在哪儿」写下来　写在纸上，倒推选校、选专业、选城市。写不出来，说明还没到交钱的时候。

ENOne last thing, and then it is your decision. You do not owe China a look. If the four countries you have already priced fit your budget, your language situation and your child's plans, that is a complete answer — and you should stop reading here.

中文最后一句，然后就是你的决定。你不欠中国一个考虑。如果你已经算过的那四个国家，符合你家的预算、语言条件和孩子的规划，那就是一个完整的答案——你可以关掉这篇文章了。

ENBut if you have read this far, something in the numbers made you pause. So do the one thing that costs nothing: put the row in. Price a year in China the same way you priced a year in Melbourne or Manchester — same inputs, tuition, rent, food, flights, insurance. Then compare the outputs, including the one that matters most: what your child can do in ten years that a cheaper or a more expensive graduate cannot.

FAQ · 常见问题

Is studying in China really that cheap, or is it a marketing number?

Both, and you have to separate them. The low end is real: a provincial university with Chinese-taught tuition at CNY 15,000–25,000, a dorm at CNY 400–1,000 a month, canteen meals at CNY 10–20 — that genuinely lands near CNY 50,000 a year. But that assumes Chinese-taught, a non-first-tier city, and on-campus housing. Move to an English-taught programme in Beijing and you are at CNY 100,000–120,000. Tuition is not nationally set; each university publishes its own schedule. Always read the university's own admission brochure, not an agency's.

在华留学真的这么便宜，还是营销数字？

两者都是，但必须拆开看。低端是真的：省属院校中文授课学费 1.5–2.5 万，校内宿舍 400–1000 元/月，食堂一顿 10–20 元——这样一年确实能压到 5 万上下。但前提是：中文授课、非一线城市、住校内。换成北京某校的英文授课项目，就是 10–12 万。学费不是全国统一定价，各校自己公布标准。所以永远看学校自己的招生简章。

Can my child work or stay after graduation?

That is a separate question from cost, and it deserves its own research. Short version: there are real routes — Z visa plus work permit, the outstanding foreign graduate policy, free-trade-zone facilitation pilots, and the K visa introduced in 2025 — but the K visa is not a work permit, and conditions change year to year. Do not accept a promise from an agency. Check the current notice from the exit-entry authority in the city where they intend to work.

毕业后能留下工作吗？

这是跟成本完全分开的另一个问题，值得单独研究。简短版：通道是真的有——Z 字签证加工作许可、优秀外籍高校毕业生政策、部分自贸区便利化试点、以及 2025 年新增的 K 字签证；但 K 签本身不是工作许可，而且条件逐年调整。不要接受中介的口头承诺。去孩子打算工作的那个城市的出入境管理部门，查当年现行公告。

Is the government scholarship realistic for an ordinary student?

It is competitive, and it is not the only route. Government scholarship students are roughly 8% of international students in China — which means about 92% are self-funded. That is the more useful number: self-funding is the normal path, not the fallback. Apply if your child qualifies, but plan the budget as if they will not get it.

政府奖学金普通学生拿得到吗？

有竞争，而且它不是唯一的路。政府奖学金生约占在华国际学生的 8%——也就是说约 92% 是自费。后面这个数字更有用：自费是常态，不是退路。够条件就申，但做预算的时候，按「拿不到」来算。

Does an undergraduate application require an exam?

Yes, if it is a Chinese Government Scholarship application. Since the 2026/27 academic year, undergraduate scholarship applicants must submit a CSCA score — Mathematics and Major Chinese are compulsory, and whether Physics or Chemistry is required depends on the university and programme. Score submission deadlines have fallen around 31 May; missing it is treated as waiving your place.

申请本科需要考试吗？

申请中国政府奖学金本科项目，需要。自 2026/27 学年起，奖学金本科申请人必须提交 CSCA 成绩——数学和专业中文为必考科目，物理或化学是否必考取决于院校和专业。成绩提交截止时间通常在 5 月 31 日前后，逾期视为放弃录取资格。

What about medical degrees?

It is the most expensive undergraduate field — roughly CNY 30,000–60,000 a year, English-taught at the top of that range. It is still several times cheaper than an English-taught medical degree in the UK, the US or Australia. But check two things before committing: whether the degree is recognised for licensing in the country where your child intends to practise, and what the clinical rotation arrangement actually is. Those matter more than the tuition figure.

医学专业呢？

它确实是本科阶段最贵的专业——大约 3–6 万元/年，英文授课取区间上限。但相比英美澳的英文授课医学学位，仍然便宜数倍。不过在做决定之前要查两件事：这个学位在孩子打算执业的国家是否被认可、临床轮转到底怎么安排。这两件事比学费数字重要得多。
