Integrity Is the Gate: Academic Honesty as a Scholarship Veto

By ORIGIN Education · 2026-11-15 · 原文：微信公众号

先给判断：中国政府奖学金早不是"考上就稳四年"。留金来管〔2026〕4号把年度评审做成硬关卡——60分合格线、道德品行占30%，诚实守信单列。学术不端和违纪，是中止甚至取消的硬线。

The rule: an annual hard gate

规则：年度硬关卡

ENUnder Notice 留金来管〔2026〕4, every Chinese Government Scholarship (CGS) student studying one academic year or longer must pass an annual review. The review scores four dimensions — moral character (30%), academic performance (30%), learning/research attitude (20%) and activity participation (20%) — and a total of 60 or above is required to keep the scholarship.

中文根据留金来管〔2026〕4号《关于做好2026年中国政府奖学金年度评审工作的通知》，就读一学年及以上的中国政府奖学金生均须参加年度评审。评审按四维度打分——道德品行（30%）、学习成绩（30%）、学习/科研态度（20%）、活动表现（20%）——总分60及以上方可继续享受奖学金。浙江大学、华中科技大学、浙江师范大学等校2026年通知均明确"60分及以上为合格"。

Integrity as a named gate

诚信被单点名

EN"Honesty and trustworthiness" is named explicitly within moral character. Tianjin University of Finance and Economics' 2026-10-27 review rules list respect for law, campus discipline, cultural tolerance and "consciously upholding civilised interaction" as the moral baseline; academic misconduct and disciplinary violations are treated as triggers for suspension or cancellation.

中文"诚实守信"在道德品行中被单独点名。天津财经大学2026-10-27发布的评审办法，把遵法守纪、尊重校规、包容多元、诚实守信列为道德底线；学术不端与违纪处分，被明确列为中止或取消资格的情形。道德品行与学习成绩各占30%，说明"做人"和"做学问"在评审里同等分量。

What cancels, what suspends

什么取消，什么中止

ENThe hard lines are clear. Zhejiang Ocean University's notice lists cancellation for breaking Chinese law, missing the review without cause, two consecutive failed reviews, or expulsion. Suspension applies to failed required courses, grade retention, or disciplinary punishment. Cancellation is permanent — the scholarship never returns.

中文硬线很清楚。浙江海洋大学的通知列明：触犯中国法律、无正当理由不参加评审、连续两次未通过、被勒令退学或开除学籍，予以取消资格。必修课不合格、留级降级、受违纪处分，则中止。取消是永久的——资格不再恢复。中止者须足额缴学费、住宿费与来华留学保险方可继续学习，且仅在下一次评审合格后方可申请恢复。

The real cost

真正的代价

ENA suspended student must pay full tuition, accommodation and insurance to keep studying, and can only apply to restore the scholarship after the next passing review. A cancelled student loses funding from the announcement date. The message is unambiguous: the scholarship is earned every year, not banked at admission.

中文被中止者须足额缴纳学费、住宿费与来华留学保险方可继续学习，且只能在下一次评审合格后方可申请恢复。被取消者自公布之日起停发。信号再明确不过：奖学金是每年"再挣一次"，不是入学时一次存进银行。这也正是"去中介化"时代的核心——靠严谨的学业与诚信，而非掮客。

English abstract

ENThe Chinese Government Scholarship (CGS) is no longer "win once, keep for four years." Under Notice 留金来管〔2026〕4, every CGS student studying a year or longer must pass an annual review scored on four dimensions — moral character (30%), academic performance (30%), learning/research attitude (20%) and activity participation (20%) — with 60/100 as the pass line. "Honesty and trustworthiness" is explicitly named within moral character; Tianjin University of Finance and Economics' 2026-10-27 rules treat academic misconduct and disciplinary violations as triggers for suspension or cancellation. Hard cancellation applies to breaking Chinese law, missing the review without cause, two consecutive failed reviews, or expulsion — and is permanent. A suspended student must pay full fees and can only restore the scholarship after a later passing review.
