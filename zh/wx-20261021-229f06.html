Study-in-China, Volunteering Together

By ORIGIN Education · 2026-10-21 · 原文：微信公众号

ENOn June 22, 2026, the "2026 Action Plan for International Student Volunteer Service in China" — branded "Study in China, Volunteering Together" (留学中国 志愿同行) — was officially released in Beijing. Initiated by the Chinese Service Center for Scholarly Exchange (CSCSE) under the Ministry of Education, the plan recruits the first cohort of 19 universities and spans cultural exchange, community service, educational support, environmental protection, rural revitalization, and international event service.

中文2026年6月22日，由教育部留学服务中心发起的"2026年来华留学生志愿服务行动计划"（品牌名称"留学中国 志愿同行"）在北京正式发布。行动计划首批吸纳19所高校加入，项目覆盖文化互鉴、社区服务、教育支持、环境保护、乡村振兴与国际赛会服务等多个领域。

ENAt the launch forum, Yang Dan, Director-General of the Ministry's Department of International Cooperation and Exchange, described volunteer service as "a vital practice classroom through which international students read, and融入, China." He urged universities to develop signature projects — "one university, one brand" (一校一品) — that help students step out of the classroom and into society to encounter a three-dimensional, vivid China.

中文在启动论坛上，教育部国际合作与交流司司长杨丹表示，志愿服务是"跨越国界、跨越文化的重要社会参与形式，也是国际学生读懂中国、融入中国的重要实践课堂"。他希望各校结合学科优势与区域特点，推动"一校一品"，培育一批叫得响、立得住、传得开的品牌项目，让更多来华留学生走出课堂、走进社会，认识立体、全面、鲜活的中国。

ENCSCSE Director Wang Daquan pledged to build a robust support system and operating guarantee mechanism, advancing projects toward standardization, institutionalization, and normalization — positioning volunteer service as a "boutique and distinctive project" within the broader "Study in China" brand system. At the same event, CSCSE and Beijing Foreign Studies University released the Action Plan's Operating Mechanism to ensure long-term, compliant implementation.

中文教育部留学服务中心主任王大泉表示，中心将持续整合高校、社会组织、用人单位与国际活动平台等多方资源，完善支持体系与运行保障机制，推动项目规范化、制度化、常态化，把志愿服务打造成"留学中国"品牌体系中的精品和特色项目。同期，留学服务中心联合北京外国语大学发布了《来华留学生志愿服务行动计划运行机制》，推进行动计划长效化、规范化实施。

We do not merely get students "in" — we accompany them to "thrive and belong."
