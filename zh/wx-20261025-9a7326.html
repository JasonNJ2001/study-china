Same Scholarship, Four Doors: Choosing a 2027 CSC Host

同一份奖学金，四扇不同的门：2027 年 CSC 院校怎么选

Four universities published their 2027/2028 Chinese Government Scholarship notices for the High-Level Graduate Programme in October 2026, and on paper they offer the same package: tuition waived, accommodation covered, and a monthly stipend of 3000 yuan for master's students or 3500 yuan for doctoral students. The similarity stops there. HSK thresholds, GPA floors, quotas, required videos and supervisor rules differ enough that the choice of host university is itself a strategic decision.

2026 年 10 月，四所高校陆续发布 2027/2028 学年中国政府奖学金"高水平研究生"项目简章。纸面上它们给的是同一个包：免学费、包住宿、硕士每月 3000 元、博士每月 3500 元。相似性到此为止。HSK 门槛、GPA 底线、推荐名额、视频材料、导师规则都不一样——选哪所学校，本身就是一项策略决定。

 

The Facts

事实梳理

All four are CSC-eligible institutions and all four apply the same selection principle: individual application, institutional recommendation, expert review, merit-based admission. What they cover differently is the subject band — comprehensive, agricultural, engineering, and traditional Chinese medicine. None is a top-ten university, and that is part of the argument for them. Competing for a CSC place at a top-ten school is a different order of magnitude, and the scholarship itself is issued by the China Scholarship Council, not by the host university.

这四所都是中国政府奖学金资格院校，都遵循同一套选拔原则：个人申请、院校推荐、专家评审、择优录取。它们真正的差别在学科带——综合、农业、工科、中医。它们都不是排名前十的学校，而这恰恰是值得考虑的理由：在顶尖院校争一个 CSC 名额是完全另一个量级的竞争；况且奖学金资格由国家留学基金委授予，不是学校授予。

The four notices differ most sharply on language and academic thresholds. Guizhou University is the only one that publishes a numeric floor for both language and grades.

四份简章差异最明显的地方，是语言与学术门槛。贵州大学是唯一同时给出语言分值和成绩底线的学校。

Guizhou University also runs the most demanding paperwork. Applicants upload a 2-to-3 minute self-introduction video, in Chinese or English, covering their background, why they chose the university and programme, and their study plan. The notice forbids post-editing, dubbing and voice-over. A supervisor acceptance form is issued by the university after review, and must then be uploaded to both the university platform and the CSC system.

贵州大学的材料要求也最重。申请人须上传 2 至 3 分钟的自我介绍视频，用中文或英文讲清个人情况、为什么选择这所学校和这个专业、以及来华学习规划。简章明确禁止后期剪辑与配音。导师接收函由学校在审核后发放，之后须同时上传到学校平台和 CSC 系统。

Jilin Agricultural University is the outlier on quota. It states that for 2027/2028 it can recommend only 5 applicants to the China Scholarship Council, with the final number subject to CSC approval. Its subject base is agricultural, and it lists participation in the China-Africa Hundred Universities Cooperation Plan. International travel is not covered.

吉林农业大学在名额上是特例。简章写明，2027/2028 学年只能向国家留学基金委推荐 5 名申请人，最终人数以基金委批复为准。学科以农业为主，并列入"中非高校百校合作计划"中方成员高校。国际旅费不在资助范围内。

Lanzhou University of Technology concentrates on engineering: materials science, control science, civil engineering, mechanical engineering, chemical engineering and related fields, with several programmes taught in English. Its language floor is the lowest in this group at IELTS 5.5 or TOEFL 80, but the research plan must be signed by a Chinese supervisor, which in practice means contact before application. Shanxi University of Chinese Medicine admits master's students only, in traditional Chinese medicine and related disciplines, with a three-year duration and an optional one-year Chinese preparatory stage. Its comprehensive medical insurance is stated at 800 yuan per person per year.

兰州理工大学集中在工科：材料科学与工程、控制科学与工程、土木工程、机械工程、化学工程与技术等，部分专业可英文授课。它的语言门槛是这一组里最低的，雅思 5.5 或托福 80；但研究计划须由中方导师签字，实际上意味着申请前就要建立联系。山西中医药大学只招硕士，方向为中医学、中药学等，学制 3 年，并设 1 年中文补习（预科）阶段。其综合医疗保险费标注为 800 元/人/年。

What This Means

深度解读

The lowest threshold is not the easiest door. Lanzhou's IELTS 5.5 looks inviting until you reach the supervisor signature requirement, which converts a document task into a relationship task. Securing a supervisor who will read and sign a research plan takes weeks, and a cold email in February with a deadline in February will not produce one. The applicants who succeed with this school start contacting departments in November.

门槛最低不等于门最好进。兰州理工的雅思 5.5 看起来友好，但一到"导师签字"这一条，整件事就从写材料变成了建立关系。找到一位愿意读并签署研究计划的导师需要数周，2 月才发第一封邮件、截止日又同在 2 月，基本不会有人回复。能走通这所学校的申请人，11 月就开始联系院系了。

Interviews are the step applicants most often underestimate. Guizhou University states that once preliminary review passes, the file goes to the relevant school, which organises its own interview and comprehensive assessment. That interview is run by subject teachers rather than admissions staff, and it is where a thin research plan becomes obvious. Preparing for it is not about rehearsing answers; it is about being able to say, in two minutes, what you want to study and why this particular department.

面试是最容易被低估的一环。贵州大学写明，材料初审合格后转至相应专业学院复审，由各学院自主组织面试和综合考评。面试由专业教师而非招生人员主持，一份单薄的研究计划在这里会暴露得很清楚。准备面试不是背答案，而是能在两分钟里说清你要研究什么、为什么是这个院系。

The smallest quota is usually the most competitive school, not the safest. Only 5 recommendations across the whole university means the internal screening happens before the CSC ever sees your file. A school with a February deadline and a 5-person quota gives you a longer calendar and a narrower funnel at the same time. If your grades are average, that is the wrong place to spend your only strong application.

名额最少的学校，通常不是最安全的，而是竞争最激烈的。全校只有 5 个推荐名额，意味着在基金委看到你的材料之前，校内筛选就已经发生。一所 2 月截止、只有 5 个名额的学校，给你的是一张更长的日历和一个更窄的漏斗。如果你的成绩只是中等，这里不是投放唯一一份强申请的地方。

Guizhou University's value is different: it is the only one that tells you, in numbers, whether you qualify. HSK 230 and a GPA of 78 per cent are checkable before you spend anything. That transparency cuts both ways. It filters you out early if you fall short, and it lets a candidate with a strong transcript but a thin research record argue their case with evidence rather than hope.

贵州大学的价值在另一处：它是唯一用数字告诉你够不够的学校。HSK 230 分、GPA 78%，在花钱之前就能自查。这种透明度是双向的：不够的人会被提前筛掉，而成绩单漂亮、科研经历偏薄的申请人，可以用证据而不是期待来说明自己。

Shanxi's March deadline and preparatory year make it a specific tool, not a general fallback. The one-year Chinese preparatory stage adds a year to the timeline, which matters if you are 34 and applying for a master's with a 35-year age ceiling. It is a genuine second chance for applicants who missed the December round; it is a poor first choice for someone who already has HSK 5 and wants to start in September.

山西中医的 3 月截止加预科年，使它成为一件专用工具，而不是通用保底。1 年中文补习会让整体时间线多出一年——如果你 34 岁、申请硕士、年龄上限 35 岁，这一点很关键。对错过 12 月那一轮的申请人，它是真实的第二次机会；对已经有 HSK 五级、希望 9 月就入学的人，它是个糟糕的首选。

What to Do

行动建议

Match first, then apply. If your transcript is strong and you can produce a coherent research statement, Guizhou is the clearest bet because the criteria are published. If you have a supervisor willing to commit in writing, Lanzhou offers the lowest language barrier and an engineering focus. If your background is agricultural and you can accept a 5-person funnel and self-paid flights, Jilin is the fit. If you are set on Chinese medicine, or you need a late deadline, Shanxi is the only option in this group.

先匹配，再申请。成绩单强、能写出清楚研究陈述的，贵大是最清晰的赌注，因为标准是公开的。已经有导师愿意书面承诺的，兰理工语言门槛最低、工科方向明确。农业背景、能接受 5 人漏斗和自理机票的，吉农是对口的。笃定走中医，或者需要一个更晚的截止日，这一组里只有山西中医。

Three practical sequences. Contact potential supervisors in November, not in the application month, and attach a one-page research outline rather than a CV alone. Prepare the video for Guizhou in one take against a plain background, since editing is explicitly banned. And if you are targeting Shanxi, calculate the preparatory year into your age and funding plan before you submit, not after you are admitted.

三条实操顺序。11 月就去联系潜在导师，不要等到申请当月，且附一页研究提纲而不是只发简历。贵大的视频要一次成片、背景干净，因为简章明确禁止剪辑。如果目标是山西中医，请在提交前就把预科年算进你的年龄与资金规划，而不是录取之后再算。

If you apply to more than one, build the dossier once and adapt it. The notarised degree certificate, the stamped transcripts, the medical form and the police clearance are identical across all four. What changes is the study plan, which must name the department and, where required, carry a supervisor's signature, and the language proof, which should be prepared against the highest threshold among your targets rather than the lowest.

如果同时申请多所，材料做一套、再分别调整。学历公证、盖章成绩单、体检表、无犯罪记录这四项四校通用。需要改的是学习计划——必须点到具体院系，需要时还要有导师签字；以及语言证明——要按你所有目标里最高的那个门槛去准备，而不是最低的。

Closing

结语

The scholarship is the same on all four websites. What you are actually choosing is a threshold you can clear, a funnel you can survive, and a city you will live in for three or four years. Read the four notices against your own transcript before you read anyone's ranking.

四所学校官网上写的奖学金是一样的。你真正在选的，是一个你过得去的门槛、一个你挤得进的漏斗，以及一座你要住三四年的城市。先拿自己的成绩单去读这四份简章，再去看任何排名。
