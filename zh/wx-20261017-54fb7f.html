Study-in-China Recast: Brand, Skills & Payment Ease

By ORIGIN Education · 2026-10-17 · 原文：微信公众号

ENIn March 2026, nine Chinese ministries — led by the Ministry of Commerce together with the Ministry of Education, the National Immigration Administration and others — issued the Policy Measures to Promote the Export of Travel Services and Expand Inbound Consumption. For the first time, "Study in China" is explicitly framed as a pillar of inbound education-service consumption, sitting alongside tourism, business, sports, entertainment and health. It is a clear signal: international education is no longer just a diplomatic or talent instrument — it is now a structured, brandable, consumption-grade service economy.

中文2026 年 3 月，由中国商务部牵头，会同教育部、国家移民管理局等共 9 部门联合印发《关于促进旅行服务出口 扩大入境消费的政策措施》。文件中首次把"留学中国"明确定位为"入境教育培训消费"的重要板块，与入境旅游、商务、赛事、文娱、健康并列。这是一个清晰信号：国际教育不再只是外交与人才工具，而是被纳入一套可品牌化、可消费的规模化服务经济体系。

ENThe policy's education block (sections 12–13) centers on brand and capability. It calls for strengthening the "Study in China" brand and capacity building, and for the first time in a cross-ministry document explicitly names the full management chain: sound recruitment and academic management, on-site pre-college education abroad, joint training with overseas universities, a refined entrance examination and assessment system, and — notably — standardized post-graduation work for outstanding international graduates in China. The inbound-student journey, from pre-departure prep to local employment, is being engineered as one coherent system.

中文政策的教育板块（第十二、十三条）以品牌与能力为核心。它提出加强"留学中国"品牌和能力建设，并首次在跨部门文件中完整点名来华留学管理链条：健全来华留学管理、鼓励赴境外开展在地预科教育、与境外高水平大学联合培养、完善留学生入学考试考核，以及——尤为关键——规范来华留学生及优秀毕业生在华工作。从出国前准备到在华就业，一条完整的留学服务链路正在被系统化设计。

ENA second pillar pushes "Chinese + vocational skills" as an export product. The measures encourage internationalization of Chinese-language institutions, cultivation of a Chinese-language testing brand, and — through vocational schools — "order-based training" with courses such as "Chinese + Tourism," "Chinese + High-Speed Rail Technology" and "Chinese + Cross-Border E-Commerce" to upskill the foreign employees of Chinese companies going global. This reframes language learning as a direct pipeline into China-linked careers, not merely cultural study.

中文第二大支柱是把"中文+职业技能"作为出海产品来推。措施鼓励中文教育机构国际化、培育中文考试品牌，并支持职业院校推广"订单式培养"，开设"中文+旅游""中文+高铁技术""中文+跨境电商"等"中文+职业技能"课程，为中国"走出去"企业的外籍员工提供培训。这让语言学习从单纯的文化研习，转变为直通中国相关职业的管道。

ENThe third pillar is friction removal — and it is where the "consumption" framing becomes concrete. The safeguards section (14–15) promises to steadily widen unilateral visa-free coverage, optimize transit visa-free rules, pilot e-visas with online application, and digitize the arrival card. Equally important for students: a multi-language, payment-friendly inbound environment — more multilingual signage at airports, rail stations and scenic areas, improved payment convenience, and encouragement for ride-hailing, food-delivery, shopping and navigation apps to ship multilingual versions. Studying and living in China is being made visibly easier.

中文第三大支柱是"去摩擦"——也正是"消费"定位落地的关键。保障措施（第十四、十五条）承诺有序扩大单方面免签范围、优化过境免签、试点网上申办电子签证、推行入境卡网上填报。对学生同样重要的是：构建多语种、支付便利的入境环境——机场、高铁、景区多语种标识覆盖率提升、支付便利性改善，并鼓励导航、打车、外卖、购物等生活服务软件提供多语种版本。在中国学习和生活，正在变得肉眼可见地更便捷。

ENFor prospective students, the practical reading is simple: the gate has moved from "can I get in" to "am I genuinely prepared and supported." A refined entrance-assessment system rewards verifiable readiness; "Chinese + skills" tracks reward clear career intent; and a frictionless daily-life environment rewards those who plan ahead. Transparent, self-driven preparation — not opaque brokers — is what the new framework actually rewards.

中文对意向申请者而言，现实含义很直接：门槛已从"能不能进"转向"是否真正准备充分、被妥善支持"。更完善的入学考核体系奖励可核验的准备度；"中文+职业"方向奖励清晰的职业意图；无摩擦的生活环境奖励提前规划的人。真正被新框架奖励的，是透明、自助式的准备，而非不透明的中介。
