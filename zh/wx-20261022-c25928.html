Origin EduGlob Visits NJUPT for Talks on IT Talent

By ORIGIN Education · 2026-10-22 · 原文：微信公众号

2026 年 10 月 21 日 · 南京邮电大学海外教育学院
October 21, 2026 — College of Overseas Education, NJUPT

尚源教育赴南京邮电大学交流座谈共商电子信息领域来华留学合作Origin EduGlob Visits NJUPT — Talks on Electronic Information Talent

ORIGIN EDUGLOB · 通讯稿 / PRESS RELEASE


2026 年 10 月 21 日，尚源（南京）教育科技有限公司总裁周迪、申请顾问周文蝶一行赴南京邮电大学海外教育学院拜访交流，与该院招生办老师贾亚超就来华留学生招生及相关合作事宜举行座谈。双方围绕生源对接、材料审核、入学服务等环节深入交换意见，为后续招生合作奠定基础。

On October 21, 2026, Jude, President of Origin EduGlob and application consultant Zhou Wendie visited the College of Overseas Education of Nanjing University of Posts and Telecommunications (NJUPT) and held talks with Naison of the college's admissions office on international student recruitment and related cooperation.

01 / 座谈座谈交流

THE TALKS

座谈中，双方就招生工作的关键环节逐项对接。尚源教育方面介绍了海外生源组织、申请材料交叉核验及行前辅导等工作的开展情况；贾亚超结合招生一线经验，就学校审核流程、报到注册安排及国际学生入学初期常见问题作了说明。双方就流程衔接中的具体事项进行了充分沟通，并明确了后续对接机制。

The two sides reviewed the key stages of the admissions process item by item. Origin EduGlob presented its work in overseas student sourcing, cross-verification of application documents and pre-departure guidance, while Mr. Jia shared first-hand experience on the university's review procedures, registration arrangements and issues commonly encountered by international students in their first weeks on campus.

02 / 校情南京邮电大学概况

ABOUT NJUPT

南京邮电大学系教育部、工业和信息化部、国家邮政局与江苏省共建高校，国家“双一流”建设高校，以电子信息为特色，被誉为“华夏 IT 英才的摇篮”。学校前身为 1942 年创办的战邮干训班，2005 年更名为南京邮电大学，现设仙林、三牌楼、锁金村三个校区，26 个教学机构，各类在籍生 3.6 万余人。学校 6 个学科进入 ESI 全球前 1%，其中计算机科学、工程学进入前 1‰。

NJUPT is co-built by the Ministry of Education, the Ministry of Industry and Information Technology, the State Post Bureau and Jiangsu Province, and is a national “Double First-Class” university distinguished by electronic information. Six of its disciplines rank among the global top 1% in ESI.

仙林校区 · 图书馆与湖景
Xianlin Campus — library and lake · Photo: NJUPT official site

在国际学生教育方面，学校招收本科、硕士、博士三个层次海外留学生，目前在校留学生 800 余人，生源覆盖 65 个国家，“一带一路”国家生源占比超过 90%。学校已通过来华留学生高等教育质量再认证，系中国政府奖学金生培养高校，并与哈萨克斯坦卡拉干达萨金诺夫技术大学共建海外卓越工程师学院。

The university enrolls international students at bachelor's, master's and doctoral levels, with more than 800 students from 65 countries, over 90 percent of them from Belt and Road nations. It has passed the quality re-certification for international student education and hosts Chinese Government Scholarship students.

03 / 机构尚源教育简介

ABOUT ORIGIN EDUGLOB

尚源（南京）教育科技有限公司成立于 2025 年，系中国教育国际交流协会会员单位，业务聚焦来华留学。公司以海外生源组织与高校对接为主业，已在 30 余个国家建立合作渠道，中亚地区形成直营与合资并行的布局。

Founded in Nanjing in 2025 and a member of the China Education Association for International Exchange, Origin EduGlob focuses on study-in-China services, with channels in more than 30 countries and operations across Central Asia.

公司网站近期完成全面更新，按服务对象分为机构端与个人端。机构端整合 CSCA（来华留学本科入学学业水平测试）题库、大学库与 EduMatch 3.0 智能匹配系统，供高校及合作机构开展生源筛选、材料核验与院校对接。

The company has recently relaunched its website with two portals. The institutional portal brings together a CSCA question bank, a university database and the EduMatch 3.0 matching system, supporting student screening, document verification and university matching for partner institutions.

个人端面向学生与家长，提供院校与专业检索、在线评估及申请进度查询。两端数据互通，机构与学生之间的对接更为直接。

The individual portal offers programme search, online assessment and application tracking for students and parents, with data shared across both ends.


双方表示，将以此次座谈为契机，在联合招生、海外定向推广、学生落地管理等方面分阶段推进合作，共同提升来华留学生招生质量与服务水平。

Both sides agreed to use the talks as a starting point for phased cooperation in joint recruitment, targeted overseas promotion and on-ground student services.
