China Launches First Two-Stage Foundation Alliance: HSK as Degree On-Ramp

By ORIGIN Education · 2026-11-17 · 原文：微信公众号

ENA new institutional door into Chinese universities opened on 15 November at the 2026 World Chinese Language Conference in Beijing. The HSK Study-in-China Foundation Alliance — the first China-foreign cooperation mechanism to run foundation education in a "home country + China" two-stage model — was formally established under the guidance of five national bodies, with 36 founding institutions and 4 supporting partners. Its core judgment is simple: Chinese proficiency (HSK) is no longer just a test score, but the connective tissue linking language learning, foundation training and a Chinese degree.

中文11 月 15 日，北京 2026 世界中文大会上，一扇通向中国高校的制度化新门开启。HSK 留学中国预科联盟正式成立——这是首个采用「国外+国内」两段式开展预科教育的中外合作机制，由五家国家级机构指导，36 家首批发起机构与 4 家支持单位共同组建。其核心判断很清晰：HSK 中文水平不再只是一张成绩单，而是贯通「中文学习—预科培养—留学中国」的人才成长通道。

ENThe structure is concrete. In the overseas stage, students study Chinese and China's national conditions near home; in the domestic stage, they continue for half a year at a partner Chinese university, taking Chinese plus mathematics, physics and chemistry. Those who meet the standard advance directly to undergraduate study. The alliance is guided by the Ministry of Education's International Cooperation and Exchange Department, the Center for Language Education and Cooperation, the China Scholarship Council, the Chinese Service Center for Scholarly Exchange and the Chinese International Education Foundation, and is operated by Hanban International (Hancao International) on a "government-guided, market-operated" basis. Fudan University leads curriculum development.

中文机制设计相当具体：国外阶段以中文和中国国情教育为主；国内阶段进入合作院校继续培养半年，学习「中文+数学、物理、化学」，达标后直接进入本科学习。联盟由教育部国际合作与交流司、教育部中外语言交流合作中心、国家留学基金管理委员会、教育部留学服务中心、中国国际中文教育基金会共同指导，由「HSK 留学中国与就业展」主办方汉考国际运营，遵循「政府指导、市场运作」原则，复旦大学参与课程研制。

ENThe scale behind it is hard to ignore. Overseas Chinese learners have reached 210 million; HSK candidates topped 1 million for the first time in 2026; HSK has set up more than 1,500 test centers in 169 countries, serving over 9 million candidates cumulatively — the widest country coverage of any language exam globally. A 2026 HSK Study-in-China Motivation Index survey of over 100,000 candidates in 27 countries found more than 70% want to study in China. The alliance converts that latent demand into a structured, credit-bearing pathway.

中文支撑这一机制的数字颇具分量：海外中文学习使用者已达 2.1 亿；2026 年 HSK 考生首次突破 100 万人次；HSK 在 169 国设立 1500 余个考点，累计服务考生超 900 万人次，考点覆盖国家数居全球语言考试首位。面向五大洲 27 国 10 万余名考生的最新调查显示，超 70% 的中文学习者有来华留学意愿。联盟正把这股潜在需求，转化为结构化、可衔接的升学通道。

ENThe most common pitfall: treating the alliance as a guaranteed-admission shortcut. Details that matter — the length of the overseas stage, fee structures, and the exact advancement standard — were not published at launch. The official reporting explicitly notes these are still open. Families who assume "join the alliance = get into a Chinese university" risk building a plan on unresolved terms; the domestic half-year and the "meet the standard" clause are real academic gates, not formalities.

中文最容易踩的坑：把联盟当成「保录取」捷径。真正关键的细节——国外阶段具体时长、费用结构、达标升学的准确标准——在成立时均未公布，官方报道明确指出这些仍有待披露。若把「加入联盟」直接等同于「升入中国高校」，等于在尚未明确的条款上搭建规划；国内半年培养与「达标」条款是实打实的学业门槛，而非走过场。
