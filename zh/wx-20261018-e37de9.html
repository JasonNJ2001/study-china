China's Motorcycle Breakthrough and the Universities Behind It

张雪机车首赛季登台：从 WSBK 亚军看中国工业制造专业怎么选

 

机车工程实验室主题底图

Congratulations: A Chinese Name on the World Grid

先说恭喜：世界领奖台上的中国名字

First, the celebration. On 18 October 2026, at the season finale of the World Superbike Championship (WSBK) in Spain, Chinese motorcycle manufacturer ZXMOTO — known in China as "Zhang Xue Motorcycle" — closed its debut WorldSSP campaign with French rider Valentin Debise taking the riders' championship second place overall. The team finished third in the manufacturers' standings and second in the teams' table. In March, Debise had already swept both races at the Portuguese round, giving a Chinese manufacturer its first-ever WorldSSP wins.

先说恭喜。2026 年 10 月 18 日，世界超级摩托车锦标赛（WSBK）西班牙站迎来赛季收官，中国摩托车制造商"张雪机车"结束了首个 WorldSSP 赛季：法国车手瓦伦丁·德比斯以年度车手亚军收官，车队获制造商年度第三名、车队积分榜年度第二名。今年 3 月的葡萄牙站，德比斯包揽两回合正赛冠军，创造了中国制造商在 WorldSSP 赛事中的首胜纪录。

Why this matters beyond sport is the machinery underneath. The race bike, the 820RR-RS, is a track-modified version of a mass-production three-cylinder model launched in 2026, with core components localized in China. It is the most visible example yet of a pattern that has been building for years: Chinese manufacturing moving from low-cost volume into high-performance engineering. For international students, that shift is exactly what makes manufacturing degrees in China worth a serious look.

比成绩更重要的是成绩背后的机器。参赛的 820RR-RS 赛车，是在 2026 年上市的三缸量产车基础上赛道化改装而来，核心部件已实现自主国产化。这是"中国制造从走量转向高端工程"这一趋势最直观的样本。对国际学生来说，正是这个转变，让来中国读制造类专业变得值得认真考虑。

What China's Industrial Base Looks Like Now

中国工业制造的现状：用数字说话

The scale is well documented. China's manufacturing value added accounts for nearly 30% of the global total and has ranked first worldwide for 16 consecutive years, according to a CCTV report of 18 September 2026. China is also the only country whose industrial system covers every category — 41 major, 207 medium and 666 sub-categories. In the mobility sector specifically, output of new energy vehicles, power batteries and key materials exceeds 70% of the world total.

规模本身已有权威注脚：据央视网 2026 年 9 月 18 日报道，中国制造业增加值占全球近三成，连续 16 年居世界第一；中国是全球唯一拥有全部工业门类的国家，覆盖 41 个工业大类、207 个中类、666 个小类。具体到出行产业，中国新能源汽车、动力电池及关键材料产量占全球比重超过 70%。

 

中国工业制造关键数字信息图

The motorcycle story fits this pattern precisely. Chinese customs and industry-association data show exports of motorcycles above 400cc reached USD 528 million in the first half of 2026, up 26.8% year on year — the fast-growing, high-margin segment. Chongqing, the country's motorcycle heartland, turned out 275,000 large-displacement leisure bikes from January to August 2026, 37.2% of national output. Add 80 national advanced manufacturing clusters, and you get an industry that is not just big but increasingly sophisticated — one that needs engineers trained in vehicle dynamics, electrification, lightweight materials and intelligent manufacturing.

摩托车正是这一逻辑的最新注脚。海关与行业商会数据显示，2026 年上半年中国 400cc 以上大排量摩托车出口额 5.28 亿美元，同比增长 26.8%——这是高附加值、高增长的细分市场。摩托车重镇重庆 2026 年 1–8 月大排量休闲摩托车产量 27.5 万辆，占全国 37.2%。再加上全国 80 个国家先进制造业集群，可以看到一个不仅规模庞大、而且技术复杂度持续上升的产业体系——它需要的是在车辆动力学、电动化、轻量化材料和智能制造方向受过训练的工程师。

What This Means for International Students

这对留学生意味着什么

It means the classroom and the factory are unusually close here. Vehicle engineering programmes in China sit inside the world's most complete vehicle supply chain, so internships, factory visits and thesis projects can involve the same firms that supply global brands. It also means the job market question answers itself: whether graduates return home or stay in China, EV and motorcycle makers across Asia, Africa and Latin America are actively hiring people who understand Chinese manufacturing standards and supply chains.

这意味着课堂与工厂的距离异常近。中国的车辆工程专业嵌在全球最完整的汽车供应链里，实习、参观和毕业课题可以直接对接为全球品牌供货的企业。就业问题也因此有了清晰答案：无论毕业后回国还是留华，亚洲、非洲和拉美的电动车与摩托车企业都在积极招聘既懂技术、又了解中国制造标准和供应链的人才。

Tuition and scholarships make the entry cost low. Public universities typically charge CNY 24,000–30,000 per year for engineering master's programmes taught in English or Chinese, and Chinese Government Scholarship (CSC) routes — Type A via embassies, Type B via universities — can cover tuition, accommodation and a monthly stipend. Compared with Europe or North America, the total cost of a manufacturing master's degree here is often a fraction of the price.

学费与奖学金让门槛变得很低。中国公立大学工程类硕士项目（英文或中文授课）学费通常为每年 24,000–30,000 元人民币；中国政府奖学金（CSC）——A 类经使领馆、B 类经高校——可以覆盖学费、住宿并提供生活费。与欧美相比，在中国完成一个制造类硕士学位的总成本往往只是前者的一小部分。

Which Universities Are Strong in This Field

哪些大学的工业制造专业比较好

Based on the 2026 ShanghaiRanking (Ruanke) subject tables, the first tier for Vehicle Engineering is Tsinghua University, Beijing Institute of Technology, Tongji University, Beihang University (BUAA) and Xi'an Jiaotong University, all rated A+. The second tier includes Jilin University, Harbin Institute of Technology, Zhejiang University, Hunan University and Southwest Jiaotong University, rated A.

根据软科 2026 中国大学专业排名，车辆工程第一梯队为清华大学、北京理工大学、同济大学、北京航空航天大学、西安交通大学，评级均为 A+；第二梯队包括吉林大学、哈尔滨工业大学、浙江大学、湖南大学、西南交通大学，评级为 A。

Jilin University is worth a specific note for international applicants: it is the birthplace of China's automotive engineering education, and its international-student brochure lists the Vehicle Engineering master's at the College of Automotive Engineering at CNY 30,000 per year (Chinese-taught). Jiangsu University offers an English-taught Vehicle Engineering master's at CNY 24,000 per year with research directions covering new energy vehicles and intelligent connected vehicles, requiring IELTS 6.0 or TOEFL 80. For applicants targeting racing-adjacent specialisations — engine calibration, chassis dynamics, lightweight frames — Chongqing's ecosystem around Changan and the motorcycle cluster is hard to replicate elsewhere.

对国际申请者，吉林大学值得单独说明：它是中国汽车工程教育的发源地，国际学生招生简章中汽车工程学院车辆工程硕士学费为每年 30,000 元（中文授课）。江苏大学提供英文授课车辆工程硕士，学费每年 24,000 元，研究方向覆盖新能源汽车与智能网联汽车技术，语言要求雅思 6.0 或托福 80。如果想做接近赛事的方向——发动机标定、底盘动力学、轻量化车架——重庆围绕长安汽车与摩托车产业集群形成的生态，在其他地方很难复制。

Your Action Plan

行动建议

  1. Decide your language track first. If your Chinese is below HSK 4 (180 points), shortlist English-taught programmes such as Jiangsu University's, or plan a preparatory year.
  2. Match the university to the specialisation: body and lightweighting (Hunan University), chassis (Jilin University), intelligent driving (Tongji, Tsinghua), transmission systems (Chongqing University).
  3. Apply through CSC Type B if your target university participates — enter its agency number at studyinchina.csc.edu.cn — or via university self-funded channels, where 2027 intakes are already open at several schools.
  4. Prepare the standard document set: degree certificate and transcripts, passport, study plan, two recommendation letters, physical examination form and police clearance, plus IELTS/TOEFL for English-taught routes.
  5. Use the industry: ask about internships with OEMs and suppliers in the university's city — Chongqing, Shanghai, Changchun and Wuhan each anchor a distinct cluster.
  1. 先定语言路线。如果中文未达 HSK 四级（180 分），优先考虑英文授课项目（如江苏大学），或规划一年预科。
  2. 按方向选学校：车身与轻量化看湖南大学，底盘看吉林大学，智能驾驶看同济和清华，机械传动看重庆大学。
  3. 目标院校若参与 CSC B 类，可在 studyinchina.csc.edu.cn 填写该校受理机构编号申请；自费通道方面，多所高校 2027 年入学申请已经开放。
  4. 备齐标准材料：学位证与成绩单、护照、学习计划、两封推荐信、体检表、无犯罪记录证明；英文授课另备雅思或托福。
  5. 用好产业资源：申请时直接询问学校所在城市的整车厂与供应商实习机会——重庆、上海、长春、武汉各自对应一个特色产业集群。

The Bottom Line

结语：把领奖台当路标

ZXMOTO's rookie season — riders' second place, manufacturers' third, two historic first wins — is a nice sports story. It is also a reading of where Chinese manufacturing now stands: good enough to compete, fast enough to improve, and open enough to need global talent. If you are choosing a manufacturing degree for 2027, the country that just took silver in world motorcycle racing is a reasonable place to earn it.

张雪机车的首个赛季——车手亚军、厂商第三、两项历史首胜——是一个漂亮的体育故事，也是中国制造业当前位置的一份成绩单：足够强、可以竞争、进步快，并且需要全球人才。如果你正在为 2027 年挑选制造类专业，这个刚在世界摩托车锦标赛登上年度领奖台的国家，是一个值得认真考虑的地方。
