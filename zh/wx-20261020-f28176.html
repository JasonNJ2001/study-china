2,397 Arrests in France: What It Means for 2027 Applicants

By ORIGIN Education · 2026-10-20 · 原文：微信公众号

2,397 Arrests in France: What It Means for 2027 Applicants

法国两天 2397 人被捕，2027 年的申请算式变了

On the weekend of 17 October, France had its worst street violence in years. Interior Ministry figures put the crowds at about 550,000 nationwide, 110,000 of them in Paris. By the ministry's count, 2,397 people were arrested and 997 police and gendarmes were injured, seven seriously.

10 月 17 日那个周末，法国发生了近年来最严重的街头暴力。法国内政部统计，全国约 55 万人上街，其中巴黎 11 万；2397 人被捕，997 名警察和宪兵受伤，7 人重伤。

By Monday the numbers had moved again. The justice minister reported 2,513 people taken into police custody and 67 already jailed, with cases reclassified as organised crime so detention could run to 72 hours instead of 48.

到周一，数字又变了。司法部长公布 2513 人被拘留、67 人已被收监，案件按「有组织犯罪」重新定性，拘留期从 48 小时延长到 72 小时。

10 月 17 日，巴黎共和国广场一带，警方使用催泪瓦斯驱散人群。摄影：Jorge Jaramillo / Wikimedia Commons / CC BY-SA 4.0

Curfews followed. From Monday night, Lille, Douai, Denain and Wattignies were under curfew from 20:00 to 06:00. Parts of Paris ran 23:00 to 06:00, with demonstrations banned from 20:00. Marseille banned all public gatherings from noon to 20:00. The Louvre closed for a day after a small group of student protesters blocked its entrance.

随后是宵禁。周一晚起，里尔、杜埃、德南、瓦蒂尼从 20 时到次日 6 时宵禁；巴黎部分街区 23 时至 6 时宵禁，20 时起禁止示威；马赛 12 时至 20 时禁止一切公众集会。卢浮宫因一小群学生抗议者堵住入口，闭馆一天。

At least 3,000 shops were damaged, with losses running into hundreds of millions of euros. Germany updated its travel advice for France. China's state broadcaster warned citizens travelling to France to take safety precautions. Organisers have called another round for this Saturday.

至少 3000 家商铺受损，损失达数亿欧元。德国更新了赴法旅行提示，中国央视提醒赴法中国公民注意安全。组织者已号召本周六再来一轮。

01What Started It

起因不是政治

The trigger was not an election or a war. In mid-September, students at a high school in Créteil, on the southern edge of Paris, walked out over unfilled teaching posts and classes with no substitute. The protest spread to other schools, then to universities, then to families, farmers and workers angry about the cost of living.

导火索既不是选举也不是战争。9 月中旬，巴黎南郊克雷泰伊一所高中因教师岗位空缺、无人代课而罢课。抗议蔓延到其他中学，再到大学，最后是家庭、农民和工人——不满的核心是生活成本。

The macro numbers explain why it caught so fast. Eurostat put euro-area energy prices 18.8 percent higher year on year in September, with headline inflation at 3.8 percent. Six months before a presidential election, that combination is combustible.

宏观数据解释了它为什么烧得这么快。欧盟统计局的数据是：9 月欧元区能源价格同比上涨 18.8%，整体通胀 3.8%。距离总统大选还有六个月，这个组合很容易点着。

同日下午，巴黎一条大道上的黑烟。摄影：Jorge Jaramillo / Wikimedia Commons / CC BY-SA 4.0

02Why This Is Not Really a Safety Story

为什么这不是治安故事

Almost no international student was anywhere near this. The violence was concentrated in city centres, over roughly two days, and was condemned across the political spectrum. Reading the weekend as proof that European universities are unsafe would be dishonest.

几乎没有留学生身处其中。暴力集中在市中心、持续约两天，各方政治人物都出面谴责。把这个周末读成「欧洲的大学不安全了」，是不诚实的。

What it does prove is something less dramatic and more expensive: that the systems a student depends on can stop on a Saturday and not restart on Monday.

它真正证明的事情没那么戏剧化，但更贵：一个学生所依赖的那些系统，可以在周六停摆，到周一还接不上。

A curfew closes the library at the hour you planned to use it. A transport shutdown cancels an exam booked a month earlier. A town hall that burned is a building where residence permits are not processed that week. When students protest, the disruption lands inside universities rather than outside them.

宵禁让图书馆在你打算去的钟点关门。交通停摆让一个月前订好的考试取消。被烧掉的市政厅，意味着那一周居留许可办不下来。学生上街时，中断是发生在大学里面的。

For a citizen, that is an inconvenience absorbed across a lifetime. For an international student on a twelve-month visa, with a registration deadline printed on an admission letter, a three-week processing gap can end a semester.

对本国人，这是一辈子里的一个小插曲。对一个持一年签证、入学函上印着注册截止日的留学生，三周的处理延误可以直接结束掉一个学期。

03The Three Numbers That Actually Change

真正会变的三笔账

If you are applying for 2027 entry, the question is not whether Europe is dangerous. It is which of your assumptions just moved. Three of them did.

如果你在申请 2027 年入学，问题不是欧洲安不安全，而是你的哪几个假设刚刚变了。有三个。

Cost. Energy at plus 18.8 percent passes through into rent, heating and campus services before it shows up in tuition. A euro that swings on political risk makes a three-year budget into a three-year bet.

第一是成本。能源涨 18.8%，会先体现在房租、取暖和校园服务上，然后才传导到学费。一个随政治风险波动的欧元，会把三年的预算变成三年的赌注。

Predictability. A presidential election in April and May, a budget under strain, and a parliament that has to respond to street pressure — all three point the same way, toward policy that can change faster than a degree programme can.

第二是可预测性。4 到 5 月的总统大选、吃紧的财政预算、必须回应街头压力的议会——这三件事指向同一个方向：政策变动的速度，可能快过一个学位项目的周期。

Campus continuity. This round started in classrooms and ended in curfews. For a taught master's, the classroom is the product. Lost teaching weeks are rarely refunded.

第三是校园的连续性。这一轮从教室开始，以宵禁收场。对授课型硕士来说，教室就是产品。丢掉的课时，通常不会退。

04What China's Side Actually Offers

中国这一侧给的是什么

Set against that, what a 2027 applicant gets from the China route is not a better story — it is a shorter list of variables.

对照之下，2027 年的申请者从中国这条路上拿到的，不是更好的故事，而是一份更短的变量清单。

The calendar is fixed. The CSC scholarship portal opens on 1 November and closes on a published date, year after year. You can plan backwards from it, which is exactly what you cannot do with a tuition policy that depends on who wins an election.

时间表是固定的。CSC 奖学金系统每年 11 月 1 日开网，截止日预先公布。你可以倒着排计划——而这恰恰是你对着一个取决于选举结果的学费政策时做不到的。

The cost structure is legible. Tuition and living costs for English-taught programmes are published per university and move slowly. There is no energy-index pass-through into your dormitory bill.

成本结构是可读的。英语授课项目的学费和生活费由各校公布，变动很慢。没有能源指数传导到你的宿舍账单上。

But the shorter list cuts both ways, and this is the part agencies leave out. Programme choice in English is narrower than in Germany or the Netherlands. Post-study work rights are more limited than in Canada. And a Chinese degree is not automatically recognised everywhere — you have to check the receiving country's list before you apply, not after.

但这份更短的清单是双向的，这也是中介不会讲的部分。英语授课的项目选择比德国、荷兰窄；毕业后留华工作的路径比加拿大受限；中国学历不是在所有国家都被自动认可——你必须在申请前查对方国家的认可名单，不是之后。

05The Comparison Worth Making

该做的比较

Stop comparing countries on safety. Compare them on three things you can actually measure.

别再按「安全」比国家了。按三个你真的能衡量的东西比。

Total cost over the full programme, including a currency buffer of at least ten percent. If a ten percent swing in the exchange rate breaks your budget, the budget was never real.

第一，整个项目的总成本，含至少 10% 的汇率缓冲。如果汇率动 10% 就撑不住，这个预算本来就不成立。

Policy stability across the length of your degree. Ask what happened to tuition and visa rules in that country over the last four years, not the last four months.

第二，你读书这几年里政策的稳定性。问那个国家过去四年学费和签证规则发生了什么，而不是过去四个月。

Exit cost. What happens if you have to leave in the middle. Deposit refund rules, credit transfer, and whether the second semester is refundable are all in the contract, not the brochure.

第三，退出成本。万一中途要走会怎样。定金退不退、学分能不能转、第二学期是否可退——这些都写在合同里，不在宣传册上。

06What to Do This Week

这一周能做的

If you were already leaning toward Europe for 2027, do not cancel anything because of one weekend. But do three things before you commit money.

如果你本来倾向 2027 去欧洲，不要因为一个周末就取消任何计划。但在付钱之前，做三件事。

First, convert your full budget into euros at today's rate, then again at ten percent worse. Second, write to the international office and ask, in writing, what happens to teaching weeks if there is a strike or a curfew — the answer tells you whether they have a plan. Third, keep one China application running in parallel. A parallel application costs you a few evenings. Not having one can cost you a year.

第一，把总预算按今天的汇率换成欧元，再按低 10% 算一遍。第二，书面问国际学生办公室：如果发生罢工或宵禁，教学周怎么补——他们的回答能告诉你有没有预案。第三，同时保留一份中国的申请。并行的申请只花你几个晚上；没有备份，可能花掉你一年。

07The Bottom Line

最后

Unrest is expensive not because of the risk of getting hurt, but because of the uncertainty it prices into every plan you make. The right response is not to pick the safest-looking country. It is to pick the plan whose assumptions you can check.

动荡之所以贵，不是因为受伤的风险，而是它把不确定性计入了你做的每一个计划。正确的反应不是挑一个看起来最安全的国家，而是挑一个假设可以被你验证的计划。

Two days of rioting in France did not change what a Chinese degree is worth. It changed how much you should charge for uncertainty when you compare it against one.

法国这两天的骚乱，没有改变一个中国学位的价值。它改变的是：当你要拿它去和另一个选择比较时，该为不确定性标多高的价。
