IELTS, PTE and Chinese: The Language Lab, Lahore

By ORIGIN Education · 2026-10-29 · 原文：微信公众号

2026 年 10 月 28 日 · 拉合尔 / 南京

雅思班与中文班，
在同一栋楼里开了近三十年

拉合尔 The Language Lab：从考试培训走到汉语课堂
IELTS, PTE and Chinese under one roof in Lahore

 

2026 年 10 月 28 日上午，尚源（南京）教育科技有限公司与巴基斯坦拉合尔的语言培训机构 The Language Lab 举行视频会议，中方代表在南京参会。这家机构 1996 年在拉合尔开办，以雅思与 PTE 备考课程为主业，同时开设中文与韩语班。后者的在读学员，正是会把中国高校放进选项的那批人。
On the morning of October 28, 2026, Origin EduGlob held a video meeting with The Language Lab, a language training institute in Lahore, Pakistan, the Chinese side joining from Nanjing. The institute opened in Lahore in 1996; its main business is IELTS and PTE preparation, and it also runs Chinese and Korean classes. The students in those classes are the ones who will put Chinese universities on their list.

对方是谁

WHO THEY ARE

官网首页的第一行写的是 “Established in 1996”。三十年下来，这家机构把自我定位写成“拉合尔最佳语言中心”，课表上并排五门课：雅思、PTE、英语口语、中文、韩语。官网还写明提供学生签证咨询——它本来就在把学生送出国，只是目的地此前不在中国。
The first line on the homepage reads “Established in 1996”. Thirty years on, the institute calls itself “the best language centre in Lahore”, with five courses on one timetable: IELTS, PTE, spoken English, Chinese and Korean. The site also lists student visa consultancy — it has been sending students abroad all along; China was simply not among the destinations.

②

课表上的两条线

FIVE COURSES, TWO LINES

课程分成两条线。一条是考试培训：雅思、PTE，加上面向日常使用的英语口语课；另一条是东亚语言：中文与韩语。两条线在同一栋楼里并行，招的学生却不完全重合——前者要的是分数，后者已经在想目的地。
The timetable runs along two lines. One is test preparation: IELTS, PTE and a spoken English course for everyday use. The other is East Asian languages: Chinese and Korean. Both run in the same building, but they serve different students — the first group needs a score, the second is already thinking about a destination.

雅思与 PTE 备考

IELTS & PTE PREPARATION

两门考试的主办方不同：雅思由英国文化协会与 IDP 主办，在英联邦体系内通用度高；PTE 属培生旗下，机考出分快，是否被某所中国高校接受要逐校核对招生简章。

The two tests have different owners: IELTS is run by the British Council and IDP and travels well across the Commonwealth system; PTE belongs to Pearson, is computer-based and returns results quickly, but whether a given Chinese university accepts it has to be checked in that university’s prospectus.

Everyday communication and interview settings, not tied to a specific exam.

中文

CHINESE LANGUAGE

从零起点往上，官网有中文学习者的访谈视频作为课程见证。

From beginner level upward, with learner interviews on the website as course testimonials.

韩语

KOREAN LANGUAGE

与中文班同属东亚语言方向，服务想去韩国读书或工作的人群。

Part of the same East Asian line, serving students heading to Korea to study or work.

对准备来华的学生，这个差别有实际影响。中国高校的英文授课项目普遍接受雅思与托福，PTE 的接受面窄一些，同一所学校的本科与硕士项目也可能不一样。把“考哪个”放到“申哪所”之后决定，比反过来稳妥。
For students heading to China, the difference is practical. English-taught programmes at Chinese universities generally accept IELTS and TOEFL; PTE is accepted more narrowly, and one university may treat its bachelor’s and master’s programmes differently. Deciding which test to sit after deciding where to apply is the safer order.

③

中文班的另一层意义

WHY THE CHINESE CLASS MATTERS

中文班不是兴趣班。官网的学员见证里有一支题为 “How I Learn Chinese Language at The Language Lab” 的访谈，学员在镜头前讲自己怎么学汉语、学到什么程度。能拿出这类素材，说明中文课在持续招生，也有走完全程的学员。官网另有博客板块接搜索流量，FAQ 与联系表单齐备，运营比较规整。
The Chinese class is not a hobby course. Among the testimonials on the website is an interview titled “How I Learn Chinese Language at The Language Lab”, in which a student describes how he studies Chinese and how far he has got. Material like that indicates the class recruits continuously and has students who stay the course. The site also runs a blog to catch search traffic, and carries an FAQ and an enquiry form — the operation is fairly disciplined.

官网中文课程页：零起点班从拼音与汉字同时起步
From the Chinese course page: a beginner class starting on pinyin and characters together

汉语班的出口比英语班更直接。HSK 等级是多数中国高校本科项目的硬门槛，中文授课的汉语言、中医与部分文科专业通常要 4 级到 5 级，没到线的学生可以先读一年预科。一个已经在教汉语的机构，离这条线只差院校选择与材料准备这一段。
The exit from a Chinese class is more direct than from an English one. HSK levels are a hard threshold for most bachelor’s programmes in China; Chinese-taught fields such as Chinese language, traditional Chinese medicine and some humanities subjects usually ask for level 4 to 5, and students below the line can take a one-year preparatory course first. An institute already teaching Chinese is one step from that route — what remains is choosing universities and preparing documents.

The other side opened with the course structure. The English transcript on screen records IELTS and PTE described as the institute’s core products, with China and Korea named among the destinations students ask about; the institute works with a number of partners to carry language training through to departure.

⑤

落到学生身上

THE ROUTE FOR A STUDENT

—英语成绩怎么用：英文授课项目普遍认雅思与托福，本科常见区间在 6.0 到 6.5，硕士偏高；PTE 要看具体院校，逐校核对招生简章最稳。
English scores: English-taught programmes generally take IELTS or TOEFL — 6.0 to 6.5 is common at bachelor’s level and higher at master’s. PTE depends on the university; checking each prospectus is the reliable route.

—汉语成绩怎么用：HSK 4 级是多数本科的起点，中文授课专业要 5 级；HSK 在中国境外设有考点，拉合尔本地可考；未达标可先读一年预科再进专业。
Chinese scores: HSK 4 is the usual starting point for bachelor’s programmes and 5 for Chinese-taught fields. HSK has test centres outside China, including in Lahore; students below the threshold can take a preparatory year before entering the major.

—时间与材料：9 月是主季，多数院校截止在 3 月到 5 月，奖学金项目更早；从 2026/2027 学年起，中国政府奖学金院校的本科申请还要加一项 CSCA 测试；材料是成绩单、语言成绩、推荐信与学习计划这几项。
Timing and documents: September is the main intake, with most deadlines between March and May and scholarships earlier. From 2026/27, bachelor’s applicants to Chinese Government Scholarship universities also sit the CSCA test. The file is transcripts, language scores, recommendation letters and a study plan.

这三条里，前两条恰好是语言机构已经在做的事，第三条才是需要外部承接的部分。
Of the three, the first two are what a language institute already does; the third is what needs an outside partner.

会谈没有停在互相介绍上。双方交换的是各自手里最具体的东西：一边是拉合尔的课表与在读学生，一边是中国高校的院校库与申请节点。一个学生在拉合尔考完雅思之后，才会决定去哪个国家；这个决定之前的窗口，是双方都认为可以谈的部分。
The meeting did not stop at introductions. What the two sides put on the table was the most concrete thing each holds: on one side, a timetable and a body of enrolled students in Lahore; on the other, a database of Chinese universities and a calendar of deadlines. A student in Lahore sits IELTS first and settles on a country afterwards. The window before that decision is the part both sides agreed is worth talking about.
