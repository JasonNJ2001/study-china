Partner Spotlight: Manchester Language Centre (Malaysia)

By ORIGIN Education · 2026-11-05 · 原文：微信公众号

PARTNER SPOTLIGHT · 合作伙伴专栏

语言为桥，山海同途｜走近马来西亚曼彻斯特语言中心

尚源教育全球合作伙伴系列 · Manchester Language Centre, Malaysia

— 尚源教育与马来西亚曼彻斯特语言中心（MLC Learning）云端会谈 · Online meeting with Manchester Language Centre, Malaysia

近日，尚源（南京）教育科技有限公司（OriginEduGlob）与马来西亚曼彻斯特语言中心（Manchester Language Centre，简称 MLC Learning）举行线上会谈。两家机构围绕国际生入学前的语言准备与来华留学通道建设展开深入交流，共同探寻一条「从语言学习到走进中国校园」的衔接之路。

Recently, OriginEduGlob held an online meeting with Manchester Language Centre (MLC Learning) of Malaysia. The two institutions held in-depth exchanges on pre-enrollment language preparation for international students and the building of Study-in-China pathways, jointly exploring a connecting road "from language learning to stepping onto Chinese campuses".

01一场跨越山海的对话

屏幕两端，尚源团队与 MLC Learning 的伙伴隔空相对。屏幕上展示的，是尚源专为海外教育机构设计的「来华留学合作伙伴计划」——覆盖选校、申请、签证到落地的全链路支持。

Across the screen, the OriginEduGlob team faced partners from MLC Learning. What was displayed is OriginEduGlob's Study-in-China Partner Program designed for overseas education institutions — covering the full chain from school selection, application, visa to arrival.

— 双方围绕合作细节展开讨论 · Discussing partnership details

每一次对话，都始于「你们的学生最需要什么」。对 MLC Learning 而言，帮学员迈过语言这道入学前的门槛，正是他们最擅长的，也是学员最关心的问题。

Every conversation starts with "what do your students need most". For MLC Learning, helping learners clear the language barrier before enrollment is exactly what they do best, and what students care about most.

— 与海外伙伴的视频连线 · Video call with overseas partners

当语言培训遇上留学规划，合作的空间便自然打开——这正是尚源与全球语言机构携手的意义。

When language training meets study planning, the space for cooperation opens naturally — this is exactly why OriginEduGlob joins hands with language institutions worldwide.

02走进我们的合作伙伴：曼彻斯特语言中心

曼彻斯特语言中心（Manchester Language Centre，简称 MLC Learning）是一家来自马来西亚的语言教育机构，秉持「We change your outlook on life」的理念，专注于国际生入学前的语言准备——无论目标是雅思、托福，还是未来攻读中国高校所需的 HSK，语言关都是踏进大学校门的第一道门槛。

Manchester Language Centre (MLC Learning) is a language education institution from Malaysia, guided by the motto "We change your outlook on life". It focuses on the language preparation international students need before enrollment — whether the goal is IELTS, TOEFL, or the HSK required for studying at Chinese universities, the language barrier is the first gate to a university campus.

— MLC Learning 官网「Why Choose Manchester?」· Why Choose Manchester? (mlclearning.com)

在 MLC Learning，学员能享受到五大优势：母语外教一对一视频授课、看得见成效的保证、小班教学且课程全程录制可回看、结课颁发课程证书，以及随时随地的在线学习。扎实的听说读写训练，正是雅思、托福等国际语言考试最需要的底子。

At MLC Learning, learners enjoy five strengths: native-teacher one-on-one video lessons, guaranteed visible results, small-group classes recorded for review, a course-completion certificate, and online learning anytime. Solid training in listening, speaking, reading and writing is exactly the foundation IELTS and TOEFL demand.

— MLC Learning 课程体系「The Most Effective English Courses」· English course catalogue

其课程体系以「最有效的英语课程」为核心，涵盖全日制英语、口语交际、少儿英语、商务英语，以及主打个性化的 1 对 1 精品课程。从少儿到职场人士，学员都能在这里把语言基础打牢，为下一步升学做好准备。

Its curriculum centres on "The Most Effective English Courses", covering Full-Time, Conversation, Kid's, Business English and the personalized 1-on-1 Prime Course. From children to professionals, learners build a solid language foundation here, ready for the next step of further study.

而当我们把目光投向中国，HSK（汉语水平考试）又成了另一道门槛。语言培训与留学规划天然相连——这正是尚源与 MLC 等全球语言机构携手的意义：让学员先过语言关，再稳稳走进理想的大学。

And when we look toward China, HSK (the Chinese Proficiency Test) becomes another gate. Language training and study planning are naturally connected — this is exactly why OriginEduGlob joins hands with language institutions like MLC: let learners clear the language barrier first, then step steadily into their ideal university.

03关于尚源教育与合作伙伴计划

尚源（南京）教育科技有限公司 2022 年正式成立于中国南京，是中国教育国际交流协会（CEAIE）会员单位、江苏省国际教育服务五星机构，并于 2026 年 10 月 26 日完成出入境中介服务机构备案。公司专注「来华留学」全流程「管家式」服务，以自研 AI 招生生态系统 EduMatch 3.0 精准匹配院校需求。

Shangyuan (Nanjing) Education Technology Co., Ltd. was officially established in Nanjing in 2022. It is a member of CEAIE, a Five-Star International Education Service Institution in Jiangsu, and completed its recordation as an exit-entry intermediary on 26 October 2026. The company focuses on end-to-end concierge Study-in-China services and uses its proprietary EduMatch 3.0 AI recruitment ecosystem to match university needs precisely.

需要提醒每一位准留学生的是——中国的大学分为两大类：英语授课项目与中文授课项目。英语授课通常要求 IELTS 或 TOEFL 成绩，中文授课则要求相应的 HSK 等级。尚源会根据学生的语言基础、专业方向与预算，帮助学生选择最合适的院校与授课语言，少走弯路。

One reminder for every prospective international student: Chinese universities fall into two broad categories — English-taught programs and Chinese-taught programs. English-taught programs usually require IELTS or TOEFL, while Chinese-taught programs require a corresponding HSK level. OriginEduGlob helps students choose the most suitable university and medium of instruction based on their language foundation, major and budget, avoiding detours.
