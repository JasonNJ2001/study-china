MBBS in China Enters a New Era of Standardized Admission: CSCA as the Gateway, Global Practice as the Goal

By ORIGIN Education · 2026-10-25 · 原文：微信公众号

ENChina's English-taught Bachelor of Medicine and Bachelor of Surgery (MBBS) programs — a flagship channel that has drawn hundreds of thousands of international students from Belt and Road and Global South countries — are shifting decisively from rapid scale-up to quality-first, standardized admission. The change is not rhetorical: concrete gatekeeping mechanisms are now written into the admissions rules of the country's leading medical schools.

中文中国高校开设的英语授课临床医学本科（MBBS）项目，历来是"一带一路"及广大发展中国家学生来华留学的旗舰通道。如今，这一通道正明确地从"规模扩张"转向"质量优先、准入规范"。这并非口号：在头部医学院的招生章程里，具体的准入把关机制已经被写入规则。

ENThe most visible shift is the arrival of the China Scholastic Competency Assessment (CSCA) as a mandatory gate. Fudan University's 2026 MBBS prospectus lists CSCA as "Step 1" of admission, with Mathematics, Physics and Chemistry (conducted in English) as compulsory subjects. Shanghai Jiao Tong University School of Medicine's 2026 MBBS admissions notice requires a CSCA score report covering the same three sciences. What was once an optional maturity signal is now a hard prerequisite for clinical study.

中文最直观的变化，是来华留学本科入学学业水平测试（CSCA）成为硬性门槛。复旦大学 2026 年 MBBS 招生简章将 CSCA 列为录取"第一步"，且明确数学、物理、化学（英文卷）为必考科目；上海交通大学医学院 2026 年 MBBS 招生通知同样要求提供 CSCA 数/理/化成绩单。曾经的可选参考，如今成了攻读临床专业的硬前置条件。

ENQuality control is also enforced at the institutional level. The Ministry of Education publishes an annual list of universities authorized to award English-taught MBBS degrees — 43 to 45 institutions in the current cycle — and strictly prohibits any "bilingual teaching" model for these programs. The intent is unambiguous: only graduates of the official list enjoy reliable degree recognition and a clean path to medical licensing at home and abroad, protecting both students and the "Study in China" brand.

中文质量把控同样发生在院校层面。教育部每年公布具备英语授课 MBBS 招生资质的高校名单——本招生周期为 43 至 45 所，并严禁此类项目采用"双语教学"模式。用意十分明确：只有名单内院校的毕业生，才能获得可靠的学位互认，并顺畅对接国内外的医师执业资格；这既保护学生，也守护"留学中国"品牌。

ENThe front door has been tightened as well. Under the Ministry's 2020 notice (Jiaowai Han [2020] No. 12) on standardizing the admission of international students, nationality and prior-residence requirements are enforced rigorously, closing loopholes that once allowed "fake international students" to crowd out genuine applicants. Combined with CSCA, the result is a fairer, more merit-based pipeline.

中文准入的"前门"也在收紧。依据教育部《关于规范我高等学校接受国际学生有关问题的通知》（教外函〔2020〕12 号），国籍与既往居留要求被严格核验，堵住了曾让"假留学生"挤占名额的漏洞。叠加 CSCA，整条通道变得更加公平、更以学业能力为导向。

ENCrucially, the standardization points outward, not just inward. MOE-approved MBBS degrees are recorded in the World Directory of Medical Schools (WDOMS) and aligned with global licensing pathways such as the USMLE, PLAB and AMC. Graduates are trained to return home or practice worldwide — turning a Chinese degree into a portable, internationally recognized credential.

中文关键的是，这种规范化不仅向内，也向外敞开。教育部认可的 MBBS 学位录入世界医学院校名录（WDOMS），并与 USMLE、PLAB、AMC 等全球执业考试路径对接。毕业生既学成归国、也能走向世界——让一张中国文凭成为可携带、获国际认可的资质。

ENDemand remains strong and regional bridges are deepening. Zhejiang University's Belt and Road International School of Medicine enrolled a record 180 MBBS students in its 2026 intake, pushing its cumulative international medical cohort past 500 from 63 countries. Kunming Medical University had trained over 2,700 international students from 61 countries by 2025, while Southwest Medical University counted 1,102 undergraduates from 20 countries. Medicine is now one of the densest, most strategically significant slices of "Study in China."

中文需求依旧旺盛，区域纽带持续加深。浙江大学"一带一路"国际医学院 2026 年 MBBS 录取 180 人、再创新高，累计外籍医学生已超过 500 人、覆盖 63 国；昆明医科大学截至 2025 年已培养来自 61 国的 2,700 余名留学生；西南医科大学则累计招收 20 国 1,102 名本科生。医学，已成为"留学中国"中密度最高、战略意义最突出的板块之一。
