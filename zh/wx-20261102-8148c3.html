One False Document Can Now Bar a Student for Five Years

By ORIGIN Education · 2026-11-02 · 原文：微信公众号

One False Document Can Now Bar a Student for Five Years

一份假材料，现在可能让学生五年进不来

 

THE LEAD

导语

China's new exit-entry regulation is no longer a draft on a lawyer's desk. State Council Decree No. 841 — the State Council Provisions on the Administration of Exit and Entry — was promulgated on 22 July 2026 and took effect on 15 September 2026. It rewrites, in enforceable detail, what happens to applicants who submit false documents, and it puts every agency that helps with an application under a filing regime with a hard deadline: 14 December 2026, which is 42 days from today.

中国的出境入境管理新规已经落地，不再是文件草案。国务院令第841号《国务院关于出境入境管理的规定》于2026年7月22日公布，2026年9月15日起施行。它用可执行的条款改写了"材料造假"的后果，也给每一家帮人办手续的中介套上了备案制度——硬截止日是2026年12月14日，距今还有42天。

For international students and their parents, the decree touches three moments of the application journey: the visa interview abroad, the paperwork an agent prepares, and the invitation letters or certificates issued on the student's behalf. Each now carries consequences that are specific, numbered and personal.

对留学生和家长来说，这份规定切中申请路上的三个环节：在境外的签证环节、中介帮你准备的每份文件、以及以学生名义出具的各种邀请函和证明。每个环节现在的后果都具体、有数字、且落到个人头上。

WHAT CHANGED ON 15 SEPTEMBER

9月15日起改了什么

Article 3 sets the baseline: applications for entry, exit and residence must rest on true and lawful grounds. The person or organisation that issues an invitation letter or supporting document is now responsible for its truth, and must cooperate when immigration authorities verify it. Article 5 is the clause students should memorise: a foreigner who provides false materials or false statements when applying for a Chinese visa abroad, or when seeking entry at a port, can be barred from entering China for one to five years.

第三条划了底线：申请出入境、停留居留，事由必须真实、合法。出具邀请函件或其他申请材料的单位和个人，要为内容的真实性负责，并配合移民管理机构核实。第五条是学生该背下来的一条：外国人在境外申请办理中国签证、或在口岸申请入境时提供虚假材料、作出虚假陈述的，可以被决定1年至5年内不准入境。

Article 11 attaches numbers to the paper trail. An individual who issues a false invitation letter or supporting document for someone else faces a fine of 5,000 to 10,000 yuan; an organisation doing the same faces 10,000 to 50,000 yuan, plus confiscation of any illegal gains. The days of casually "arranging" an invitation letter are over.

第十一条给纸面材料标上了价格。个人为他人出具虚假邀请函件或其他申请材料的，罚款5000元至1万元；单位这么做的，罚款1万元至5万元，有违法所得的一律没收。随手"安排"一份邀请函的时代结束了。

YOUR AGENT EITHER HAS A FILING NUMBER, OR IS IN BREACH

你的中介要么有备案编号，要么在违规

The middle section of the decree aims squarely at the intermediary industry. Under Article 7, any organisation or person providing exit-entry intermediary services — policy consulting, document handling, application processing — must file with the local immigration administration within 15 days of being established. Agencies already operating when the rule took effect had 90 days from 15 September, which lands on 14 December 2026. From that date, an unfiled agency is not merely unprofessional; it is in breach.

规定的中段直指中介行业。按第七条，从事出境入境中介服务（政策咨询、证件代办、手续办理）的机构和人员，应当自设立之日起15日内向所在地移民管理机构备案；规定施行前已在营业的存量机构，须在施行之日起90日内完成备案——即2026年12月14日。从那天起，没备案的中介不只是"不正规"，而是违规。

Article 10 lists what a compliant agency may never do: publish false information, recruit clients through exaggerated or misleading claims, provide or help provide false documents, exceed the scope of its filing, or organise cross-border violations. Articles 12 and 13 attach fines from 5,000 up to 50,000 yuan, business suspension and licence revocation for serious offences. Article 9 then requires the immigration authorities to work with education, market-regulation and other departments to supervise the industry — and to publicise agencies' violations and bad records. For a student, that record list is about to become the cheapest background check available.

第十条列出了合规中介的禁区：不得发布虚假信息、不得以夸大或误导性宣传招徕客户、不得提供或协助提供虚假材料、不得超出备案范围经营、不得组织跨境违法犯罪活动。第十二、十三条配上了从5000元到5万元的罚款、责令暂停业务乃至吊销执照的阶梯式罚则。第九条则要求移民管理机构会同教育、市场监督管理等部门建立监管制度，依法公布中介的违法信息和不良记录。对学生来说，这份名单很快会成为成本最低的背景调查工具。

One more clause closes the loophole students meet most often. Article 12 provides that an individual who runs exit-entry intermediary services in violation of the rules can be ordered to stop, stripped of illegal gains, and fined up to 5,000 yuan in serious cases. The "helpful senior" or the chat-group agent who promises to handle everything for a fee is, from now on, operating outside the law — with none of the accountability a filed agency carries.

还有一条堵上了学生最容易撞上的漏洞。第十二条规定，个人违反本规定从事出境入境中介服务的，由移民管理机构责令停止违法行为，有违法所得的没收违法所得，情节严重的可以并处5000元以下罚款。那位"热心的学长"、微信群里收费包办的"老师"，从今往后都是在法外经营——他们不承担任何备案机构要承担的责任。

WHAT IT MEANS FOR A 2027 APPLICATION

对2027年的申请意味着什么

First, the honesty bar has moved. "Packaging" a document — a retouched bank statement, an embellished work certificate, an invitation letter that describes nothing real — is no longer a risk that ends with a refusal stamp. Under Article 5 the same act can now close the door for one to five years, which spans an entire degree programme. A refusal costs a semester; a five-year ban costs you China.

第一，诚实的门槛提高了。"包装"材料——修图的银行流水、夸大的工作证明、内容与事实不符的邀请函——不再只是"被拒签了事"。同样的行为，现在可能换来1到5年不准入境，覆盖整个学位项目。拒签损失一个学期；禁入损失的是整个中国。

Second, responsibility now runs uphill. If a relative in China, a host school or an employer issues an invitation letter for you, that issuer answers for its truth, with the fines of Article 11. Students should stop treating invitation letters as favours to be obtained, and start treating them as documents to be verified.

第二，责任会向上追溯。如果国内亲友、接待学校或单位为你出具了邀请函，出具方要对真实性负责，并面临第十一条的罚款。不要把邀请函当成"托人搞到的面子"，要当成需要逐字核实的文件。

Third, the agent market is about to thin. From 14 December, compliant agencies hold a filing and the rest operate illegally. By early 2027, when the 2027/2028 Chinese Government Scholarship applications reach their peak, the check takes one question: "What is your filing number, and with which immigration administration?"

第三，中介市场会变薄。12月14日之后，合规中介持有备案，其余的都在违规经营。到2027年初、2027/2028学年奖学金申请进入高峰时，核查只需一个问题："你们的备案编号是多少？在哪个移民管理机构备的案？"

 

WHAT TO DO BEFORE 14 DECEMBER

12月14日之前要做的事

  • If you already use an agency, ask for its filing certificate in writing before paying any further instalment. After 14 December, an unfiled agency is a red flag, not a discount.
  • 如果你已经在用中介，在支付下一笔款项前，要求对方书面出示备案凭证。12月14日之后，未备案的中介是红灯，不是便宜。
  • Read every document before it is submitted on your behalf. You — not the agent — carry the Article 5 consequences.
  • 任何以你名义提交的文件，提交前逐字读过。承担第五条后果的人是你，不是中介。
  • Treat every invitation letter as a fact sheet: names, dates, places and purpose must match the itinerary exactly. If a passage is generic rather than factual, ask for a rewrite.
  • 把每份邀请函当成事实清单：姓名、日期、地点、事由必须与你的行程完全一致。凡是写得空泛而不具体的段落，都要求重写。
  • Keep originals of everything you sign, and photograph every form before upload. If an agency objects to transparency, that itself is the answer.
  • 签署的所有文件保留原件，上传前逐份拍照存档。中介若反对你留底，这本身就是答案。
  • If you are unsure whether an offer letter, an invitation or an "internal channel" promise is legitimate, have it checked against the university's official admissions channel before anything is filed. Originedu's advisors run such verifications for applicants every intake.
  • 如果你拿不准一份录取函、邀请函或"内部渠道"承诺是否靠谱，先对照学校官方招生渠道核验，再决定是否提交。Originedu的顾问每个招生季都在为申请者做这类核验。

THE BOTTOM LINE

结语

Decree No. 841 is not aimed at students; it is aimed at the shortcuts around them. But students sit inside the blast radius, because the false document in an application usually carries the student's own name. The regulation gives every legitimate actor a paper trail — a filed agency, a verifiable invitation, a truthful form — and gives every false one a date of reckoning that starts at one year and reaches five. The 14 December filing deadline is the easiest checkpoint in the whole decree: one question, asked in time. Always confirm current requirements with the National Immigration Administration or the issuing embassy before you apply.

841号令针对的不是学生，而是学生周围的捷径。但学生处在波及范围内，因为申请里的那份假材料，通常挂的是学生自己的名字。新规给每一个走正路的人留下了凭据——备案的中介、可核实的邀请函、真实填写的表格——也给每一份假材料标上了清算日期，从1年起、到5年止。12月14日的备案截止日，是整部规定里最容易把握的一道检查点：一句问话，及时问出口。申请前，请以国家移民管理局或出具签证的使馆的最新要求为准。
