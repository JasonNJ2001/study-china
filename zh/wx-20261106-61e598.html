Partner Spotlight: Global Education Basket, Qatar

By ORIGIN Education · 2026-11-06 · 原文：微信公众号

PARTNER SPOTLIGHT · 伙伴特写

海湾来风，教育相连｜走近卡塔尔 Global Education Basket

尚源教育国际伙伴档案 · Global Education Basket, Qatar

— 尚源教育与卡塔尔 Global Education Basket 线上会谈 · Online meeting with Global Education Basket, Qatar

2026年11月6日，尚源（南京）教育科技有限公司（OriginEduGlob）与卡塔尔多哈的 Global Education Basket（GEB）团队，通过一场线上深谈，围绕如何助力更多中东青年，将「来华求学」从心中的一个念想，变为背起行囊便可启程的现实，展开了深入交流。

Recently, OriginEduGlob sat down online with the team from Global Education Basket (GEB) in Doha, Qatar. The focus of the conversation was how to help more young people in the Middle East turn "study in China" from a lingering thought into a tangible plan they can act on.

01屏幕两端，共话留学这件小事


尚源团队与 GEB 的伙伴分处两个时区。我们向对方展示的，是一份覆盖海外合作全流程的协作方案——选校、申请、签证、落地，环环相扣、有人承接，而非将学生送至机场便草率收尾。

GEB 始终聚焦的，是学生求学路上真正的难点所在。于他们而言，将语言基础与升学目标有机融合，既是其核心优势，也是中东家庭最为恳切的期盼。

Across time zones, the OriginEduGlob team and GEB partners faced the same screen. What we presented was a collaboration framework covering the full overseas journey — school selection, application, visa, arrival, each stage connected and staffed, rather than ending at the airport door.

What GEB keeps returning to is where students truly struggle. For them, weaving language readiness together with study goals is both their core strength and the deepest hope of Middle Eastern families.

— 会谈中：尚源向 GEB 伙伴展示合作框架与院校资源 · Presenting our cooperation framework during the call

02认识一下：Global Education Basket


Global Education Basket（简称 GEB）2013 年创立于卡塔尔多哈，是一家面向中东北非（MENA）青年的升学咨询机构。两位创始人 Jessica Naimé 与 Mylène El Fakhry，将其从初创工作室，发展为业务遍及海湾多国的教育顾问机构。

Global Education Basket (GEB) was founded in Doha, Qatar in 2013 as an academic-advisory firm for youth across the Middle East and North Africa (MENA). Its founders, Jessica Naimé and Mylène El Fakhry, grew it from a startup studio into an education-consulting institution operating across the Gulf.

它的服务非常聚焦：学业规划、本硕博及海外升学咨询、IELTS 与 ACT 备考训练，以及翻译与研究协助。作为多所院校在中东地区的官方代表，GEB 的收益源自院校支付的佣金，而非向学生层层加价——这一点，恰与尚源「透明、以学生为中心」的理念不谋而合。

Its services are sharply focused: academic orientation, bachelor's/master's/PhD and study-abroad advisory, IELTS and ACT preparation, plus translation and research support. As the official representative of several institutions across the Middle East, GEB is paid a commission by universities rather than marking up fees to students — a stance that resonates with OriginEduGlob's own transparent, student-centered ethos.

03为什么携手：尚源的相遇之道


尚源（南京）教育科技有限公司，是中国教育国际交流协会（CEAIE）会员。我们每年主动走进国内外的教育交流展、国际教育论坛与项目洽谈会，把一方展台，当作与世界对话的窗口。

As a member of CEAIE (China Education Association for International Exchange), OriginEduGlob (Nanjing) takes an active part each year in education fairs, international forums and project matchmaking events at home and abroad, treating each booth as a window onto the wider world.

对尚源而言，每一次展会都是一份机缘——展台前一次认真的深谈，往往就是一段跨国合作的起点。正是这样一场场走出来的相遇，让伙伴网络从南京延伸到了 30 多个国家、142 所合作院校。我们珍惜每一份机缘，也把每一次相遇，交付成对海外学生踏实的托举。

To us, every exhibition is a chance encounter — a serious talk at the booth can become the start of a cross-border partnership. It is through these face-to-face meetings that our partner network has grown from Nanjing to over 30 countries and 142 institutions. We treasure every chance, and turn each encounter into steady support for students overseas.

04面向中东，把路走得更宽


教育最珍贵的，是让相隔万里的人，在求知这件事上成为彼此的同行者。与 GEB 这样的中东桥梁并肩，我们愿让更多阿拉伯青年先迈过语言关，再从容地走进中国校园。

The most precious part of education is letting people ten thousand miles apart become each other's fellow travelers in the pursuit of knowledge. Standing with Middle East bridges like GEB, we hope to help more Arab youth clear the language gate first, then walk calmly onto Chinese campuses.

每一次相遇都值得珍惜，每一条通道都值得用心修。下一站，我们希望与更多像 GEB 一样的伙伴携手，把连接中国与中东的教育之路，铺得更顺畅、更紧密。

Every encounter deserves to be cherished, and every corridor deserves care. The next stop is to join hands with more partners like GEB, and make the education pathway between China and the Middle East smoother and closer.
