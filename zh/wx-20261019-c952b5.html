Today's China-Study Pick · Fudan University (FDU)

今日推荐大学 · 复旦大学

EN: One of China's most prestigious comprehensive universities (founded 1905), in the heart of Shanghai, blending world-class academics with a famously international, welcoming campus.
中文：中国最负盛名的综合性大学之一（始创于1905年），坐落上海腹地，世界顶尖学术与国际化、包容的校园氛围在此交汇。   

🎓 Today's Recommended University · 今日推荐大学

Fudan University (复旦大学), founded in 1905 as Fudan Public School, is a comprehensive research university directly under China's Ministry of Education and a member of the C9 League and the "Double First-Class" initiative. It is located in Shanghai, with its main Handan campus in the Yangpu District. In the 2026 QS World University Rankings it places #30 globally (rankings vary by source and year — this figure is from Fudan's own 2026 admission guide).

复旦大学始创于1905年（复旦公学），是直属教育部的综合性研究型大学，亦为C9联盟与"双一流"建设高校，坐落于上海，主校区邯郸校区位于杨浦区。在2026年QS世界大学排名中位列全球第30位（不同榜单与年份略有差异，该数据出自复旦2026年招生指南）。

✨ One-Line Highlight · 学校一句话亮点

A 120-year-old flagship of Chinese higher education in Shanghai — top-tier across humanities, social sciences, medicine and science, with a campus that has welcomed international students for over 70 years.

上海一所有着120年历史的顶尖学府——人文、社科、医学、理学全面发展，更拥有超过70年接收国际学生的传统。

🙋 Who It's For · 适合哪些留学生

Students aiming for strong degrees in medicine, economics, journalism, international relations, management, or Chinese language & literature; those who want a deeply international, English-friendly yet Chinese-rich environment; and anyone who dreams of studying in China's most global city. Beginners of Chinese are also welcome in the university's language programs.

目标在医学、经济、新闻、国际关系、管理或汉语言文学等领域深造的学生；希望置身于高度国际化、既通英语又浸染汉语环境的学子；以及向往在中国最具国际化都市求学的人。汉语零基础者也可报读其语言项目。

🏆 Strength Programs · 优势专业 / 项目

Fudan is particularly strong in medicine (Shanghai Medical College), economics, journalism, international relations, management, and Chinese language & literature. It spans 12 discipline categories, with 20 disciplines in China's second-round "Double First-Class" list and 21 disciplines in the global ESI top 1% (10 in the top 1‰).

Its International Cultural Exchange School (ICES) has run Chinese-language programs for foreign students since 1952 and, in 1993, launched China's first bachelor's program specially designed for international students — the 汉语言(对外) major, with language-culture and business-Chinese tracks. The school is fondly called a "little United Nations," hosting 1,000+ international students from 100+ countries every year.

复旦在医学（上海医学院）、经济学、新闻学、国际关系、管理学及汉语言文学等领域实力突出。学校覆盖12个学科门类，20个学科入选中国第二轮"双一流"建设名单，21个学科进入ESI全球前1%（其中10个进入前1‰）。

其国际文化交流学院自1952年起招收留学生，并于1993年开设全国首个专门面向外国留学生的本科专业——汉语言（对外），设有语言文化、商务汉语等方向。学院素有"小小联合国"之称，每年接收来自100多个国家的千余名国际学生。

🗣️ Language & Admission · 语言与入学要求

Requirements depend on the program track (figures below are common baselines — always confirm with the official guide / an advisor):

  • Chinese-taught undergraduate:
     HSK 5 (210+) or HSK 6 (180+). Fudan's 2026 undergraduate checklist lists the CSCA score report as a required document and marks HSK as "not mandatory" in that particular list — but for Chinese-taught study you should still build a strong HSK profile; confirm the exact requirement for your major.
  • Chinese-taught postgraduate:
     HSK 5 (humanities / social sciences 240+; sciences / engineering / medicine 210+) or HSK 6 (180+).
  • English-taught programs:
     no HSK; typically IELTS 6.0 / TOEFL 80 (undergraduate) or IELTS 6.5 / TOEFL 90 (postgraduate), with native-English speakers often exempt.
  • Chinese language / preparatory (non-degree):
     long- and short-term options; beginners welcome; an intensive preparatory program feeds into degree study.

HSK 3.0 China is moving to the new HSK (三等九级 / three bands, nine levels). Fudan already publishes level-based HSK requirements, but the exact mapping to the new framework can differ — check the latest official admission guide for your program.

要求因轨道而异（以下为常见基线，请以官方简章 / Origineduglob 顾问为准）：

  • 中文授课本科：
    HSK 5（210分以上）或 HSK 6（180分以上）。复旦2026本科申请材料清单将"CSCA成绩原件"列为必交项，并把HSK标注为"非必交"——但中文授课学习仍建议夯实HSK，具体专业要求请确认。
  • 中文授课研究生：
    HSK 5（人文社科240分以上；理/工/医210分以上）或 HSK 6（180分以上）。
  • 英文授课项目：
    不要求 HSK；通常需雅思6.0/托福80（本科）或雅思6.5/托福90（研究生），英语母语者常可豁免。
  • 汉语 / 预科（非学历）：
    长短期可选，欢迎零基础；密集预科项目可衔接学历学习。

HSK 3.0 中国正逐步推行新版 HSK（三等九级）。复旦已发布分级 HSK 要求，但对应新框架的具体等级可能不同——请务必以你所申请项目的最新官方招生简章为准。

🌆 City & Life · 城市与生活

Shanghai is China's most international metropolis — a cosmopolitan, fast-paced city with excellent public transport, global food, and a huge international-student community. Fudan's main Handan campus sits in the Yangpu District, near the Wujiaochang business hub, with additional Jiangwan, Fenglin (medical) and Zhangjiang campuses.

As a tier-1 city, rent and daily costs are higher than in smaller cities, but on-campus dormitories and student services help keep expenses manageable. Importantly for Chinese-learners, Fudan's International Cultural Exchange School has been an authorized HSK test center since 1990, hosting HSK sessions in May, July and December — you can literally sit your HSK exam on campus.

上海是中国最具国际化的都市——兼容并蓄、节奏明快，公共交通发达、美食全球汇聚，国际学生社群庞大。复旦主校区邯郸校区位于杨浦区，临近五角场商圈，另有江湾、枫林（医学）、张江等校区。

作为一线城市，房租与日常开销高于中小城市，但校内宿舍与学生服务可帮助控制开支。对汉语学习者尤为重要的一点是：复旦国际文化交流学院自1990年起即为国家HSK（汉语水平考试）南方考点，每年5月、7月、12月举办考试——你几乎可以在校园内直接参加HSK。

🎁 Scholarships & Tips · 奖学金与申请提示

Fudan offers several scholarship routes for international students (amounts and quotas change yearly — confirm with an Origineduglob advisor):

  • Chinese Government Scholarship (CSC):
     bilateral program, and the university's High-Level Graduate Program / Silk Road Program (apply in Phase One).
  • Shanghai Government Scholarship
     and Fudan University International Students' Scholarship (merit-based tuition waiver / allowance).
  • International Chinese Language Teachers Scholarship
     (for Chinese-language educators / learners).

Application tip: Fudan's 2026 Chinese-taught undergraduate admission explicitly requires the CSCA (China Scholastic Competency Assessment) score report as part of the application materials — so completing the CSCA early is a concrete, required step for undergraduate applicants.

复旦为国际学生提供多条奖学金渠道（金额与名额逐年调整，请以 Origineduglob 顾问确认为准）：

  • 中国政府奖学金（CSC）：
    含国别双边项目，以及高校自主的高水平研究生项目 / "丝绸之路"项目（第一阶段申请）。
  • 上海市政府奖学金
     与 复旦大学外国留学生奖学金（基于学业表现的学费减免 / 生活补助）。
  • 国际中文教师奖学金
    （面向中文教育工作者 / 学习者）。

申请提示：复旦2026中文授课本科招生明确将 CSCA（来华留学本科入学学业水平测试）成绩原件列为申请材料之一——因此尽早完成 CSCA，是本科申请者一个具体且必须的环节。
