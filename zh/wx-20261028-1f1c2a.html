Chinese Government Scholarship 2027/2028 Now Open

By ORIGIN Education · 2026-10-28 · 原文：微信公众号

Chinese Government Scholarship 2027/2028 Now Open

中国政府奖学金 2027/2028 学年申请在多国开放，最早 1 月 29 日截止

 

If you are planning to study in China in September 2027, the biggest funding window of the year just opened. Chinese embassies and consulates in Indonesia, Sweden and the Maldives all published their Chinese Government Scholarship (CSC) notices for the 2027/2028 academic year in late October, and each sets its own national deadline — the earliest of them closes on 29 January 2027.

如果你计划 2027 年 9 月来中国读书，全年最大的资助窗口刚刚打开。中国驻印度尼西亚大使馆、驻哥德堡总领事馆和驻马尔代夫大使馆都在 10 月下旬发布了 2027/2028 学年中国政府奖学金（CSC）受理通知，各国截止日期不同——其中最早的一条线是 2027 年 1 月 29 日。

The Facts: One Scholarship, Three National Deadlines

事实梳理：一项奖学金，三条国家截止线

The scholarship itself is the same programme everywhere: tuition waived, free university accommodation or a housing subsidy, comprehensive medical insurance at 800 yuan per year (400 yuan for programmes under six months), and a monthly stipend — 2,500 yuan for bachelor's students, 3,000 yuan for master's students and general scholars, 3,500 yuan for doctoral students and senior scholars. A preparatory Chinese year, normally one year, is funded on top of the degree programme.

奖学金本身在全球是同一套标准：免学费，提供免费宿舍或住宿补贴，综合医疗保险 800 元/人/年（学习期限少于 6 个月的为 400 元），外加每月生活费——本科生 2,500 元，硕士研究生和普通进修生 3,000 元，博士研究生和高级进修生 3,500 元。中文补习（预科）一般为一年，在专业学习资助之外另行提供。

The deadlines differ by country of application. China's embassy in Indonesia (notice dated 27 October 2026, agency number 3602) accepts applications until 14 February 2027 for its China-Indonesia Higher Education Scholarship, a fully funded quota of 20 places. The Consulate General in Gothenburg, Sweden (27 October 2026) requires complete submissions by 6 February 2027 through the CGSIS system at campuschina.org. The Embassy in the Maldives (agency number 4622) closes earliest, on 29 January 2027.

各国受理截止日不同。中国驻印度尼西亚大使馆（通知日期 2026 年 10 月 27 日，受理机构编号 3602）受理"中印尼高教奖学金"至 2027 年 2 月 14 日，全额奖学金共 20 个名额。驻瑞典哥德堡总领事馆（2026 年 10 月 27 日）要求完整的申请材料不晚于 2027 年 2 月 6 日通过 campuschina.org 的 CGSIS 系统提交。驻马尔代夫大使馆（受理机构编码 4622）截止最早，为 2027 年 1 月 29 日。

Eligibility lines are fixed nationwide: applicants for bachelor's degrees must be under 25, master's applicants under 35, doctoral applicants under 40, general scholars under 45 and senior scholars under 50. Chinese-taught master's and doctoral applicants need HSK Level 4; general and senior scholars need HSK Level 3. Everyone must submit a criminal record certificate issued within six months of applying, and a passport valid for at least twelve months from the intended start of study.

年龄与学历门槛全国统一：本科一般不超过 25 周岁，硕士不超过 35 周岁，博士不超过 40 周岁，普通进修生不超过 45 周岁，高级进修生不超过 50 周岁。中文授课的硕士、博士申请人须达到 HSK 四级；普通进修生和高级进修生须达到 HSK 三级。所有申请人都须提交提交之日前 6 个月以内开具的无犯罪记录证明，以及自预计学习开始日期起算有效期不少于 12 个月的护照。

Beyond the shared standards, each channel has its own character. Indonesia's China-Indonesia Higher Education Scholarship funds 20 fully funded places in fields such as ocean studies, medical health, tourism and education — but international travel is at the applicant's own expense. Sweden's route runs entirely through the national CGSIS portal. Maldives applicants quote agency number 4622 in the system. Because quotas and subject priorities differ from country to country, always read your own country's notice rather than borrowing a friend's checklist from elsewhere.

在统一标准之外，每条通道又各有特点。印尼的"中印尼高教奖学金"提供 20 个全额奖学金名额，面向海洋、医学健康、旅游、教育等所需专业——但国际旅费自理。瑞典通道完全走全国统一的 CGSIS 门户。马尔代夫申请人在系统里填报受理机构编码 4622。由于各国名额与专业侧重不同，请务必读你所在国家的通知，而不是照搬别国朋友的清单。

One more flexible door deserves attention: if your Chinese is not yet at HSK 4, the scholarship is still within reach. Funding includes a preparatory Chinese year — normally one year — before the degree begins, and applicants who enrol as general scholars to study the Chinese language itself are exempt from HSK requirements altogether. Build that prep year into your timeline honestly: it can change your graduation year.

还有一扇灵活的门值得注意：如果你的中文还没到 HSK 四级，奖学金依然可及。资助包含中文补习（预科）——一般为一年——安排在专业学习之前；而以普通进修生身份来华专门学习汉语的申请人，则完全免于 HSK 要求。请诚实地把预科这一年纳入你的时间规划：它可能改变你的毕业年份。

  

What It Means for Applicants

深度解读：对申请者意味着什么

The hardest requirement is not the paperwork — it is the pre-admission letter. You cannot simply apply for the scholarship and wait: the notices require at least one admission letter or document indicating intent to admit (a pre-admission letter or a supervisor's acceptance certificate) from one of your preferred universities before you submit online. In practice, that means contacting university admissions offices and, for research degrees, individual supervisors — a process that takes weeks of emails, so start it now.

最难的不是材料，而是预录取文件。你不能只填奖学金申请然后干等：各国通知都要求在在线提交时至少附上任意一所志愿院校出具的录取通知书或意向录取文件（预录取通知书、导师接收证明等）。实际操作中，这意味着要主动联系院校招生部门，攻读研究型学位的还要联系导师——来回邮件往往要耗上数周，所以现在就应启动。

Undergraduate applicants face one extra gate: the CSCA (China Scholastic Competency Assessment). For 2027 entry, your score report must come from a test sitting after 1 March 2025, and anyone planning to start in September 2027 must sit the November 2026, December 2026 or January 2027 session. Registration runs on csca.cn.

本科申请人还多一道门：CSCA（来华留学本科入学学业水平测试）。2027 年入学的成绩单必须是 2025 年 3 月 1 日之后考试日期出具的有效成绩；计划 2027 年 9 月入学的，必须报名 2026 年 11 月、12 月或 2027 年 1 月的 CSCA 考试。报名通道为 csca.cn。

Finally, know the exclusivity rule: CSC scholars may not hold another scholarship from any level of Chinese government or their host university (one-off prizes excepted). Concealing other funding means cancellation, repayment, and a three-year ban on reapplying.

最后要记住排他规则：中国政府奖学金生不得同时享受中国各级政府和录取院校设立的其他奖学金（各类一次性奖励金除外）。刻意隐瞒资助情况的，资格将被取消、已领款项须退还，且三年内不得再申请。

There is also a second layer of deadlines worth watching. Alongside the embassy track, many universities run their own Type-B quotas with separate campus deadlines — Yantai University closes its application system on 1 March 2027 with priority review for submissions before 1 January, and Northeast Normal University closes on 28 February 2027. These routes do not replace the embassy process; each is a parallel door. Applying through the wrong one, or discovering a campus deadline in March, is how strong candidates lose a full season.

还有第二层截止日期值得盯住。在使馆通道之外，许多高校自设 Type-B 名额，校内截止日各不相同——烟台大学申请系统 2027 年 3 月 1 日关闭（2027 年 1 月 1 日前提交可进入第一批集中审核），东北师范大学则截止于 2027 年 2 月 28 日。这些通道不替代使馆流程，而是并行的另一扇门。走错通道、或在 3 月才发现某个校内截止日，是强者错失一整季的常见方式。

Your Action List

行动建议：三步走清单

Step 1 — This week: confirm your national agency number and deadline on campuschina.org or with the local Chinese embassy, and create your account in the CGSIS system (www.campuschina.org).

第一步（本周）：在 campuschina.org 或当地中国使领馆确认你所在国家的受理机构编号与截止日期，并在 CGSIS 系统（www.campuschina.org）注册账号。

Step 2 — November: contact your preferred universities for a pre-admission letter or supervisor acceptance; prepare notarised highest degree certificate, transcripts (with certified translations), the physical examination form for foreigners, and the criminal record certificate. Undergraduates book their CSCA session at csca.cn.

第二步（11 月）：联系意向院校获取预录取通知书或导师接收证明；准备经公证的最高学历证明、成绩单（非中英文需公证翻译）、外国人体格检查表和无犯罪记录证明。本科申请人请在 csca.cn 锁定 CSCA 场次。

Step 3 — Before your national deadline: upload everything, double-check that scans are clear and complete, and remember that results are published in the system around July–August 2027. If you are unsure whether your documents meet the standard, Originedu's advisors review CSC applications against the official checklist every season.

第三步（各国截止日前）：上传全部材料，逐项确认扫描件清晰完整；录取结果大约在 2027 年 7—8 月通过系统公布。如果你不确定材料是否达标，Originedu 的顾问每个申请季都会按官方清单逐项复核 CSC 申请。

The embassy notices list the same core documents everywhere. Have these ready before you start the online form:

各使馆通知所列核心材料基本一致。开始在线填写前，请备齐以下材料：

  • CSC application form (completed in Chinese or English)
  • 中国政府奖学金申请表（中文或英文填写）
  • Passport information page — renew first if validity falls short of 12 months
  • 护照首页——有效期不足 12 个月的请先换发新护照
  • Notarised highest degree certificate, or an expected-graduation certificate
  • 经公证的最高学历证明，或预计毕业证明/在学证明
  • Academic transcripts, with certified translations for other languages
  • 学习成绩单，非中英文的须附经公证的中文或英文译文
  • Foreigner Physical Examination Form (valid version)
  • 外国人体格检查表（有效版本）
  • Criminal record certificate issued within six months of applying
  • 提交之日前 6 个月以内开具的无犯罪记录证明
  • Pre-admission letter or supervisor acceptance certificate from a preferred university
  • 志愿院校出具的预录取通知书或导师接收证明
  • Valid CSCA score report (bachelor's applicants)
  • 有效 CSCA 成绩单（本科申请人）

Upload quality matters more than people expect: the notices warn that documents must be clear, true and valid, recommend professional scanning equipment, and place the consequences of unreadable files squarely on the applicant. Scan with a machine, not a phone, and check every page after upload.

上传质量比多数人预想的更重要：通知明确要求材料清晰、真实、有效，建议使用专业设备扫描，且因材料不清晰造成的后果由申请人自行承担。用扫描仪而不是手机拍照，上传后逐页回看。

A Final Note

写在最后

A fully funded CSC place covers tuition, housing, insurance and a living stipend — but it rewards the applicants who move first. Match your profile to a programme, secure the pre-admission document, and let the deadline you cannot move work in your favour rather than against you.

一个全额资助的 CSC 名额覆盖学费、住宿、保险和生活费——但它奖励的是先动手的人。把个人背景与项目匹配好、拿下预录取文件，让那条不可移动的截止线为你所用，而不是被它追着跑。
