Southeast University Opens Its 2027 International Intake

东南大学 2027 年国际学生招生启动，本科第一批 11 月开放

 

Southeast University (SEU) in Nanjing has published its 2027 international student admission brochure, and the first application round for bachelor's programmes opens in November 2026. With six English-taught undergraduate majors, 2,381 international students already on campus and application fees of just 800 yuan, it is one of the most concrete new options for students planning a September 2027 start.

南京的东南大学已发布 2027 年国际学生招生简章，本科项目第一批申请将于 2026 年 11 月开放。六门全英文授课本科专业、2381 名在校国际学生、仅需 800 元申请费——对于计划 2027 年 9 月入学的学生来说，这是目前最具体的新选择之一。

The Facts: Programmes, Fees and Rounds

事实梳理：专业、费用与申请批次

SEU is a national key university under the Ministry of Education, a member of China's "985 Project" and "211 Project", and a Class-A "Double First-Class" university. Twelve disciplines are on the national Double First-Class list (eighth nationwide), and in the ESI rankings Engineering sits at No. 13 and Computer Science at No. 5 globally. The 2027 brochure covers 70 bachelor's majors, 81 master's majors and 68 doctoral majors; six undergraduate programmes — Architecture, Urban and Rural Planning, Civil Engineering, Transportation Engineering, International Economics and Trade, and Clinical Medicine — are taught fully in English with no Chinese requirement, and about 20 of the master's programmes are in English.

东南大学是教育部直属全国重点大学、"985 工程"与"211 工程"重点建设高校、世界一流大学建设 A 类高校。12 个学科入选国家"双一流"建设学科名单（并列全国第八）；ESI 排名中，工程学位居全球第 13 位、计算机科学全球第 5 位。2027 年简章覆盖本科 70 个专业方向、硕士 81 个、博士 68 个；本科有 6 个全英文授课专业——建筑学、城乡规划、土木工程、交通运输工程、国际经济与贸易、临床医学——无需汉语基础即可入读，硕士约 20 个专业方向可全英文授课。

Programme lengths are 4 years for most bachelor's degrees (5 for Architecture and Clinical Medicine, 6 for English-taught Clinical Medicine), 3 years for master's (2 for Public Health and Engineering Management), and 4 for doctoral programmes. Chinese-taught tuition starts at 16,000 yuan per year for humanities bachelor's degrees (19,000 for engineering, 20,000 for medicine); English-taught bachelor's runs 30,000–50,000 yuan. Application fee is 800 yuan regardless of outcome, insurance 800 yuan per year, and accommodation 9,000 yuan per year for a bed in a double room.

学制方面：本科多数 4 年（建筑学、临床医学 5 年，英文授课临床医学 6 年），硕士 3 年（公共卫生、工程管理 2 年），博士 4 年。中文授课本科学费从文科每年 16,000 元起（理工 19,000 元、医科 20,000 元）；英文授课本科 30,000—50,000 元。申请费 800 元（无论录取与否不予退还），保险 800 元/学年，住宿为双人间床位 9,000 元/学年。

The application calendar runs in rounds. Bachelor's programmes accept applications in three batches: November 2026–February 2027, March–May 2027, and June 2027. For postgraduates, the CSC Type-B High-Level Graduate Programme runs November 2026–February 2027, the Silk Road Programme March–May 2027, university scholarships in two batches, and self-funded applications November 2026–May 2027.

申请分批次进行。本科三批：2026 年 11 月—2027 年 2 月、2027 年 3—5 月、2027 年 6 月。研究生方面：中国政府奖学金 Type-B 高水平研究生项目为 2026 年 11 月—2027 年 2 月，Type-B 丝绸之路项目为 2027 年 3—5 月，校级奖学金分两批，自费项目为 2026 年 11 月—2027 年 5 月。

Behind the headline rankings sits a large research and teaching machine. SEU has 33 schools and departments and 43,651 full-time students, runs 24 international joint research platforms in fields such as architecture, communications, biomedical science, zero-carbon energy and intelligent manufacturing, and leads the first China-UK university engineering education alliance as well as the world's first carbon-neutrality university consortium. It also maintains partnerships with more than 60 world-class universities across 20 countries and regions.

排名之外，是一台庞大的科研教学机器。东南大学共有 33 个院系、全日制在校生 43,651 人，在建筑、通信、生物医学、零碳能源、智能制造等领域建有 24 个国际合作科研平台，牵头发起首个中英大学工程教育与研究联盟和首个碳中和世界大学联盟。学校还与 20 个国家或地区的 60 余所世界一流大学建立合作。

Postgraduate tuition deserves a closer look. Chinese-taught master's programmes cost 18,000–30,000 yuan per year and English-taught master's 30,000–33,000 yuan; doctoral programmes run 28,000–50,000 yuan in Chinese and 33,000–38,000 yuan in English. Dormitory rooms are double-occupancy with a private bathroom, water heater and air conditioning, and each building has shared laundry rooms — the sort of detail that matters when you compare the real cost of two offers on a spreadsheet.

研究生学费值得细看。中文授课硕士每年 18,000—30,000 元，英文授课硕士 30,000—33,000 元；博士中文授课 28,000—50,000 元、英文授课 33,000—38,000 元。宿舍为双人间，配有独立卫浴、热水器与空调，每层楼设有公共洗衣房——当你在表格里对比两份录取的真实成本时，这些细节都很重要。

  

What It Means for Applicants

深度解读：门槛在哪里、机会在哪里

Language requirements are clear and layered. Chinese-taught programmes — bachelor's and master's alike — ask for HSK Level 4 with 200 points or above, while medicine, economics and International Chinese Language Education require HSK Level 5. English-taught applicants need TOEFL 80, IELTS 6.0 or Duolingo 100, waived for native or official-language English speakers. Note the graduation threshold too: Chinese-taught bachelor's students must reach HSK 5 by graduation, English-taught bachelor's students HSK 4, and postgraduates HSK 3.

语言要求分层清晰。中文授课的本科与研究生都要求 HSK 四级 200 分以上，医学类、经济类及国际中文教育专业要求 HSK 五级。英文授课需托福 80 分、雅思 6.0 或多邻国 100 分，母语或官方语言为英语者免交。还要注意毕业语言门槛：中文授课本科毕业须达 HSK 五级，英文授课本科 HSK 四级，研究生 HSK 三级。

Bachelor's applicants must also take the CSCA: one of Humanities Chinese or STEM Chinese, plus Mathematics, and at least one of Physics or Chemistry for science, engineering, agriculture or medicine majors. Admission can use SAT, IB, A-Level or ACT scores for academic documents, which widens the route for students from international schools.

本科申请人还须参加 CSCA：文科中文或理科中文选考其一，加数学；理工农医类专业须在物理、化学中至少选考一门。学术材料可用 SAT、IB、A-Level 或 ACT 成绩申请，这为国际学校背景的学生拓宽了通道。

One nationality rule deserves special attention: applicants who migrated from mainland China, Hong Kong, Macao or Taiwan must have held a valid foreign passport before 30 April 2023 and resided abroad for at least two years between 30 April 2023 and 30 April 2027 (nine months counting as one year). Document requirements mirror other top universities: notarised degree certificates, transcripts, a non-criminal record, a physical examination form within six months, a financial guarantee — plus two recommendation letters, a research proposal of at least 800 words and a supervisor acceptance letter for master's and doctoral applicants, and a portfolio for architecture-related majors.

有一条国籍规则要特别注意：原中国大陆、香港、澳门和台湾居民移民外国者，须于 2023 年 4 月 30 日前取得有效外国护照，且在 2023 年 4 月 30 日至 2027 年 4 月 30 日期间在国外实际居住 2 年以上（满 9 个月按一年计）。材料要求与其他顶尖高校类似：经公证的学历学位证书、成绩单、无犯罪记录证明、6 个月内体检表、经济担保书；硕士和博士还须提交两封推荐信、不少于 800 字的研究计划及导师接收函；建筑学相关专业需附作品集。

The campus itself is proof that the international route works: 2,381 international students from more than 100 countries already study at SEU, 1,688 of them in degree programmes. That scale matters to a new applicant in two ways — visas, dormitories and language support are handled by an experienced international students office, and the network you join reaches across ASEAN, Africa and Europe long after graduation day.

校园本身就是这条国际通道的证明：已有来自 100 多个国家的 2,381 名国际学生在东南大学就读，其中学历生 1,688 人。这个规模对新生意味着两件事——签证、宿舍与语言支持由经验成熟的国际学生办公室统一处理；而你加入的校友网络，会在毕业之后很久仍延伸至东盟、非洲与欧洲。

Your Action List

行动建议：怎么把握第一批

Shortlist before November. The first round (November 2026–February 2027) is where scholarship quotas are richest. Decide between the six English-taught bachelor's majors and the Chinese-taught list, and check which campus and tuition band your major falls into.

11 月前定好清单。 第一批（2026 年 11 月—2027 年 2 月）奖学金名额最充裕。在 6 个英文授课本科专业与中文授课目录之间做出取舍，并确认你的专业在哪个校区、哪一档学费。

Book your tests. If you need HSK, schedule a sitting that leaves scores ready before February 2027; bachelor's applicants register for the CSCA session at csca.cn that matches their major's subject requirements.

把考试排上日程。 需要 HSK 的，安排一个能在 2027 年 2 月前出分的场次；本科申请人请在 csca.cn 报名与所报专业科目要求匹配的 CSCA 场次。

Assemble the file early. Two recommendation letters and a supervisor acceptance letter take weeks to arrange — request them in November, not January. If you want help weighing SEU against other 2027 options, Originedu's advisors build university shortlists and document checklists for applicants every admission season.

提前备齐材料。 两封推荐信和导师接收函往往要数周时间——11 月就去约，别拖到 1 月。如果你需要把东南大学和其他 2027 年选择放在一起权衡，Originedu 的顾问每个申请季都会为申请人做院校清单和材料清单。

  • 护照首页
  • Degree certificates and transcripts (SAT / IB / A-Level / ACT accepted for bachelor's)
  • 学历学位证书与成绩单（本科可用 SAT / IB / A-Level / ACT 成绩申请）
  • Language certificates — HSK or TOEFL / IELTS / Duolingo, by track
  • 语言证明——按授课通道提交 HSK 或托福 / 雅思 / 多邻国
  • CSCA score report (bachelor's applicants)
  • CSCA 成绩（本科申请人）
  • Non-criminal record and a physical examination form within six months
  • 无犯罪记录证明及 6 个月内体检表
  • Statement of financial support
  • 经济担保书
  • Two recommendation letters, a research proposal of 800+ words and a supervisor acceptance letter (master's and doctoral)
  • 两封推荐信、不少于 800 字的研究计划及导师接收函（硕士、博士）
  • A portfolio for architecture-related majors
  • 建筑学相关专业需附作品集

A Final Note

写在最后

SEU's 2027 brochure is a reminder that strong Chinese universities are now publishing detailed, bilingual-friendly admission calendars months in advance. The advantage goes to applicants who treat November not as the start of the process, but as the deadline for having already prepared.

东南大学的 2027 年简章提醒我们：如今实力强劲的中国大学都会提前数月发布详尽、对国际生友好的招生日历。优势属于那些不把 11 月当作起点、而是当作"准备必须已完成"的截止线的申请人。
