今日推荐大学 ｜ Today's University

EN — Zhejiang University (ZJU), in Hangzhou, is one of China's most prestigious research universities. It traces its roots to the Qiushi (Seek Truth) Academy founded in 1897, and was reconstituted as National Zhejiang University in 1928. Today it is a founding member of the C9 League and a Double First-Class (A-class) university, consistently ranked among the global top 50 (QS World University Rankings 2026: #47; rankings vary by list, please use the year's figure as reference).

中文 — 浙江大学（ZJU）坐落于杭州，是中国最负盛名的研究型大学之一。其前身是 1897 年创办的求是书院，1928 年定名国立浙江大学；现为 C9 联盟成员、双一流（A 类）高校，常年位居世界主流排名中国内地高校最前列（2026 QS 全球第 47 位，不同榜单略有差异，以当年为准）。

学校一句话亮点 ｜ One-line Highlight

EN — "Seek Truth, Innovate" (求是创新): a 128-year 求是 tradition meeting a fast-rising global research powerhouse — with an on-campus HSK test center and a dedicated international campus.

中文 — 一句亮点：百年"求是"传统，遇上快速崛起的世界级科研强校——校内即设 HSK 考点，并拥有专门的国际化校区。

适合哪些留学生 ｜ Who This University Is For

EN — ZJU fits international students who want English-taught degrees such as MBBS, engineering, or international business; have a solid Chinese foundation (HSK 5 and up) aiming for top-tier programs; are interested in agriculture, life sciences, food, environment, CS, or clinical medicine; want big-city access with a livable, greener, more affordable environment; or plan to sit the HSK exam (ZJU is an authorized center) or prepare for the CSCA bachelor's entrance test. Non-degree Chinese language programs with placement testing welcome beginners from zero.

中文 — 浙江大学尤其适合：想读英文授课项目（如 MBBS、工程、国际商务）的同学；汉语基础较好（HSK5 起）、目标顶尖学科的同学；对农业、生命科学、食品、环境、计算机、临床医学感兴趣的同学；既想在大城市求学、又希望环境宜居、成本可控的同学；以及计划参加 HSK（校内即考点）或准备 CSCA 本科入学测试的同学。非学历汉语进修零基础亦可起步。

优势专业与项目 ｜ Strength Programs

EN — Standout areas: Agriculture & Life Sciences (plant protection, horticulture, tea science, veterinary medicine — an A+ cluster); Engineering (control science A+, CS, software, optical engineering A+, agricultural engineering); Management (management science A+, agricultural & forestry economics A+); Clinical Medicine — a 6-year English-taught MBBS incl. the "Belt and Road" International School of Medicine; Science and cross-disciplinary research; and the Haining International Campus with the University of Illinois Urbana-Champaign (ZJUI) and the University of Edinburgh, offering English-taught dual-degree pathways.

中文 — 优势领域：农业与生命科学（植物保护、园艺、茶学、兽医学等 A+ 学科群）；工科（控制科学与工程 A+、计算机、软件、光学工程 A+、农业工程）；管理学（管理科学与工程 A+、农林经济管理 A+）；临床医学——六年制全英文 MBBS，含"一带一路"国际医学院；理科与交叉学科；以及海宁国际校区——与 UIUC（ZJUI）、爱丁堡大学合作，提供全英文授课与双学位通道。

语言与入学要求 ｜ Language & Admission

EN — From ZJU's official 2026 international undergraduate brochure & catalogue: Chinese-taught bachelor's generally require HSK 5 (≥180); Chinese-medium secondary education may apply for HSK exemption. English-taught programs (incl. MBBS, some engineering/business) require TOEFL/IELTS; HSK not needed for admission, but HSK 4 is required for graduation. MBBS (EN): IELTS 6.0 (no band <5.0) or TOEFL 75+; before internship reach HSK 4 (≥195) + HSKK Intermediate. Bachelor's applicants generally under 25; fees & deadlines per official calendar (usually Oct–May). CSCA: from 2026/2027 all bachelor's applicants must submit a valid CSCA score (5 sittings/yr: Jan, Mar, Apr, Jun, Dec). HSK 3.0 note: reformed HSK is "three levels, nine grades" (L1–3 / L4–6 / L7–9); ZJU still uses older HSK 4/5/6 banding — confirm conversion with an advisor.

中文 — 依据浙大 2026 国际本科招生简章与专业目录：汉语授课本科一般要求 HSK5（≥180）；汉语授课中学经历者可申请免 HSK。英语授课专业（含 MBBS、部分工程 / 商科）须提交托福 / 雅思；入学不强制 HSK，但毕业前须达 HSK4。MBBS（英文）：雅思 6.0（单项≥5.0）或托福 75 起；实习前须 HSK4（≥195）+ HSKK 中级。本科一般 25 岁以下；费用与截止时间以官方为准（通常 10 月–次年 5 月）。CSCA：自 2026/2027 起所有本科国际生须提交有效成绩单（每年 1/3/4/6/12 月 5 次）。HSK 3.0 提示：新版"三等九级"（初级 L1–3/中级 L4–6/高级 L7–9），浙大仍按旧版 HSK4/5/6 发布，换算请向顾问确认。

城市与生活 ｜ City & Living

EN — Hangzhou, capital of Zhejiang, is "Paradise on Earth" and home to UNESCO-listed West Lake. A thriving e-commerce & digital-economy hub (Alibaba HQ) yet green and livable. Hangzhou East high-speed rail reaches Shanghai in ~1 hour; Xiaoshan International Airport serves global routes. Cost of living is moderate — generally lower than Beijing/Shanghai, though rising. Campus life centers on the Zijingang (紫金港) main campus near West Lake.

中文 — 杭州是浙江省会，"人间天堂"，西湖为世界遗产。既是电商与数字经济重镇（阿里巴巴总部），又绿意盎然、宜居宜学。杭州东站高铁约一小时达上海，萧山国际机场通达全球。生活成本相对适中——低于北京、上海，但近年上升。校园生活以靠近西湖的紫金港主校区为核心。

奖学金与申请提示 ｜ Scholarships & Tips

EN — Options: Chinese Government Scholarship (CSC), Zhejiang Provincial Government Scholarship, Zhejiang University scholarships, and the International Chinese Language Teachers Scholarship. Exact amounts, quotas, and eligibility — and the latest tuition (the official 2026 catalogue lists many science/engineering programs at ~RMB 24,800/year, subject to annual update) — should be confirmed with an Origineduglob advisor or the admissions office. Tip: ZJU is itself an authorized HSK test center (HSK 4/5/6 + HSKK at Zijingang International Education College), so you can sit the exam on campus.

中文 — 渠道包括：中国政府奖学金（CSC）、浙江省政府奖学金、浙大校级奖学金、国际中文教师奖学金。具体名额、金额与条件——连同最新学费（官方 2026 目录多数列约 24,800 元 / 学年，以当年为准）——建议与 Origineduglob 的留学顾问或招生办确认。实用提示：浙大本身就是授权 HSK 考点（紫金港校区，开考 HSK4/5/6 与 HSKK），校内即可应考。
