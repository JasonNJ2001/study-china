OriginEduGlob Meets Cool Panda: Bridging India and China

By ORIGIN Education · 2026-11-04 · 原文：微信公众号

ORIGINEDU · STUDY IN CHINA

携手同行，共育英才

尚源教育 × 印度 Cool Panda 中文语言学校云端会谈圆满举行

11月3日下午，尚源（南京）科技教育有限公司（OriginEduGlob）与印度 Cool Panda 中文语言学校举行线上会谈。双方围绕来华留学项目合作、中文人才培养与留学通道建设等议题深入交流，气氛友好而热烈。

On the afternoon of November 3, OriginEduGlob held an online meeting with Cool Panda Chinese Language School of India. The two sides held in-depth exchanges on study-in-China project cooperation, Chinese-language talent cultivation and study-abroad pathways in a friendly and warm atmosphere.

01一场跨越山海的对话

南京的初冬，梧桐叶染上金黄。尚源教育会议室内，双方团队如约见面：屏幕一端是来自印度孟买的 Cool Panda 团队，另一端是尚源教育的来华留学项目团队。

In early winter in Nanjing, the plane trees turned golden. Inside the meeting room, the two teams met as scheduled. On one side of the screen was the Cool Panda team from Mumbai, India, and on the other the study-in-China project team of OriginEduGlob.

会谈中，双方就印度学生来华留学的市场需求、语言培训与申请衔接、行前准备与落地服务等话题逐一探讨，并就未来合作方向达成诸多共识。

During the meeting, the two sides explored the market demand for Indian students studying in China, the link between language training and applications, and pre-departure and on-ground services, reaching broad consensus on the direction of future cooperation.

— 双方团队就合作议题深入交流 · The two teams in in-depth discussion

从语言学习，到院校申请，再到落地中国，两家机构各展所长，共同描绘出一幅无缝衔接的留学服务图景。

From language learning and university application to arrival in China, the two organizations each brought their strengths to paint a picture of seamless study-abroad service.

— 尚源教育团队认真记录会谈要点 · OriginEduGlob team taking notes

02走进 Cool Panda：让中文学习有趣又硬核

Cool Panda 是印度颇具影响力的中文语言教育机构，创立于孟买，以有趣、地道、科技驱动为特色，学员覆盖学生、商务人士、旅行者与职场专业人士。

Cool Panda is a leading Chinese-language education institution in India, founded in Mumbai, known for being fun, authentic and tech-driven, serving students, business people, travellers and professionals.

机构坚持科技赋能学习，自主研发 PDF 教材、有声翻翻书、记忆卡片、AR 概念演示与在线题库，并推出配套学习应用。其团队宣称零基础学员约 365 小时即可达到熟练水平；创始人 Parth Shah 一年内通过 HSK 六级，并曾为古吉拉特邦政府（经 FICCI）与广东省政府活动提供 20 名翻译人员。

It empowers learning with technology, building PDF materials, audio flipbooks, flashcards, AR concepts and online quizzes, plus a companion app. The team claims beginners reach proficiency in about 365 hours. Founder Parth Shah passed HSK 6 within a year and the school once provided 20 interpreters for events of the Gujarat Government via FICCI and the Guangdong Government.

在如何教印度学生学好中文这件事上，Cool Panda 既是热情的践行者，也是专业的引路人。

In teaching Indian students Chinese, Cool Panda is both a passionate practitioner and a professional guide.

— Cool Panda 官网形象（科技驱动的中文语言学习机构）· Cool Panda official website

OriginEduGlob was established in Nanjing in 2025, prepared in 2023, and is a member of the China Education Association for International Exchange, focused on study-in-China services.
