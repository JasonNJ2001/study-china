Liaoning University 2027: Costs, CSCA Subjects and Deadlines

辽宁大学 2027 年国际学生招生：从学费到住宿的全年成本清单

 

Liaoning University (LNU) in Shenyang has released its 2027 international student admissions brochure, and it is unusually concrete: the school publishes every cost line — from tuition to dormitory beds to visa fees — alongside its full testing schedule. For families comparing Chinese universities on total annual spend rather than prestige alone, this is one of the most transparent budgets published so far this cycle, with tuition for a bachelor's degree at 18,000 RMB per year.

位于沈阳的辽宁大学已发布 2027 年国际学生招生简章，而且公布得出奇地具体：学校把每一项费用——从学费到宿舍床位再到签证费——连同完整的考试时间安排一并公示。对于按"全年总花费"而不是只按名气来比较中国大学的家庭来说，这是本轮招生季透明度最高的预算之一，本科学费为每年 18,000 元人民币。

The brochure, published by LNU's College of International Education, covers degree programs, a Chinese language training track, and the entrance testing process. Applications and all fee-payment instructions are handled exclusively through the university's official admissions email, studyinlnu@lnu.edu.cn — the school warns that payment details are sent only from that address, a useful anti-scam signal worth remembering.

这份由辽宁大学国际教育学院发布的简章，涵盖学历项目、汉语进修项目以及入学考试流程。报名及所有缴费说明均通过学校唯一的招生官方邮箱 studyinlnu@lnu.edu.cn 发送——校方特别提示，缴费方式只会从这一邮箱发出，这是一个值得记住的防骗信号。

The Full-Year Budget, Line by Line

一年要花多少钱：逐项拆解

Tuition differs by level: 18,000 RMB per year for bachelor's students, 25,000 RMB for master's students, 30,000 RMB for doctoral students, and 13,000 RMB per year — or 2,500 RMB per month — for Chinese language trainees. The application fee is 800 RMB per person per program.

学费按学历层次区分：本科生每年 18,000 元，硕士研究生每年 25,000 元，博士研究生每年 30,000 元，汉语进修生每年 13,000 元（或每月 2,500 元）。报名费为每人每项 800 元。

 

Living costs are published just as precisely. Compulsory medical insurance runs 800 RMB per year, collected by Lianhua International Brokerage (Beijing) Co., Ltd. Dormitory beds cost 64 RMB per day in a single room or 32 RMB per day in a double room. Budget also for the entry health check of 550–600 RMB and an annual visa-related fee of 800 RMB, both charged at the rates set by the quarantine and exit-entry authorities at the time.

生活成本同样公布得非常精确。必须购买的医疗保险为每年 800 元，由联华国际经纪（北京）有限公司收取。宿舍床位为单人间每天 64 元、双人间每天 32 元。此外还需预算体检费 550 至 600 元，以及每年 800 元的签证相关费用，两项均以检疫部门和出入境机关届时收费为准。

Add it up and a typical bachelor's year in a double room lands near 25,000–30,000 RMB including insurance, housing and fixed fees — before food and personal spending. That puts LNU well below the coastal tier of Chinese universities, without giving up a comprehensive university environment.

把各项加起来：住在双人间的本科生一年固定支出（含保险、住宿和固定费用）大约在 25,000 至 30,000 元之间——这还不算伙食和个人开销。这一水平明显低于中国沿海地区的大学，同时又不牺牲综合性大学的环境。

The CSCA Testing Route

CSCA 考试：科目组合与六个考试月份

 

Bachelor's applicants take the China Scholastic Competency Assessment (CSCA), with sessions running across six months: November and December 2026, then January, March, April and June 2027. The LNU subject combination is fixed: Professional Chinese is compulsory, Mathematics is compulsory for everyone, and Physics or Chemistry is taken according to the requirements of the chosen major.

本科申请者需参加来华留学本科入学学业水平测试（CSCA），考试分布在六个月份：2026 年 11 月、12 月，以及 2027 年 1 月、3 月、4 月和 6 月。辽大的科目组合是固定的：专业中文为必考，数学对所有人必考，物理或化学按所报专业要求选考。

There is one worthwhile exemption: applicants to the Chinese Language bachelor's program who hold a valid HSK Level 4 report with a score of 180 or above are exempt from the Professional Chinese subject. Registration, fees and session details are published on the official CSCA website, www.csca.cn.

这里有一条值得利用的免考政策：申请汉语言本科专业的申请人，如能提供有效期内 HSK 四级 180 分及以上的成绩报告单，可免考"专业中文"科目。考试报名、收费标准与场次安排均通过 CSCA 官方网站 www.csca.cn 发布。

Interviews run from May to August 2027, with exact times announced by email, and graduate entrance examinations take place between May and July 2027. Chinese language trainees take a placement test in the first week of each semester. After testing and comprehensive review, admission results arrive by email within ten days.

面试时间为 2027 年 5 至 8 月，具体安排以邮件通知为准；研究生入学考试在 2027 年 5 至 7 月进行；汉语进修项目的分班考试在春、秋季开学第一周举行。考试及综合考核结束后 10 日内，学校将通过邮件通知录取结果。

How Admission Actually Works

录取是怎么定的：三条输入，一个名单

The brochure is clear about the formula: the university selects candidates based on entrance test results, the study plan, and academic transcripts. There is no separate English interview track and no bond with agents — every step, from fee instructions to admission letters, is communicated by the university itself through its official email channel.

简章把录取逻辑写得很清楚：学校根据入学考试成绩、学习计划和成绩单择优确定录取名单。这里没有另一条英文面试通道，也没有与任何中介绑定的报名入口——从缴费说明到录取通知，每个环节都由学校通过官方邮箱直接与申请人沟通。

That emphasis on the study plan matters in practice. Two applicants with identical CSCA scores are separated by how concretely they describe what they want to study and why at LNU. Write your plan around the program you actually chose, mention the subjects you passed in the test, and keep it consistent with your transcript — reviewers do read it line by line.

这一点在实际操作中很有分量：两位 CSCA 成绩相同的申请人，高下往往取决于谁能具体说清自己在辽大学什么、为什么学。学习计划要围绕你实际报考的专业来写，提到自己在考试中通过的科目，并与成绩单保持一致——审核老师会逐行阅读。

Shenyang itself strengthens the value case. As the capital of Liaoning province, it offers big-city infrastructure — metro lines, an international airport, major hospitals — at living costs well below Beijing or Shanghai. For a student budget built around published numbers, that gap compounds over four years of a bachelor's degree.

沈阳这个城市也在为性价比加分。作为辽宁省会，它拥有大城市的基建——地铁、国际机场、大型医院——而生活成本明显低于北京和上海。对一份按公布数字编制的留学预算来说，这个差距会在四年的本科生涯里不断累积。

For students whose Chinese is not yet ready for a degree program, the Chinese language training track offers a gentler entry: tuition is 13,000 RMB per year or 2,500 RMB per month, and placement tests are held in the first week of each semester, so new arrivals are grouped by real level rather than paper certificates. Many families use this one-year track to build the HSK scores needed for degree admission in the following cycle.

对中文还达不到学历项目要求的学生，汉语进修项目提供了更平缓的入口：学费每年 13,000 元，或按月缴 2,500 元；分班考试安排在每学期开学第一周，新生按真实水平分班，而不是只看证书。不少家庭会先用这一年积累 HSK 成绩，再在下一个招生季冲击学历项目。

What To Watch Out For

三个注意事项，都在简章原文里

1. Fees are non-refundable and deadlines are hard. If you cannot register within the stated period, your admission is treated as automatically forfeited, and the 800 RMB application fee is not returned under any circumstances. Choose your CSCA months early so scores land before interview season.

第一，报名费不退、时限刚性。 如不能在规定期限内报到，将被视为自动放弃入学资格；报名费一经缴纳不予退还。请尽早选定 CSCA 考试月份，确保成绩在面试季之前到手。

2. Honesty clauses apply to every field you fill in. The brochure states that misreporting or concealment discovered at application, admission or after enrollment leads to cancellation of the application, admission or student status. Read every field twice before submitting.

第二，诚信条款覆盖每一项填报信息。 简章明确：在申请、录取及入学后被认定存在迟报、谎报、瞒报情况的，将取消申请资格、录取资格或学籍。提交前请逐项核对两遍。

3. Confirm costs at payment time. The health-check fee (550–600 RMB) and visa fee (800 RMB per year) follow the rates of the quarantine and exit-entry authorities, so treat the brochure numbers as a planning baseline rather than a final invoice.

第三，缴费时以当期标准为准。 体检费（550 至 600 元）和签证费（每年 800 元）以检疫部门与出入境机关收费为准，请把简章里的数字当作规划基线，而不是最终账单。
