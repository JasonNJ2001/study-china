Tongji 2027 CSC Graduate Window Opens: 56 Days, Two Portals

同济 2027 中国政府奖学金研究生通道开启：56 天，两个系统

Tongji University opened its 2027 Chinese Government Scholarship window for master's and doctoral applicants on 25 October 2026. It closes on 20 December at 23:59 Beijing time. 56 days looks comfortable until you count the notarisation, the stamped transcripts, the two recommendation letters and the medical form that all have to be in place before the portal locks.

2026 年 10 月 25 日，同济大学 2027 年中国政府奖学金（CSC）硕士、博士项目申请通道正式开放，北京时间 12 月 20 日 23:59 截止。56 天看起来宽裕，但把学历公证、盖章成绩单、两封推荐信和体检表都算进去，时间并不富余。

The Facts

事实梳理

According to the notice released by Tongji's International Students Office on 25 October 2026, this round covers enrolment in autumn 2027, that is, August to September. Applicants must file through Tongji's own portal at study-info.tongji.edu.cn and, in parallel, complete an application in the CSC online system at studyinchina.csc.edu.cn. The Chinese Government Scholarship Application Form is downloaded from the CSC system. Tongji's agency number is 10247 and the programme type is Type B.

根据同济大学留学生办公室 2026 年 10 月 25 日发布的简章，本轮申请对应 2027 年秋季入学，即 8 月至 9 月。申请人须在同济大学网上申请系统（study-info.tongji.edu.cn）提交申请，同时登录国家留学基金委来华留学网上报名系统（studyinchina.csc.edu.cn）完成申请。《中国政府奖学金申请表》在 CSC 系统内下载。同济大学机构代码为 10247，留学项目种类为 B 类。

The application fee is 600 yuan and is not refundable under any circumstances. Tongji states plainly that once the online system closes, no amendments or supplementary materials will be accepted. This is not a soft deadline with a grace period.

报名费为 600 元，任何情况下不予退还。简章明确写明：网申系统关闭后，不再接受材料修改与补充。这不是留有余地的软截止日。

What the scholarship actually covers is set out below. The stipend figures are monthly and paid throughout the funded period.

奖学金的具体资助内容见下表。生活费按每月计发，覆盖整个资助期。

Two eligibility points are easy to overlook. The age ceiling is 35 for master's applicants and 40 for doctoral applicants. Joint-training programmes are excluded, as are programmes teaching a student's own native language or a third-country language and literature such as English or French. Applicants also may not hold any other Chinese Government Scholarship at the same time.

有两条资格限制容易被忽略。年龄上限为硕士 35 周岁、博士 40 周岁。联合培养项目不可申请；学习本国语言，或英语、法语等第三国语言文学类专业的申请人也不可申请。此外，申请人不得同时获得其他各类中国政府奖学金。

Payment runs through one of two routes: online inside the application system, or by bank transfer with the receipt uploaded into the same system. If you choose the transfer route, the receipt has to be attached before you submit. There is no later reconciliation.

缴费有两种方式：在申请系统内在线支付；或通过银行、网银汇款，再把汇款凭证上传进申请系统。如果选择汇款，凭证必须在提交前一并上传，事后不会补做对账。

One clarification, because it circulates widely in agent forums: the CSCA, the China Scholastic Competency Assessment, applies to bachelor's applicants, not to this graduate programme. A notice issued by the Chinese Embassy in Burkina Faso on 23 October 2026 confirms that from the 2027/2028 academic year every bachelor's applicant for the Chinese Government Scholarship must submit a valid CSCA transcript. Tongji's graduate notice contains no CSCA item at all. Master's and doctoral applicants should put their effort into language proof, the study plan and the recommendation letters.

有一点需要澄清，因为它在各种中介群里流传很广：CSCA（来华留学本科入学学业水平测试）的要求针对本科申请者，不适用于本轮研究生项目。中国驻布基纳法索大使馆 2026 年 10 月 23 日的通知明确，自 2027/2028 学年起，所有申请中国政府奖学金本科项目的学生均须提交有效 CSCA 成绩单。而同济这份研究生简章的材料清单中，完全没有 CSCA 一项。硕士、博士申请人应把精力放在语言证明、学习计划和推荐信上。

What This Means

深度解读

Opening day is not a race. Tongji does not hand out places in the order applications arrive, so there is no advantage in submitting a half-finished file on 25 October. What the first day genuinely changes is the length of the runway you have for the parts that depend on other people: a notary office, your undergraduate university's registrar, two professors, and a designated hospital for the medical examination.

首日开放不是抢名额。同济不按提交先后分配名额，因此在 10 月 25 日仓促交一份不完整的材料没有任何优势。首日真正改变的，是你为那些"依赖别人"的环节留出的时间：公证处、本科学校教务处、两位教授，以及指定医院的体检。

The real failure mode is the two-portal structure. Every year some applicants finish the university form, receive a confirmation email, and assume they are done. They are not. A CSC Type B application only becomes valid when both records exist and match: the same programme, the same agency number 10247, the same personal details. If the two systems disagree, the university cannot forward the file to the China Scholarship Council, and the applicant usually discovers this in January, when nothing can be fixed.

真正容易出问题的是"两个系统"的结构。每年都有申请人填完学校系统、收到确认邮件，就以为结束了——其实没有。CSC B 类申请只有在两边记录都存在且一致时才有效：同一个专业、同一个机构代码 10247、同一套个人信息。如果两个系统信息不一致，学校就无法把材料推荐给国家留学基金委，而申请人往往要到次年 1 月才发现，那时已经无法补救。

The 600-yuan fee also changes the decision. Because it is non-refundable and charged at submission, "trying" a programme you are unsure about is expensive. It pushes applicants toward committing to one programme early, which is the right behaviour anyway: Tongji's form asks for a specific programme, and a vague application reads as a vague applicant.

600 元报名费也在改变决策逻辑。因为不退、且在提交时缴纳，"试试看"的代价变高了。这会促使申请人尽早锁定一个具体专业——而这本来就是正确的做法：同济的申请表要求填写明确专业，一份含糊的申请，读起来就是一个含糊的申请人。

Whether you need a supervisor before applying depends on the school, not on the scholarship. Tongji's notice does not ask for a supervisor acceptance letter at submission. A number of Type B universities do: Guizhou University, for example, issues its own supervisor acceptance form after review and requires it to be uploaded to both systems. If your shortlist mixes both types, contact potential supervisors in November rather than December, because replies take time and silence is common.

申请前是否需要导师，取决于学校，而不是奖学金本身。同济的简章在提交阶段不要求导师接收函。但不少 B 类院校要求：例如贵州大学会在审核后发放本校的导师接收函，并要求上传到两个系统。如果你的目标院校里两类都有，请在 11 月而不是 12 月联系导师，因为回复需要时间，而不回复是常态。

It is also worth being precise about what the scholarship does not cover: international travel, visa fees and the 600-yuan application fee are all borne by the applicant. For a family comparing offers, those three items belong in the budget alongside tuition.

也要清楚奖学金不覆盖的部分：国际旅费、签证费，以及这 600 元报名费，均由申请人自行承担。对正在比较不同方案的家庭来说，这三项都应该和学费一起算进预算。

What to Do

行动建议

Treat the 56 days as four blocks rather than one long stretch. Week one, from 25 to 31 October, is for accounts and decisions: register on both portals, confirm that the programme you want is open under Type B, and check whether it requires prior contact with a supervisor. November is for documents that other institutions must produce. The first half of December is for the medical examination and the police clearance certificate. The last five days are buffer, not working time.

把 56 天拆成四段，而不是当成一整段。第一周（10 月 25 日至 31 日）解决账号和决策：两个系统都注册，确认目标专业在 B 类下开放，并核实是否需要提前联系导师。11 月处理必须由外部机构出具的材料。12 月上半月做体检和无犯罪记录证明。最后五天是缓冲，不是工作时间。

The document list below is the one that actually causes delays. Note the two validity windows: the medical examination result is valid for six months, and the police clearance certificate should normally be issued within six months of submission.

下面这份清单才是真正拖慢进度的地方。注意两个有效期：体检结果有效期为 6 个月，无犯罪记录证明通常应为提交之日前 6 个月内出具。

Two practical warnings. First, submit three to five days before 20 December, because the portal is busiest in the final 48 hours and a payment failure at 23:50 cannot be resolved. Second, keep a full copy of everything you upload; if the university asks for a correction by email, you will need to resend quickly rather than reconstruct.

两点实操提醒。第一，在 12 月 20 日前三到五天提交，因为最后 48 小时系统最拥挤，23:50 出现支付失败根本无法补救。第二，把你上传的每一份材料完整留档；如果学校邮件要求补正，你需要快速重发，而不是重新制作。

Three mistakes account for most of the files that get returned. A transcript uploaded without the registrar's stamp. A study plan under 1000 characters, or one that talks about China in general rather than about the specific programme and supervisor. And a CSC system left half-completed because the university portal had already sent a confirmation email. Each takes ten minutes to check, and each is fatal on its own.

大多数被退回的材料，问题集中在三处：成绩单扫描件没有教务处盖章；学习计划不足 1000 字，或者通篇在讲"中国很好"，而没有讲具体专业和导师；以及学校系统已经发了确认邮件，就任由 CSC 系统停留在半填状态。这三项每项花十分钟就能自查，但任何一项单独出现都足以致命。

Closing

结语

56 days is enough time to do this properly, and not enough time to do it twice. The applicants who succeed are rarely the ones who submit earliest; they are the ones who treated the two portals as one application and started the notarisation in the first week.

56 天，够你把这件事认真做好，但不够你做两遍。顺利走完的申请人，往往不是提交最早的，而是把两个系统当成一份申请来对待、并且在第一周就去办学历公证的人。
