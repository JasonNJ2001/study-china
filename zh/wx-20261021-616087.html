Origin EduGlob Visits NUFE on International Student Recruitment

By ORIGIN Education · 2026-10-21 · 原文：微信公众号

走进南财，共话来华
A Visit to NUFE — Origin EduGlob Meets the School of International Education

一次目标明确的拜访，为日后的招生合作打下扎实基础。

A purposeful visit — laying a solid foundation for future recruitment cooperation.

2026 年 10 月 21 日，南京财经大学行政楼会议室里，来了一场聚焦的会面——尚源（南京）教育科技有限公司总裁周迪、申请顾问周文蝶一行到访，与南京财经大学国际教育学院招生办老师刘宁围桌而坐，讨论来华留学相关事宜。

On October 21, 2026, in the meeting room of the Administration Building at Nanjing University of Finance and Economics, Jude, President of Shangyuan (Nanjing) Education Technology Co., Ltd., and Application Consultant Zhou Wendie sat down with Liu Ning of the Admissions Office, School of International Education, for one focused conversation: matters related to international students studying in China.

从生源对接、招生流程，到学生入学后的落地服务，双方一项一项地聊、一条一条地对，把"留学生相关事宜"落到了具体细节上。这不是一次寒暄式的拜访，而是一次为日后招生合作打基础的务实交流。

From student sourcing and admission procedures to on-ground services after enrollment, the two sides went through every item and matched every detail. This was not a courtesy visit, but a pragmatic exchange laying the groundwork for future recruitment cooperation.

🤝 一场围绕"留学生"的深谈
A Deep Talk Centered on International Students

交流中，双方把重点放在了招生链路的真实痛点上：国际学生从哪来、材料怎么核、进来之后怎么带。尚源教育介绍了自己在生源网络、材料核验与行前辅导上的做法，南财国际教育学院的老师则结合招生办的一线经验，讲清了流程衔接与学生管理的实际要求。

The discussion centered on the real pain points of the admission chain: where international students come from, how their materials are verified, and how they are supported once they arrive. Origin EduGlob introduced its student sourcing, document verification and pre-departure coaching, while NUFE faculty shared first-hand Admissions Office experience.

没有空泛的表态，只有对细节的反复确认——这恰恰是双方愿意继续往下谈的原因。

No empty statements, only repeated confirmation of the details — which is why both sides are willing to keep talking.

🏢 走进尚源教育（Origin EduGlob）
About Origin EduGlob

Origin EduGlob尚源（南京）教育科技有限公司筹建于 2023 年，2025 年正式成立于中国南京，是中国教育国际交流协会会员单位。公司专注"来华留学"核心业务，致力于以前沿科技赋能国际教育，与中国高校共同拓展国际生源市场，全面提升招生效率与生源质量。

Prepared for establishment in 2023 and formally founded in Nanjing, China in 2025, Origin EduGlob is a member unit of the China Education Association for International Exchange. The company focuses on study-in-China services, using technology to empower international education and expand student markets with Chinese universities.

🎓 走进南京财经大学（NUFE）
About Nanjing University of Finance and Economics

南京财经大学NUFE坐落于江苏省南京市，始建于 1956 年的粮食部南京粮食学校，是新中国自己创办的第一批粮食院校之一；2003 年经教育部批准正式更名为南京财经大学。学校是江苏省人民政府与国家粮食局共建高校、江苏高水平大学建设高峰计划 B 类建设高校，已形成以经济管理类学科为主，经济学、管理学、法学、工学、理学、文学、艺术学等多学科支撑配套、协调发展的格局。

Founded in 1956 as the Nanjing Grain School, NUFE was among the first grain institutions established by New China itself, and was renamed Nanjing University of Finance and Economics in 2003. Co-built by the Jiangsu Provincial Government and the former State Grain Administration, it is now a high-level university led by economics and management.

2022 年 8 月，学校成为博士学位授予单位；2025 年获批推免资格。学校现有教师 1300 余人，22 个教学院（部），50 个普通本科专业，并拥有仙林、福建路、桥头三个校区。2026 年，南京财经大学迎来建校 70 周年——在这个节点上接待到访的国际教育同行，别有一番意义。

In August 2022 NUFE became a doctoral degree-granting institution, and in 2025 it was approved for recommended exemption from entrance examinations. With over 1,300 faculty, 22 schools (departments), 50 undergraduate programs and three campuses, NUFE celebrates its 70th anniversary in 2026.

国际教育学院承担学校国际学生教育与来华留学工作，是南财面向世界的重要窗口。招生办的老师常年站在国际学生招录的第一线，最清楚流程里哪里容易卡、哪里可以更顺——这样的经验，正是合作中最需要的部分。

The School of International Education handles NUFE's international student education and study-in-China work. Its Admissions Office faculty know exactly where processes get stuck — the first-hand experience that matters most in any partnership.
