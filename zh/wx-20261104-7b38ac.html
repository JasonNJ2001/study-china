XJTU English-Taught Engineering: 40 Seats for 2027 Entry

XJTU English-Taught Engineering: 40 Seats for 2027 Entry

西安交大2027英文授课工科本科：全球40个名额

 

THE LEAD

导语

Xi'an Jiaotong University (XJTU) has opened applications for its 2027 English-taught bachelor's programmes in engineering — four degrees, a global quota of just 40 seats, and a first application window that runs from 1 November 2026 to 15 May 2027. For students who want a C9-League engineering degree without first passing HSK, this is one of the most concrete offers published this season. It is also a four-year commitment, so the details deserve a careful read.

西安交通大学已开放2027年英文授课本科工程项目的申请——四个学位、全球仅40个名额，第一阶段申请窗口为2026年11月1日至2027年5月15日。对想读C9高校工科、又不想先考出HSK的学生来说，这是本考季公布的最具体的项目之一。它同时也是一份四年之约，细节值得仔细读。

FOUR DEGREES, TAUGHT IN ENGLISH

四个学位，全英文授课

The programmes, announced by XJTU's School of International Education on 31 October 2026, are Intelligent Manufacturing Engineering, Energy and Power Engineering, Electrical Engineering and Automation, and Materials Science and Engineering. All four run four years, are taught entirely in English, and award a Bachelor of Engineering. The university leans on its strong suit: XJTU's engineering discipline ranks 7th globally in the 2026 ESI rankings, mechanical engineering holds 1st worldwide in the 2025 U.S. News subject rankings, and the university itself sits at 79th in the 2026 ARWU.

这批项目由西安交大国际教育学院于2026年10月31日发布，包括智能制造工程、能源与动力工程、电气工程及其自动化、材料科学与工程。四个项目均为四年制、全英文授课、授予工学学士学位。学校拿出的都是看家学科：2026年ESI排名中西安交大工程学位列全球第7，机械工程在2025年U.S. News学科排名中居全球第1，学校本身在2026年ARWU排名中位列第79。

The teaching pipeline is built around industry. The notice names partnerships with Huawei, BYD and State Grid for internships, and points out that the degrees carry Washington Accord accreditation — meaning graduates can pursue further study or professional registration across the 58 signatory countries. For parents weighing the return on four years of tuition, the employment logic — China's manufacturing and energy sectors hiring directly from these programmes — is the core of the pitch.

整个培养链条围绕产业搭建。通知中提到与华为、比亚迪、国家电网等的产学研合作及实习机会，并强调学位获《华盛顿协议》认证——毕业生可在58个签署成员国家和地区继续深造或进行职业资格认证。对家长来说，衡量四年学费回报时，其就业逻辑——中国制造业和能源行业直接从这些项目招人——正是项目的核心卖点。

THE NUMBERS THAT ACTUALLY DECIDE

真正决定成败的几个数字

The quota is 40 seats worldwide, across all four programmes combined — not 40 per programme. Applications run in two windows: Phase 1 from 1 November 2026 to 15 May 2027, Phase 2 from 16 May to 30 June 2027, both on Beijing time. Applicants must be under 25, hold foreign citizenship with a valid ordinary passport (valid beyond March 2028), and meet the Ministry of Education's "4-4-2" rule: at least four years of foreign residency within the last four years as of 30 April of the enrollment year.

全球名额是40个，且是四个项目合计——不是每个项目40个。申请分两个窗口：第一阶段2026年11月1日至2027年5月15日，第二阶段2027年5月16日至6月30日，均按北京时间。申请人须未满25周岁、持外国国籍及有效普通护照（有效期须至2028年3月之后），并符合教育部"442"规定：截至入学当年4月30日，最近4年内持有外国护照满4年、且在境外实际居住累计满2年；未满18周岁的申请人须提供监护人授权书。

English proficiency is required but Chinese is not — the threshold is TOEFL 80, IELTS 6.0 or Duolingo 110 (native English speakers can request a waiver). What replaces the HSK here is the CSCA: applicants to the three programmes — intelligent manufacturing, energy and power, and electrical engineering — need mathematics and physics scores, while materials science requires mathematics and chemistry. Score reports may be submitted later during the comprehensive review stage, which buys time but does not remove the requirement.

英语成绩是硬要求，中文则不是——门槛为托福80、雅思6.0或多邻国110（英语母语者经批准可豁免）。在这里替代HSK的是CSCA：智能制造工程、能源与动力工程、电气工程及其自动化三个专业要求数学和物理成绩，材料科学与工程要求数学和化学成绩。成绩单可在综合审查阶段补充提交，这为考生争取了时间，但不能免除这项要求。

Fees and funding round out the picture. The application fee is 500 yuan for self-funded applicants and 800 yuan for scholarship-track applicants, plus a 400-yuan annual registration fee; on-campus housing runs 8,000 to 19,000 yuan per year. Two scholarship routes exist — XJTU's international freshman scholarship and the Xi'an Municipal "Belt and Road" scholarship — and first-year awards can cover up to 100% of the year's tuition plus a monthly living stipend, with renewals reassessed on academic performance. The exact tuition figure is published on the school's official site and should be confirmed there before you budget.

费用与资助情况如下。申请费为自费项目500元、奖学金项目800元，注册费每年400元；校内住宿每年8000至19000元。奖学金有两条通道——西安交大国际本科生新生奖学金和西安市政府"一带一路"奖学金——第一学年最高可覆盖当年100%学费并发放月度生活津贴，续得与否按在校表现重新评定。具体学费数字以学校官网公布为准，做预算前请先在官网核实。

The selection process itself has three layers: document review, CSCA scores, and interviews, with the university admitting the best on merit. After you submit online, the school screens the file first; only applicants who pass the initial review are invited to pay the application fee, which formally completes the application. Offers move through pre-admission, tuition payment and final confirmation via the application system and your registered email — so an inbox you never check is a real risk. Successful applicants register in early September 2027, and enrollment is conditional on a re-verification of identity, original certificates and language scores at that point.

选拔流程有三层：材料评审、CSCA成绩、面试，学校择优录取。在线提交后，学校先做初审；只有通过初审的申请人才被开放缴纳申请费，缴费完成才算申请正式完成。录取信息按预录取、缴费、终审的节奏，通过申请系统和注册邮箱推送——所以一个从不查看的邮箱，本身就是真实的风险。录取者于2027年9月初报到，报到时学校将复核身份信息、证书原件和语言水平等，复查通过方可正式注册。

 

WHAT TO DO NOW

现在该做什么

  • Register on the application system (isso.xjtu.edu.cn) and select "Degree Programs — Bachelor — English-taught Programs in Advantageous Disciplines". Phase 1 gives you six months, but 40 seats reward early files.
  • 在申请系统（isso.xjtu.edu.cn）注册，选择"学历项目—本科项目—优势学科本科英文授课项目"。第一阶段有六个月窗口，但40个名额属于早交材料的人。
  • Book the right CSCA sitting: mathematics and physics for three programmes, mathematics and chemistry for materials science. Registration for the December sitting opens soon — check csca.cn weekly.
  • 报名正确的CSCA场次：三个专业考数学加物理，材料科学考数学加化学。12月场报名即将开启——每周查看csca.cn。
  • Assemble the document set: notarized high-school certificate and transcripts, one recommendation letter from your principal or teacher with contact details, a study plan of at least 600 words in Chinese or English, the Foreigner Physical Examination Form, a clean criminal record certificate, and guardian authorization if you are under 18. All documents must be in Chinese or English, with official translations for anything else.
  • 备齐材料：经公证的高中毕业证书和成绩单、一封注明推荐人联系方式的高中校长或老师推荐信、不少于600字的中英文学习计划、《外国人体格检查记录》、无犯罪记录证明，未满18周岁还需监护人授权书。所有材料须为中文或英文，其他语种须附官方翻译公证件。
  • Note the fee discipline: the application fee is non-refundable whether or not you are admitted, and materials cannot be mailed — everything is uploaded. Double-check each file before payment.
  • 注意费用规则：无论录取与否，申请费不退还；材料不接受邮寄——全部在线上传。缴费前逐份核对文件。

THE BOTTOM LINE

结语

Forty seats for four flagship engineering degrees at a top-80 world university, taught in English, with scholarships that can cover the first year — the constraint is not interest but timing. The CSCA subjects must be chosen correctly, the Phase 1 window is long but not bottomless, and the scholarship renewals depend on grades from year one onward.
