哈尔滨工业大学

Harbin Institute of Technology (HIT) · 2026-11-12

校园实景 · Campus photo © Harbin Institute of Technology (www.hit.edu.cn) · 角标 Origin Eduglob

【今日推荐大学】Today's University Pick

哈尔滨工业大学（Harbin Institute of Technology，简称 HIT）—— 中国九校联盟（C9）成员、工业和信息化部直属的顶尖工科研究型大学，主校区位于黑龙江哈尔滨，另设威海、深圳校区。

【学校一句话亮点】One-line Highlight

EN: One of China's nine elite C9 universities, HIT is the "Cradle of Engineers" — a powerhouse in aerospace, robotics and computer science, and it runs its own HSK test center right on campus.
中文：中国九校联盟（C9）成员、被誉为"工程师的摇篮"，在航天、机器人、计算机等领域实力顶尖，而且校内就设有 HSK 考点。

【适合哪些留学生】Who It's For

EN:

  • Students aiming for engineering, computer science, AI, aerospace, or architecture at a top-tier research university.
  • Learners who want rigorous, project-based technical training and don't mind cold winters.
  • Chinese-track students holding HSK 4+; or English-track students with IELTS 6.0 / TOEFL 80+.
  • Those who value a lower-cost northern city with strong Russian-heritage culture.

中文：

  • 目标为顶尖研究型大学的工程、计算机、人工智能、航天、建筑等方向的学生；
  • 希望接受严谨、以项目为导向的工科训练、不介意寒冷冬季的同学；
  • 汉语方向已有 HSK4 及以上，或英语方向 IELTS 6.0 / TOEFL 80+ 的学生；
  • 看重生活成本较低、俄式文化浓厚的北方城市的同学。

【优势专业 / 项目】Strengths & Programs

EN: HIT is especially strong in astronautics/aerospace engineering, robotics, computer science & AI, mechanical & materials engineering, civil and environmental engineering. It operates Schools of Astronautics, Mechatronics, and a Faculty of Computing. English-taught bachelor's majors include Civil Engineering, Computer Science & Technology, Artificial Intelligence, Architecture (5-yr), Intelligent Medical Engineering, and Business Administration (open when ≥20 students enroll). Three campuses: Harbin (main), Weihai, Shenzhen.

中文：哈工大在航天、机器人、计算机与人工智能、机械与材料、土木、环境等方向实力突出，设有航天学院、机电工程学院、计算学部等。英语授课本科专业含土木工程、计算机科学与技术、人工智能、建筑学（5年）、智能医学工程、工商管理（满20人开班）。拥有哈尔滨（本部）、威海、深圳三大校区。

【语言与入学要求】Language & Admission

EN:

  • All bachelor applicants must take the China Scholastic Competency Assessment (CSCA) — its score report is a required admission document.
  • Chinese-taught programs (except Chinese Language & Literature): HSK 4 ≥ 210 (valid within 2 years). Chinese Language & Literature: no language certificate required.
  • English-taught programs: IELTS ≥ 6.0 (no subscore below 5.5) or TOEFL iBT ≥ 80.
  • Age ≤ 30 (HIT Scholarship applicants ≤ 25); high-school diploma required; under-18 needs notarized guardianship.

中文：

  • 所有本科申请者均须参加"来华留学本科入学学业水平测试（CSCA）"，成绩单为录取必备材料；
  • 汉语授课专业（汉语言文学除外）：新 HSK 四级 210 分及以上（两年内有效）；汉语言文学专业无需语言证书；
  • 英语授课专业：雅思 ≥ 6.0（单项不低于 5.5）或托福 iBT ≥ 80；
  • 年龄不超过 30 周岁（申请哈工大奖学金者不超过 25 周岁）；须持高中及以上学历；未满 18 岁须提交监护公证。
📘 HSK 3.0「三等九级」提醒 · HSK 3.0 Framework

EN: China's HSK now follows a "three levels × three grades" (三等九级) framework — HSK 1–3 (elementary), 4–6 (intermediate), 7–9 (advanced). HIT's current requirement of "new HSK 4" sits in the intermediate tier. Always check the exact level your target program demands, as requirements differ by track and may be adjusted under the new framework.

中文：汉语水平考试现已形成"三等九级"框架——HSK 1–3 级（初等）、4–6 级（中等）、7–9 级（高等）。哈工大目前要求的"新 HSK 四级"属于中等层级。不同专业、不同授课语言对汉语水平的要求可能不同，请以目标专业当年公布为准。

📍 HSK 考点 · HSK Test Center

EN: Good news — HIT itself is an authorized HSK computer-based test center in Harbin. Register via chinesetest.cn by selecting "China – Heilongjiang – Harbin – Harbin Institute of Technology (Computer-based)". In 2026 the center scheduled four exam sessions (May, June, July, December).

中文：好消息：哈工大本身就是哈尔滨的 HSK 机网考授权考点。考生可登录汉语考试服务网（chinesetest.cn），选择"中国–黑龙江–哈尔滨–哈尔滨工业大学（网考）"报名；2026 年该考点安排了 4 场考试（5、6、7、12 月）。

【城市与生活】City & Life

圣索菲亚教堂 · Saint Sophia Cathedral, Harbin — Wikimedia Commons, CC BY-SA 4.0 (Pseudora) · 角标 Origin Eduglob

EN: Harbin, capital of Heilongjiang in China's northeast, is famous for long snowy winters, the Harbin Ice and Snow World, the Byzantine-style Saint Sophia Cathedral, and Central Street (中央大街). It has a unique Russian heritage, hearty northern cuisine, and a notably lower cost of living than Beijing or Shanghai — comfortable for a student budget, though winters are very cold (often −20 °C), so pack warmly.

中文：哈尔滨是黑龙江省会，位于中国东北，以漫长雪季、冰雪大世界、拜占庭风格的圣索菲亚教堂和中央大街闻名，俄式风情浓厚，北方美食实在，生活成本明显低于北京、上海，适合学生预算；不过冬季严寒（常达 −20°C），需做好保暖。

【奖学金与申请提示】Scholarships & Application

EN:

  • Chinese Government Scholarship (Type A, Bilateral):
     covers tuition, accommodation, comprehensive medical insurance, and a monthly stipend (exact amounts set by the program — confirm with an advisor).
  • HIT International Students Scholarship:
     Elite (100% tuition), First (50%), Second (70%), Third (20%). You cannot hold two scholarships at once.
  • Practical notes: application is open year-round; the September intake deadline is announced each year on cie.hit.edu.cn (typically around late May–July). A certificate of deposit of at least CNY 20,000 is required. Specific tuition, stipend, and quota figures change yearly — for current numbers, contact an Origineduglob study-abroad advisor.

中文：

  • 中国政府奖学金（A类·国别双边项目）：
    含学费、住宿费、综合医疗保险及月度生活补助（具体金额以项目公布为准，建议向顾问确认）；
  • 哈工大国际学生奖学金：
    特等（免全额学费）、一等（减免50%）、二等（减免30%）、三等（减免20%）；不可同时兼得两类奖学金；
  • 实务提示：申请全年开放，9月入学截止日期每年于 cie.hit.edu.cn 公布（往年约在5–7月）；须提供不少于 2 万元人民币的存款证明。学费、奖学金金额与名额每年可能调整——最新数字建议与 Origineduglob 留学顾问联系确认。

【给留学生的一句话建议】One Tip

EN: If engineering is your goal and you can embrace the snow, HIT gives you C9-level training plus the rare convenience of taking your HSK exam without leaving campus.
中文：如果你志在工程、也能拥抱冰雪，哈工大会给你 C9 级的训练，还能让你"不出校门就能考 HSK"——这份便利很难得。
