Choosing a Masters Supervisor in China: Contact, Expectations and Communication

By ORIGIN Education · 2026-11-18

In China the supervisor is not an academic adviser among several; they control your topic, your funding, your lab access and, in practice, your graduation date. The match is made through a formal two-way selection, so the decision is worth more preparation than the university ranking that brought you there.

When Supervisors Are Assigned

Most programmes run the two-way selection, called 双向选择 or 双选, at the end of the first semester, after coursework begins and before the thesis proposal. Some universities assign a provisional supervisor at admission and confirm the pairing later; a few let you arrive with a placement already agreed by email.

The 双选 calendar

The process usually opens in November or December for September entrants, with a list of available supervisors and their remaining quota circulated by the school office. Students submit preferences, supervisors respond, and unmatched students are allocated by the programme director. Missing the window means accepting whoever has space left.

Why early contact beats the form

A supervisor who has already met you and read your transcript will hold a place on the list before the form opens. Email in October, not December, and ask what the selection process looks like in that school.

What to Check Before You Commit

Four facts decide whether a supervisor is a good fit: whether they hold 博导 status and can take doctoral students, how many masters students they graduate each year, whether they have active grant funding, and how their recent graduates describe the workload. Two of these are invisible from the university website.

The four checks

  1. Search their name in CNKI and Web of Science to see the last three years of output, not the career total.
  2. Ask the school office for the number of students currently supervised and the number who withdrew or changed supervisor.
  3. Look for a named grant, such as a 国家自然科学基金 project, which signals money for reagents and conference travel.
  4. Message one current student in the group on WeChat and ask how many hours a week the group meets.

Age and status are not proxies for quality

A young lecturer with recent publications and time to supervise often serves a masters student better than a senior professor with twelve students and an administrative post. The relevant question is how many hours a month they actually give you.

The First Email

Write in English unless the supervisor's profile lists Chinese-language publications only, keep it under 200 words, and lead with the specific paper of theirs you read and the method you want to learn. Chinese faculty receive many template emails from agents; a message that quotes their own work is answered far more often.

What to attach

A one-page CV, an undergraduate transcript, and a single paragraph describing a research question you would like to pursue. Do not attach certificates, photographs or a personal statement longer than a page; unrequested files are usually not opened.

What a good reply looks like

A reply asking for your programming or laboratory experience is a serious reply. A reply saying only that you are welcome to apply is administrative courtesy, and should be read as a no.

Quotas and How Allocation Really Works

Supervisors hold a fixed annual quota, typically two to five masters students, plus a share of the school's 调剂 places for students who were not matched. A professor's willingness is therefore constrained by arithmetic, and the popular names fill before the form opens.

What to do if you are unmatched

Accept a provisional placement, complete the first semester with strong grades, and request a change during the official window. Switching is possible but requires both supervisors and the school to agree, so the second choice is easier than the third.

What a Supervisor Expects

The baseline expectation is attendance at the 组会, a weekly group meeting where each student reports progress and reads a paper, plus a written progress report each semester. Publication before defence is required in many academic masters programmes, and the requirement is set by the school rather than negotiated individually.

The publication rule

Academic masters degrees at most Chinese universities require one paper accepted in a core Chinese journal or an SCI-indexed journal before the defence, though professional masters degrees often waive it. Confirm the exact wording in your programme handbook in week one, because it determines a two-year plan.

Stipends and Research Funding

Funding comes from three sources: the university stipend, usually 600 to 1,500 RMB a month for a masters student, the supervisor's 助研费 top-up of 300 to 1,000 RMB, and national or provincial scholarships awarded competitively. A funded supervisor can also pay for reagents, conference registration and a laptop.

Ask about money directly

Ask in the first meeting what the group's funding situation is and whether conference travel is covered. The question is routine in Chinese academia and marks you as serious rather than greedy.

Warning Signs and Switching Out

The signals worth acting on are a group meeting that never happens, students who have not graduated on time, a supervisor who answers messages once a month, and pressure to publish work you did not do. Any two of these justify changing supervisor before the thesis proposal, when the process is still administrative.

How a switch works

You need a written request, acceptance from another supervisor with quota, and approval from the school. It is refused more often after the proposal stage, so act in the first year. A switch is not held against you if it is framed as a research-direction mismatch rather than a personal complaint.

Choosing a supervisor is choosing a working relationship for two to three years, and the students who do it well treat the email, the group meeting and the funding question as one decision rather than three. Applicants who want programme-level detail on supervision practice before committing to a Chinese masters degree will find it worth comparing departments directly rather than relying on the university brochure.
