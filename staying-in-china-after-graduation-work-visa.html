Staying in China After Graduation: Work Visa Routes

By ORIGIN Education · 2026-11-04

A degree does not convert into a work permit on its own. China operates three separate post-study routes, and which one you can use depends less on your degree than on whether an employer has already agreed to hire you and how that employer is registered.

The three routes, and which one is the default

China offers three legal paths after graduation: employer sponsorship leading to a Foreigner's Work Permit, a one-year private-affairs residence permit annotated for job-hunting or entrepreneurship in the cities that issue one, and departure followed by re-entry on a Z visa. Employer sponsorship is the only route that leads to long-term residence, and it needs a signed contract before your student permit expires.

The second route buys time. The third is the default for anyone who leaves China to job-hunt from abroad, and it is the slowest of the three.

Which work permit category you fall into

The Foreigner's Work Permit is graded A, B or C, and the grade comes from a points system rather than from your salary alone. Category A requires 85 points and covers senior talent; Category B requires 60 points and covers the ordinary professional hire; Category C is quota-limited and is where most fresh graduates without work experience land.

The category is decided by the employer's application, not by your preference, so ask which one is being filed before you sign anything.

The graduate exemption most applicants never hear about

Category B normally demands a bachelor's degree plus two years of relevant work experience, a bar no fresh graduate can clear. Graduates of Chinese universities at master's level or above, and graduates of high-ranked overseas universities, can be treated as outstanding foreign graduates and admitted without that experience requirement.

What "outstanding" is measured against

In practice the test is the ranking of the university that issued your degree and the level of the degree itself. Master's and doctoral graduates of Chinese institutions are the clearest case; bachelor's graduates usually need a degree from a university inside the top tier named in the published list. City-level rules differ, and the exit-entry administration in your host city is the authority that applies them.

Why the degree level matters more than the grade

A master's degree also lifts your points total, because the scoring table rewards qualifications, Chinese language ability and age under 30. Two applicants from the same faculty can therefore land in different categories purely because one stayed for a master's.

Converting inside China versus leaving for a Z visa

Staying inside China is faster but only permitted while your student residence permit is still valid and the local exit-entry administration accepts an in-country change of purpose. Leaving and applying for a Z visa abroad is slower, but it is rarely refused once the work permit notification has been issued, and it resets your entry record.

Documents the employer files for you

  • Business licence and the company's foreign employment registration.
  • Your degree certificate, authenticated or verified as the city requires.
  • A criminal record check covering your time in China.
  • The medical examination record from a designated clinic.
  • The signed labour contract, with the position and salary stated.

If your student permit lapses first

You fall out of status and must leave. Re-entry is possible, but you will be applying for a Z visa from outside with no in-country record, and any gap longer than your permit's validity shows up in the file.

The one-year job-hunting residence permit

Several cities, Shanghai and Shenzhen among them, issue a private-affairs residence permit valid for up to twelve months to graduating international students who have not yet found work, annotated for job-hunting or for starting a business. It legalises your stay but not your employment, so paid work during that year still requires a work permit.

The permit is a bridge, not a status. Apply before your student residence permit expires, bring the graduation certificate and a letter from your university, and check whether your city requires a minimum grade point average or a recommendation from the international office.

What the employer must already hold

The hiring company needs a valid business licence, a registered foreign employment account with the local science and technology bureau, and no history of foreign employment violations. Small companies and newly founded startups sometimes cannot file at all, which is why the question to ask in a second interview is not about salary but about whether the company has hired a foreign national before.

The timeline you are working with

Start four months before graduation. The work permit notification alone takes about twenty working days, the permit card another ten, and the residence permit conversion seven to fifteen. Filing in sequence rather than in parallel is what avoids a gap in status, and a gap is the one error that turns a routine case into a discretionary one.

  1. Sign the contract and confirm which work permit category the employer will file.
  2. Employer submits the work permit notification and waits for approval.
  3. You apply for the work permit and the residence permit at the exit-entry administration.
  4. Register your new address within 24 hours of moving, as the registration rules require.
  5. Renew one month before expiry, not on the last day.

What gets applications refused

The three recurring failures are a job whose duties do not match the degree, an employer with no foreign employment quota left in Category C, and a criminal record check that has passed its six-month validity. A fourth is quieter: applying after the student residence permit has expired, which converts a routine filing into a discretionary case.

None of these are fatal individually, but each one costs a month, and a month is enough to lose a job offer in a market that hires on fixed September cycles.

If the paperwork looks heavy, the reason is that this route is the one that leads to permanent residence later, and the file you build now is the file that is read then. Planning the sequence before graduation is cheaper than repairing it afterwards.
