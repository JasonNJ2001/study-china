CSC Scholarship 2027 — Full Guide for International Students

By ORIGIN Education · For the 2027 intake · 7 min read

The Chinese Government Scholarship (CSC) is the most generous funding route for studying in China — it typically covers tuition, accommodation, and a monthly living stipend. Competition is real, but the process is transparent if you understand the two application tracks.

Type A vs Type B — know which one you are

  • Type A (via embassy): You apply through the Chinese embassy or consulate in your home country. Quotas are allocated by country, so competition is country-level.
  • Type B (via university): You apply directly to a Chinese university that holds a CSC quota. The university pre-screens and nominates you.
You can apply through both routes in the same year, but you may not hold two CSC awards simultaneously. Many successful applicants use Type B through a university they already target.

Key eligibility

  • Non-Chinese citizen in good health.
  • Age limits: bachelor's under 25, master's under 35, PhD under 40 (typical).
  • Meet the language requirement for your chosen program (HSK for Chinese-taught; some English-taught programs waive HSK).

Typical timeline (2027 intake)

  1. Nov–Jan: University applications and Type B submissions open.
  2. Feb–Apr: Embassies (Type A) and universities review and nominate.
  3. Jun–Jul: Final CSC approval and Admission Notice issued.
  4. Aug–Sep: Registration at the host university.

Deadlines shift slightly each year — always confirm on the official CampusChina (CSC) portal.

Document checklist

  • CSC online application form (generated after submission)
  • Notarized highest-degree certificate + transcripts
  • Study plan (≥800 words for graduate applicants)
  • Two academic recommendation letters
  • Physical examination form (valid 6 months)
  • Language certificate
