China Degree Verification: Getting Your Qualification Recognised at Home

By ORIGIN Education · 2026-11-05

Verifying a Chinese degree abroad is not one process. A degree awarded by a Chinese university is verified through CHSI, the education ministry's student records database, and that report is free and instant. A foreign degree earned at a Chinese campus, or a joint-programme degree, is certified through CSCSE, which charges a fee and takes weeks. Most delays come from applying to the wrong one.

Two certificates, two different offices

CHSI holds the national register of Chinese higher-education qualifications and issues online verification reports for degrees conferred by Chinese universities. CSCSE is the Chinese Service Center for Scholarly Exchange, which evaluates foreign qualifications, including those awarded by foreign universities operating in China. Your degree's awarding body, printed on the certificate itself, decides which of the two you need.

The CHSI report, which is free and instant

Log in to chsi.com.cn, verify your identity with passport details as they appear on your student record, and generate the online verification report for your degree certificate. It issues in minutes, carries a QR code that a foreign employer can scan, and has an English interface. The report expires after six months by default, which matters if you are timing it for a job application.

What to do when your name does not match

Chinese student records use the name exactly as printed on your passport at enrolment. If you have since changed your name, married, or your passport was reissued with a different transliteration, the CHSI report will not match the passport you are submitting to an employer. Request a name-change note from your university's registry office first, because CHSI cannot amend the underlying record.

The CSCSE certification, which costs money and time

CSCSE certification applies when the awarding institution is foreign: a British degree earned at a Ningbo campus, an American degree earned in Kunshan, or a foreign master's completed in China under a joint arrangement. The application is filed online at the CSCSE service portal, the fee is RMB 360 and non-refundable, and the centre's published standard is ten working days, extended to twenty when the foreign university has to confirm details.

  • The degree certificate, plus a certified translation if it is not in Chinese or English
  • The full official transcript, sealed by the awarding institution
  • Passport pages showing the visa or residence permit covering the entire study period
  • The signed authorisation letter that lets CSCSE contact the awarding university
  • The programme approval number, where the degree came from a joint arrangement

The documents that get rejected

Rejections are almost always documentary rather than substantive. The usual causes are a passport that does not show the visa or residence permit covering the study period, a transcript that is a student printout rather than an official sealed copy, and a programme with no approval number on the ministry platform. The last one cannot be fixed by resubmitting.

Since November 2023 the apostille replaces consular legalisation

China acceded to the Hague Apostille Convention, which entered into force for China on 7 November 2023. For use in any other member state, a Chinese public document now needs a single apostille from a Chinese foreign affairs office instead of the old two-step chain of notarisation and embassy legalisation. Degree certificates and CHSI reports both qualify, and the saving is measured in weeks.

Where the apostille does not help

Canada, which acceded in January 2024, is covered; most of Europe, the United States, Australia and Japan are covered. Countries outside the convention still require consular legalisation, and some employers and licensing bodies insist on a verification report regardless, because an apostille authenticates a signature, not the truth of the qualification.

What evaluators abroad actually want

Credential evaluators do not read your certificate; they read a verification that arrives from the issuing authority. World Education Services, which most American and Canadian universities use, requires Chinese degrees to be verified through CHSI and sent directly, and will not accept a report you upload yourself. UK ENIC issues a statement of comparability on a paid basis and accepts CHSI reports as supporting evidence.

The difference between equivalence and verification

Verification proves the degree exists. Equivalence states what it is worth in the receiving country's system, and the two are separate purchases. A three-year Chinese bachelor's is commonly assessed as below a four-year American bachelor's for graduate admission purposes, and no amount of verification changes that assessment, so check the equivalence outcome before you plan a master's application around it.

When verification fails and why

Three failures account for most cases: an institution not accredited by the education ministry, a joint programme operating outside its approved scope, and a degree completed mostly online without a corresponding residence permit. In each case the certificate may be genuine and still uncertifiable, because the systems verify authorisation to award, not the paper itself.

The check to run before you enrol

Search the ministry's joint-programme platform for the programme and confirm the approval is current for your intake year. Ask the admissions office, in writing, which body will issue your degree and whether that body's qualifications are registered in CHSI or require CSCSE certification. Two sentences in an email now save a rejected application in three years.

A realistic timeline

Start eight weeks before you need the document, not eight days. CHSI takes minutes; CSCSE takes two to four weeks in practice; an apostille from a provincial foreign affairs office takes about a week; and a foreign evaluator adds four to six weeks on top. Running all four in sequence is a three-month project, and running them in parallel is possible only if you already hold the sealed transcript.
