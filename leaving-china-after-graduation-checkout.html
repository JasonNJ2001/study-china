Leaving China After Graduation: Campus Checkout and Visa Cancellation

By ORIGIN Education · 2026-11-25

Graduation ceremonies end in June or July, but the administrative exit runs on its own schedule and usually finishes weeks later. Between the last exam and the flight home there is a checkout sheet with six or seven signatures, a room inspection, a residence permit that stops being valid, and a set of documents that are far harder to obtain once you have left the country.

The checkout sheet is the real deadline

The checkout sheet, sometimes called the leaving-school form, is a single page that must be stamped by every office you have touched: library, dormitory, finance, department, international office. No diploma and often no transcript is released until it is complete. Students who book flights for the day after the ceremony routinely miss it.

Who signs what

The library checks for unreturned books and unpaid fines, which can be as small as a few yuan and still block the stamp. The finance office confirms tuition and dormitory fees are settled. The department confirms laboratory equipment, keys or thesis materials are returned. The international office is the last stamp and the one that triggers the report to exit-entry.

Why it takes a week, not an hour

Offices keep office hours, and the person who holds the stamp may be at a graduation rehearsal or on leave. Start the sheet ten working days before your flight, not two. Ask the international office which single day of the week is quietest for stamps; most will tell you.

Giving up the room and getting the deposit back

The dormitory inspection is a physical walk-through, and the deposit is released against it, not against your departure date. Expect the money in 5 to 30 working days, usually to a Chinese bank card. If you close that card before the refund lands, the money has nowhere to go and the process restarts.

Photograph the room on the day you hand back the key: walls, furniture, bathroom, meter readings and the inventory sheet you signed on arrival. Damage deductions are calculated at replacement cost, and a photo taken at handover is the only evidence that survives the conversation.

Your residence permit ends with your student status

A student residence permit is issued against your enrolment, not against the printed expiry date. Under the Measures for the Administration of Schools' Admission and Cultivation of International Students — a joint order of the Ministry of Education, the Ministry of Foreign Affairs and the Ministry of Public Security effective 1 July 2017 — the university must report a change of status, including graduation, to the local exit-entry administration, normally within ten days.

Once that report is filed, the permit can be cancelled before its printed date. Plan your flight for before the report is submitted, or convert to another permit category first. Buying a non-refundable ticket for three weeks after the ceremony is the single most common expensive mistake of the graduation season.

The three legal ways to stay a little longer

There are only three realistic routes, and each has a different clock. The stay visa gives you up to 30 days to travel or wrap up affairs. The private affairs residence permit, available to graduates who start a business or take a job, can run longer. Work permits require an employer that is already registered to hire foreigners.

The 30-day stay visa

Apply at the exit-entry administration before your student permit is cancelled, with a letter from the university confirming graduation. It is issued for tourism or departure preparation and cannot be extended indefinitely. Bring a passport photo, the graduation certificate and a copy of your flight booking.

The private affairs permit for entrepreneurs

Graduates of Chinese universities who register a company can apply for a private affairs residence permit, a policy introduced to keep foreign graduates with viable businesses in the country. It requires a business licence, proof of premises and a capital plan. Processing is slower than a stay visa and should be started a month before the permit expires.

Documents to collect before you fly

These are the items that are painful, expensive or impossible to obtain from abroad, and each one is issued by a different office with a different queue. Ask for all of them in the final two weeks, while the staff still recognise you, your student number is active in every system, and the seal for your department has not yet been packed away for the summer.

The apostille question

China has been a party to the Hague Apostille Convention since 7 November 2023. That means public documents such as degree certificates, transcripts and notarised copies can be legalised with an apostille certificate issued by the Ministry of Foreign Affairs or an authorised local foreign affairs office, instead of the older two-step consular legalisation. If your home country is also a member, this is faster and cheaper, but it must be done inside China while the original is with you.

Sealed transcripts matter

Many universities abroad will not accept a transcript that has been opened, scanned or emailed by the student. Ask for sealed envelopes, one per institution you plan to apply to, and carry them flat in hand luggage.

Money, phones and accounts

Close or keep your Chinese bank account deliberately. A closed account blocks a late deposit refund and any scholarship balance. A kept account needs a Chinese phone number for verification, so the two decisions are linked: cancel the SIM and the banking app locks you out.

Before departure, clear WeChat Pay and Alipay balances, screenshot your transaction history for insurance or tax purposes, and settle any campus card balance at the card office. Campus cards are usually refundable in cash at the service desk, but only while the account is still active.

What to do in the last 72 hours

  • Return every library book and pay any fine, however small.
  • Hand back the key, the card and the air-conditioner remote, and get the inspection signed.
  • Collect the stamped checkout sheet and photograph all pages.
  • Confirm with exit-entry that your permit status is clear for departure.
  • Declare cash above USD 5,000 at customs; undeclared amounts can be seized.
  • Keep the boarding pass and entry stamp photos for your insurance claim.

The order matters more than the speed. A graduate who starts the sheet ten days early and keeps every photo spends the last week saying goodbye instead of queueing for a stamp that nobody warned them about.
