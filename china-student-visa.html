China Student Visa (X1 / X2) — Documents & Process

By ORIGIN Education · 5 min read

Admission is only half the journey — the student visa is what gets you through the border. China issues two student visa types, and choosing the right one depends entirely on how long you'll study.

X1 vs X2 — which one do you need?

  • X1 visa: For programs longer than 180 days (most degree programs). You enter on X1, then must convert it to a Residence Permit at the local Public Security Bureau within 30 days of arrival.
  • X2 visa: For programs of 180 days or less (exchange, short courses). It is typically single-entry and does not convert to a residence permit.

Required documents

  • Valid passport (6+ months remaining, blank pages).
  • Admission Notice from the Chinese university.
  • JW201 (government-funded / CSC) or JW202 (self-funded) form.
  • Completed visa application form + recent photo.
  • Physical examination record (for X1, often required).

Process & timing

  1. Receive Admission Notice + JW form from your university.
  2. Book an appointment at the Chinese embassy/consulate in your country.
  3. Submit documents; standard processing is about 4–7 working days (express options vary).
  4. Enter China, then (X1) apply for a residence permit within 30 days.

Common mistakes that cause rejection or delay

  • Applying for X1 when your program is under 180 days (or vice versa).
  • JW form number mismatch with the admission letter.
  • Expired or smudged physical-exam form.
  • Booking flights before the visa is in hand.
Rule of thumb: start the visa file the day you receive your admission documents — don't wait.
